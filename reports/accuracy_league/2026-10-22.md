# Accuracy league table, 2026-10-22

**This is a monitor, not a claim.** It scores every pre-tip source on every player-prop line it priced, descriptively. It is not a decision rule, it pre-registers nothing, and no CI here supports a conclusion. Decisions come only from frozen rules. Sources price different line sets, so standalone scores are NOT comparable across rows; only the paired differences against the Kalshi mid (on lines both priced) are like-for-like. Lower Brier / log loss is better; a negative difference means the source beat Kalshi on the shared lines.

Cumulative: slate dates 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-22 (America/New_York). Last 7 days: 2026-10-21 to 2026-10-22. Universe: 2845 lines (game, player, stat, k) priced by at least one source before tip. A Kalshi k+ line is the sportsbook over at k - 0.5; whole-number sportsbook lines are skipped. Scored only on settled games, players with minutes > 0 (DNP lines are counted, not scored).
