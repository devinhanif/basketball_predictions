## Production as served (key question 1)

Read in code (`nba/daily/predict.py:247-271`, `nba/daily/pipeline.py:552-580,735-751`, `nba/props/forward.py:360-470`, `nba/props/rosters.py:184`, `ops/roster_source.sh`):

* **OUT**: `slate_report_outs` uses `ReportTriggerConfig(statuses=("out",))`. That is the latest official report stamped at or before
  min(now, real tip - 60 min) and at most 36 h old, chosen per game. Those players are excluded before P(play). The Cell-P population applies
  the same rule, and the run-time gate proved the sets identical (3,952 games). Both arms already exclude them.
* **DOUBTFUL / QUESTIONABLE / PROBABLE**: unused anywhere in the props P(play) path. Doubtful enters only injury-Elo (game winner, weight
  0.5). Production as served never sees a Q/D tag.
* **`--roster-source official`** (through 2026-11-30 via `ops/roster_source.sh`) is the `CommonTeamRoster` membership switch, not the injury
  report. It changes who is a candidate, plus the rookie prior and the new-team multiplier. The parity gate shows no Cell-P row carries
  either, so it changes no Cell-P p.
* **What production does that P0 does not**: it drops rows with raw p < 0.5 (`min_p_play`), projected minutes < 2, or beyond the 13-man cap.

## Findings, severity-ranked

1. **MAJOR (transfer, not leak): the population is conditioned on tonight's box score.** `build_base` builds rows by inner join to `pgs`.
   A rostered player who is not OUT at T-60 but does not dress (late scratch, waived or traded but still on production's last-10 roster,
   unreported absence) is not in the test, yet production serves him. Measured on nba.duckdb (read-only, `scratchpad/population.py`):
   production-style roster rows with n_played_prior >= 5 number 39,192 / 39,967 (2023 / 2024). Of these, 6,670 / 7,077 are absent from the box:
   3,992 / 4,417 listed OUT at some time (most already excluded at T-60), 606 / 622 Q/D and never OUT, and **2,058 / 2,022 on no report at
   all**. Every one of them has y = 0. Both arms share the selection, so D1 and D2 are fair *on Cell-P*. But R19 learned
   P(play | dressed), and its behaviour on undressed served rows (large `days_since_played`, no tag) is unmeasured. A bound: if R19 were
   badly wrong on ~2,000 such rows a season, the served-population gap could shrink materially. Realistically it cannot flip at -0.085, but
   that is not shown. The T462 2025 diagnostic used the same population, so it does not cover this either. **The live log does.** Its
   population is all rostered not-OUT shadow rows, with y = 0 for a player absent from the box.
2. **MAJOR (decision scope): R19 is not the arm the live venue tests.** `PPLAY_INTO_PROPS_LIVE` (3a305482) scores `p_play_r` from
   `nba/daily/pplay_shadow` (R on the 2022-start production frame, no era channels). A production switch to R19 would put an arm into
   production that the live verdict does not score. The difference is small offline (R19 - R22 -0.0026 [-0.0039, -0.0014] / -0.0009
   [-0.0017, -0.0000]), but it is a different model. Switch to what the live log scores, or give R19 a new live id. Do not substitute
   silently.
3. **MINOR: the T482 ledger caveat is wrong in substance.** Production's upstream OUT removal is already in both arms (identical OUT sets).
   The row should say so and cite this review.
4. **MINOR: CI code is not in `nba/truth/`.** `nba/truth/bootstrap.py` does not exist. The harness uses
   `pplay_minutes_valid_core.cluster_boot`, a correct ratio estimator over multinomial game weights, and my independent re-derivation
   agrees to 1e-5. Flagged per the CI-code rule, not a defect in this result.
5. **INFO: pos_code.** R19 does not use `pos_code` (48 inputs listed by the rerun, `pos_code in R19: False`). M2_19 (the minutes
   secondary) does. In the rebuilt M frame `pos_code` is never NULL and its share of 0 is flat by season (0.477 / 0.483 / 0.484 / 0.466 /
   0.469 / 0.493 for 2019-2024). The 21:33 history fill held, with no 2019-21 era signature.

## The one biggest risk

The scored population excludes players who do not dress. If R19 assigns high P(play) to rostered, unreported, undressed players (the
waived, traded, or silently absent), the served-population gain is smaller than -0.085, by an amount nobody has measured. The live log
under PPLAY_INTO_PROPS_LIVE is the test that sees those rows.
