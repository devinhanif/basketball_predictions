**Verdict: WOUNDED.** The result is real, not a leak, and it replicates. But the win is not model skill. CAL-rung beats
the table almost entirely because it uses **the sportsbook's own main line**, which turns out to be a better median than

## Model class and playbook check
A logistic recalibration layer over a stored GBM forecast (linear/logistic; quantile/distribution layers).
1. **Information or capacity?** Information, from the market. The gain is the book's main line (`zm`), a pre-tip market
   input. With that line removed, the extra capacity is worth nothing (model-only terms −0.0002 vs C0).
2. **Data enough?** Yes. Seven terms on thousands of fit rungs per block. The stability across 14 months and both seasons
   says the fits are not starved.
3. **Valid test?** Yes. Gating is real (all main-line and rung quotes are at least 62.8 min before the real tip), the
   comparator is fair (shift table), supports are integer, and the rows are the same for every arm. The test was valid; it
   answered a different question than the word suggests.
4. **Lineage.** This is LINE_CALIBRATION's lesson again: the market's line beats our median (T493's "a recency-only line map
   gets half of CAL-C0"). The same shape showed up in "CAL-C0 by stat".
5. **Scored against:** binary log loss at rung thresholds, not money and not the de-vigged rung price.

## The single biggest risk if the win is read wrongly
Reading RUNG_REFIT_FIXES as "our model calibrates rungs well" or as an edge. It is "our P becomes calibrated once you tell
it the book's main line". Any comparison against the book's **rung prices** would then compare the book's line against the
book's own line. T491 already found A0 hits at about the de-vigged price. Nothing here says CAL-rung beats the price, and the
honest prior is that it does not.

## What I did not verify

  role, no market input) would capture. This review's model-only knockouts say a shift on z does little. The bias may be
  player-specific, which is exactly what the market line knows.
