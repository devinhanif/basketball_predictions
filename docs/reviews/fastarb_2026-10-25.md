# Fast pre-tip poller and Kalshi / Polymarket arbitrage checker (2026-10-25)

Market agent, branch `agent/market-fast-arb`. Approved by Devin 2026-10-25 (DECISIONS row of that date;
Underdog addendum the same day). Advice followed: "arbitrage and news speed are the only edges not dependent
on prediction". Read-only: public GETs, no Kalshi key, no order or portfolio endpoint, no execution code.

## What it is

`python -m nba.markets.fastarb {run, dry-run, underdog}` (package `nba/markets/fastarb/`, config
`configs/fastarb.yaml`).

* `run`: from first tip - 180 min, one tick per 60 s, each game polled until its own tip and then dropped; exits
  after the last tip. Refuses (rc 2) unless the fee objects it needs are `verified: true`. One poller at a time
  (`flock` on `data/ops/fastarb_lock`). Write-once raw files `data/raw/fastarb_<ET date>/<HHMMSS>_<venue>_<label>.json.gz`
  (raw before parse; Polymarket events are stored filtered to winner / spread / total markets, the full event is 2 MB
  of player props), heartbeat `data/ops/heartbeat/fastarb.json`, alerts to `data/ops/ALERTS.md`
  (`- <time> [fastarb] ... key=<8 hex>`, dedup window 30 min, state in `data/ops/fastarb_dedup.json`).
  No DuckDB file is opened for writing; `nba.duckdb` is never opened; the lineups DB is opened `read_only`.
* `dry-run`: one tick over a slate (default today, any pre-tip state), prints the gaps, writes no files (`--with-pinnacle` appends to the credit ledger). International
  Polymarket is included for comparison only.
* `underdog --csv F`: the Underdog comparison from a hand-made file (see below). Underdog is never polled.

## Sources

| Venue | Endpoint (all GET, no auth) | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Kalshi | `api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/markets?series_ticker=KXNBAGAME / KXNBASPREAD / KXNBATOTAL` (the endpoints `nba.live` and `nba.kalshi` already use) | asks and top-of-book sizes, both sides |
| Polymarket US | `gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events/slug/nba-<away>-<home>-<date>` (metadata, 5-min cache), `/v1/markets/<slug>/book` (depth, per tick) | the venue a US person can trade |
| Polymarket international | `gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?slug=...` (top of book only) | comparison only; NOT tradable from the US |
| Pinnacle | the-odds-api `events/{id}/odds?bookmakers=pinnacle&markets=h2h,spreads,totals`, through `LiveClient` and its daily ledger | no-vig reference for the lag check |

Why two Polymarkets: the gamma/CLOB API Devin named is polymarket.com, where "Trading is blocked in the United
States". The US exchange (Polymarket US, QCX LLC) has its own public gateway; its prices differ from the
international book (DET@PHI moneyline 0.50 / 0.51 US against 0.51 / 0.52 international at the same minute). The
alerts name buys only on Kalshi and Polymarket US. International gaps are printed and labelled `INTL-ONLY`, not written
to ALERTS.md.

## Canonical propositions and team mapping

Every market is reduced to one proposition about the score, with a YES and a NO side: `margin > c` (moneyline c = 0;
"T wins by over L" or "T +h") or `total > c`. Half-point lines only; a whole-number line has a push and is skipped
and counted. Team mapping: Kalshi event ticker (`KXNBAGAME-26OCT25DETPHI` = away DET, home PHI) and the
Polymarket slug (`nba-det-phi-2026-10-25`) both resolve through `nba.ingest.teams.TEAM_ABBREV_TO_ID` plus a short
hand-reviewed alias list (`NY`, `GS`, `SA`, ...); Polymarket side names resolve through the event's own `teams[]`
(`id` -> `abbreviation`). Anything that does not map is skipped and counted (`skipped` in the report), never guessed.
Tonight: 6 of 7 games matched on both venues; `nba-orl-nyk-2026-10-25` has no Polymarket US event (skipped,
counted).

## Arbitrage check

For each proposition quoted by Kalshi and a Polymarket venue: buy YES at one venue's ask and NO at the other's, both
directions. Net per contract = `1 - (cost + fee_K + fee_PM) / n`, each fee rounded once per order at n contracts,
evaluated at `fee_eval_contracts` = 20 (a rounding-evaluation size, not a stake) or the largest fill the visible
depth supports. Polymarket US depth is the full book (levels walked, payload sort order not trusted); Kalshi depth is the
top-of-book size from the markets endpoint. Alert when net >= 2 c and the depth supports at least 5 contracts at that
margin (`thin` otherwise, counted). Alert text names both exact buys and the ask prices; there is no stake, no size
prompt, no execution. Resolution risk is not in the number: both venues settle on the final score including
overtime, but they are different contracts with different postponement rules (the international Polymarket
event text keeps the market open until played; the US and Kalshi rule texts were not compared here). A cross-venue "arbitrage" is only risk-free if both legs settle the same way.

## Lag check (Pinnacle)

A side priced at least 3 c under Pinnacle's proportionally de-vigged probability for the same proposition, within
10 minutes of a news event for that game, where Pinnacle's own `last_update` is at or after the news (otherwise
Pinnacle is not evidence of anything). News events from data we already collect: a change in a team's
(player, status, starter) fingerprint between successive lineup snapshots (`data/lineups/lineups.duckdb`, read-only),
and a new `Injury-Report_*.pdf` in `data/availability_official/` (event time = file mtime; the PDF is not parsed, so
a new report is an event for every game that day). Whole-number Pinnacle lines are skipped (push). The edge is raw;
the net after the venue fee is printed beside it. This check cannot run in this build's dry run (no
`THE_ODDS_API_KEY` in the agent shell); it is covered by tests on a synthetic Pinnacle body.

## Fees (config objects, verified or refuse)

* Kalshi: `configs/parlay.yaml fee_model` (taker 0.07, `p(1-p)`, per-order round up, verified there). Reused as is.
* Polymarket US: `fee = 0.0695 x C x p x (1-p)`, banker's rounding to $0.01 per order, taker. Source:
  docs.polymarket.us/fees.md, read 2026-10-25, effective 2026-10-19 13:00 ET; the live gateway payload carries
  `feeCoefficient: 0.0695` on every NBA market and the poller skips a market whose coefficient differs from the
  config. Maker rebates (tier 1: none) and taker rebates (tier 1: none) ignored. `verified: true`.
* Polymarket international: `verified: false`, three disagreeing sources (docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees says
  sports taker 0.05; the gamma payload says `zero_fees`; a search result said 0.03). 0.05 is used in the dry run,
  and the poller refuses to enable international unless Devin sets `verified: true`.

## Terms of use (automated reads of public data)

* Polymarket international (polymarket.com): the Terms of Use body is a client-rendered page that my fetch could not
  read (`/tos` returned only the shell and the banner "Trading is blocked in the United States on polymarket.com");
  `/terms-of-use` is a 404. Their API docs publish rate limits for the public read endpoints (gamma `/events`
  500 req / 10 s; CLOB `/book` 1,500 req / 10 s, docs.polymarket.com/api-reference/rate-limits) and say nothing
  against unauthenticated reads. **Not confirmed either way. Devin should read the Terms himself before the
  international venue is enabled** (it is off by default, dry-run only).
* Polymarket US (gateway.polymarket.us): docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/introduction says "The public API requires
  no API key" and "No API key needed" for markets, events, order books and BBO. Its rate-limit page covers the
  Retail API (25 req/s shared) and asks integrators to cache metadata (300 s TTL example), back off on 429 and prefer
  WebSockets to repeated REST polling for market data. No clause forbidding automated reads was found, but the
  participant agreement and exchange rulebook are PDFs I could not read. This poller uses about 70-140 requests a
  minute across all games, well under 25 req/s, caches metadata for 300 s and backs off on 429 (shared `Fetcher`).
  A WebSocket client would be gentler; it is a larger change and is left for Devin to ask for.
  Finding: **no prohibition found for public, unauthenticated, rate-limited market-data reads; the legal documents
  themselves were not readable, so this is "not forbidden in what I could read", not a clearance.**
* Underdog Fantasy (legal.underdogsports.com/?g=42203, Fantasy Terms of Use v7.0): section 6(ix) prohibits
  "[accessing] the Services in an automated way, using any robot, spider, scraper, web crawler" and "any method of
  access other than manually accessing the publicly-available portions of the Services through a browser or ... any
  approved mobile application"; 6(x) prohibits circumventing access measures; 6(xviii) unauthorized scripts.
  **Automated reads are prohibited, so nothing polls Underdog.** The module imports no network library (a test pins
  it). The comparison runs from a CSV a person types.

## Underdog vs Pinnacle (manual input only)

CSV columns: `player,stat,ud_line,pin_line,pin_over_dec,pin_under_dec[,game]` (Pinnacle decimal odds for the main line
of that prop, copied by hand). A prop is flagged when `|ud_line - pin_line| >= 1.0` (0.5 for rebounds, assists,
threes) AND the model P(win) of the favoured side exceeds the 2-pick break-even; otherwise `no_positive_ev_found`.

Payout table ASSUMED (`verified_payouts: false`; check the app): all-correct multipliers 2 picks 3x, 3 picks 6x,
4 picks 10x, 5 picks 20x. Per-leg break-even `m^(-1/n)`: 57.7 % (2 picks), 55.0 % (3), 56.2 % (4), 54.9 % (5).
P(win) comes from a Normal count model anchored to Pinnacle's no-vig P(over) at its line, sd = k x sqrt(mean)
(k = 1.4 points, 1.15 rebounds, 1.1 assists, 0.95 threes; assumed dispersions, not fitted). It supports "worth a manual
look", not a calibrated edge. Pushes void the leg (probability conditioned on no push); Underdog's real push and
boost rules are not modelled.

## Credit estimate (Pinnacle)

3 credits per call (3 markets x 1 bookmaker group; `/events` is free). One call per active game per tick:
180 calls x 3 = 540 credits per game per night, ~4,900 on a 9-game night, 215 peak-month games x 540 = 116,100 a
month = 2.3 % of the 5,000,000-credit plan. It books through the existing `LiveClient` ledger
(`data/odds/budget/the_odds_api_live_<ET date>.json`, `daily_max_credits` 72,000, server floor 100,000), the same
file the live capture uses: a 15-game night of capture (48,060) plus this (8,100) is 56,160, inside the guard, and
the guard refuses a call that would pass it. Not run in this build (no key in the agent shell).

## Changes after the adversary review (7160409 -> this commit)

B1 `unknown_depth` verdict, counted never emitted (event quotes select candidates only; the lag check also
uses booked asks only, M2). B2 every candidate is re-fetched back to back (Kalshi market, then Polymarket US
book), refused if the reads span more than `alert.confirm_max_gap_s` = 5 s, logged with its verdict in
`candidates.jsonl` (which makes "false alerts per night" measurable). B3 Pinnacle errors are isolated per game
and a credit / auth / key error disables the client for the run; each game's body is wrapped. B4 a failed first
tick is `error`, "no games" needs a clean Kalshi tick, the previous tips survive a locked lineups DB, and the
run ends at 06:00 ET at the latest (m1). B5 `RESOLUTION RISK` on every arbitrage line until
`settlement_rules_verified: true`; rules text archived daily. M1 `price_band` is applied to the Kalshi mid in the
scans. M3 live / ended Polymarket events are skipped. M4 heartbeat keys added; watchdog `JOBS` entry and any
delivery to a phone are not done (alerts go to `ALERTS.md` only). m2 `Retry-After` honoured and the rate halved for
the rest of the tick after a 429 (`nba/markets/fastarb/http.py`). m3 Pinnacle only on news or every 10th tick.
m4 refuses to write a Pinnacle body containing the key. m5 `--env-file .env.fastarb`. m6 fee-at-last-fill
noted in `margin_at`. m7 doc wording. m8 the GET-only scan covers the import closure.

Credit estimate after m3: Pinnacle is called while news is inside the 10-minute window plus every 10th tick, so
roughly 60-250 credits per game per night depending on how many injury PDFs and lineup changes land (the
unconditional 540 per game was the upper bound), under the same shared daily ledger.

Polymarket US client interface for other branches: `nba/markets/fastarb/polymarket_us.py` (`PolymarketUSClient`,
`ask_levels`, `quotes`, `is_live`; documented in its docstring and pinned by `test_polymarket_us_interface`).

## Known limits

* Kalshi depth is top-of-book only; a Kalshi leg bigger than that is unknown.
* The lag check needs the key and a live news event; it has not seen a real event yet (tests only).
* Injury-report events are per-day, not per-team (the PDF is not parsed): expect extra lag checks, which cost nothing
  but only alert at 3 c under Pinnacle.
* A WebSocket feed would see gaps faster than a 60 s poll; a gap that lasts under a minute is missed.
* Kalshi `fee_multiplier` per series is not applied by series in this module (the object has none set).
