## Why T2 DEPENDENT is an artifact (the one broken claim)
The frozen T2 shifts every leg by g, the mean residual over **all** of a pool-season's legs. The construction draws at most 20 parlays per date with games picked first. Legs on thin dates therefore appear in many parlays, and legs on busy dates in few. In ge70 the legs that actually sit in parlays are better calibrated (g_w -0.066 / -0.087) than the pool average (-0.084 / -0.096). Under exact independence, E then picks up about m P^(m-1) (g_w - g). For 2-leg ge70 that is about 2 x 0.74 x 0.018, or +0.027, which is the whole observed +0.024. Weight g by appearance and E falls to +0.002 to +0.016, with every interval covering 0. The harness implemented the rule as written, so this is not a code bug: the rule's estimator mismatches its own leg weighting. The pre-freeze review did not catch it, and nor did I. The frozen words stand as the rule's mechanical output. The **reading** in the T490 result note ("legs in the high-probability pools carry a positive cross-game dependence beyond the common bias") is not supported and should be withdrawn. The INDEPENDENCE SUPPORTED words (55 and 65) are unaffected (re-centred E +0.002 to +0.008). The descriptive rho uses the same centring and carries the same caveat.

## Code findings (severity-ranked)
- **major (rule design, T490):** the T2 estimator centres E by the unweighted pool-leg mean, while the parlays weight legs by appearance (`research/eval/alt_parlay_x_gate.py:198`, `g = float(np.mean(hit - p))`). The frozen rule cannot be edited. The fix is to amend the T490 reading and ledger row and to carry the appearance-weighted centring, `g = sum((hit - p) * cnt) / sum(cnt)` with `cnt = np.bincount(idx.ravel(), minlength=len(p))`, into any future parlay rule (new id).
- **minor:** the CI code lives in `research/eval/alt_harness_common.py` (`cluster_matrix`, `boot_ratio`), not `truth/bootstrap.py`. The arithmetic checked out (game- and date-cluster ratio on cluster totals, season-stratified), but it is a second CI implementation. Fix: route future harnesses through `truth/bootstrap.py`, or record the exception in BEST_PRACTICES.
- **minor:** `research/eval/alt_ladder_hist_eval.py:113` `load_model_rows` does not filter `minutes > 0`, unlike `alt_line_70_eval.load_model` (line 152). Harmless today (the stored rows have 0 non-positive minutes), but it is a latent DNP-as-loss path if the model-row store ever gains DNP rows. Fix: add `.filter(pl.col("minutes") > 0)` and read `minutes`.
- No settlement, pricing, timing or join defect found.
