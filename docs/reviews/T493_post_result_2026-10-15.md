### Too good to be true?
- **CAL-A0 (-0.0117) lies outside the modeler's 80% interval (-0.0110 to -0.0040).** It matches what the exposure predicted: the
  gate's shuffled maps scored -0.0078 on these labels, and the scratch's slope map -0.0089 on DraftKings.
- **CAL comes within 0.0017 of the de-vigged price** while using no price. It still trails the market, as the class and lineage
  predict.
- **No other red flag.** The gain is not concentrated in one month or the playoffs, and no group shows a missingness pattern.

## Multiplicity and power
- **Family.** The verdict is a conjunction, so it needs no correction. Per-stat CAL-A0 (the declared secondary, BH m = 4): every
  q_BH is 0.0005, so all four survive.
- **Per-stat CAL-C0 (not declared; computed here).** Every CI upper bound is below 0, so all four survive BH m = 4 at 0.05. pts is
  the weakest (upper bound -0.00012 Pinnacle, -0.00015 DraftKings).
- **The family actually run in this attack** is about 30 contrasts. Only the registered pooled contrasts are claims; everything
  above is descriptive.
- **Power.** The CAL-C0 standard error is 0.00050 at Pinnacle (n 37,528), so the minimum detectable effect at 80% power is about
  0.0014. The observed -0.0037 is about 2.6 times that.

## Playbook check (quantile and distribution layers; recalibration at the line)
1. **Information or capacity.** No new pre-tip information. It re-expresses production's distribution and the line. Half of the
   gain over the base rate is available from the line against a recency average (REC). Production's own increment beyond that
   is -0.0016.

3. **Test validity.** Valid. The inputs are as-of, the lead is 64 min, played legs only, exact x.5 support, and the null is sized.
   The test is weak as evidence because the select season was exposed.
4. **Lineage.** CALIBRATED_GRID (T339) follow-up. Unlike T339, the shuffled-map control does not beat the map here
   (CAL-SH -0.0039 [-0.0048, -0.0029]).
5. **Scored against.** Log loss at the line. It beats raw production and the base rate, and trails the market by 0.0017.
   Reliability is at the line, with slices fixed in advance.

## Single most likely failure mode
If the reading "production carries information at the line" is wrong, the most likely reason is that the gain over the base rate
comes mostly from the line's position relative to recent form (REC gets 54% of it at Pinnacle), plus fg3m. That is not a leak, and
REC is market-free in prices. It still means the proposal should say "the calibrated map beats the base rate, about half of
that is available from a recency baseline, and pts gains almost nothing beyond the base rate". It should not say that
production's pts forecast is informative at the line.
