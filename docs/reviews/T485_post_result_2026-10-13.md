## Inference
- **Multiplicity.** The decision is one intersection (m = 1, no correction), which is correct. Secondary family S (BH, m = 6, as
  run): reb A, ast A, ast B, fg3m A and fg3m B survive (p_bh <= 0.025); reb B does not (p_bh 0.109). The per-step table, slices
  and coverage are unfamilied and descriptive. My extra slices (cohorts, months, drop-50, team) are exploratory, about 30
  contrasts. None reverses a sign, so none needs a correction to read.
- **Power and MDE.** A: bootstrap se 0.0009, so the MDE at 80% power is about 0.0025. With seed variance (se 0.0014) it is about
  0.0039. A = -0.0062 clears both. B: se 0.0008 bootstrap, 0.0013 with seed variance, so the MDE is about 0.0036 with seeds and
  **B = -0.0029 is below the seed-inclusive MDE**.
- **Consequence for the follow-up (SEQ_LEARNING_CURVE_2, windows 9-10).** On this curve one added season is worth about
  0.0009-0.0020. With one 3-seed ensemble per window, a 2-season end contrast is underpowered. Use pooled seed sets (6 or more
  per window) and a seed-inclusive CI, or the follow-up will be unable to conclude.
- **Bootstrap.** Paired, game-clustered, one shared draw set: correct. The CI code is `SharedBoot` in
  `research/eval/seq_learning_curve_eval.py`, a replica of `nba.props.metrics` chunking. `nba/truth/bootstrap.py` does not exist
  yet. **Minor:** flag it as one more CI implementation to fold in when the truth module lands.

## Single most likely failure mode if the claim is wrong
**The last-two-seasons term B is seed noise plus extra warm-refit steps, not data.** Three things point that way:
- B is about 2 seed-sd.
- One replicate pairing loses its CI.
- N = 8 takes about twice the warm-refit gradient steps of N = 6 to N = 4 (pools of 210-237k rows against 156-184k and
  101-129k).

The cold validation curve (1.3123 to 1.3087 main, 1.3105 to 1.3061 replicates, N6 to N8) argues against this. It uses no warm
steps, but it is also the early-stopping set. A real "still rising at 8" is more likely than not. "Still rising enough to justify
seasons 9-10" is weaker than RISING suggests.
