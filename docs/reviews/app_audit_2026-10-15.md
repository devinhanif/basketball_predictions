## Findings

Severity: **blocker** (wrong or dishonest on screen, or the feature is empty on opening night), **major** (misleads,
breaks a flow, or fails a stated rule), **minor**, **polish**.

### Honesty and wrong numbers

**F01 - blocker - Game page and Today (export data) - "book line" printed on a line no book set.**
Steps: open `http://127.0.0.1:4302/#/game/0022690008`, then open any player. The prop header says "Reference line 19.5 (no usable
sportsbook line)", and the distribution bar directly under it labels the same tick "book line 19.5". The SVG aria-label also says
"book line". Shot: `SH/tiles/B_game_open_m375l_01.png`.
Cause: `app/web/src/components.tsx:295,304` hardcode "book line". The `DistLegend` text ("The black line is the number the
sportsbook set") has the same problem.
Fix: label from `prop.line_source`: "line" when posted, "our reference line" when reference. Use the same wording in the aria-label
and the legend. Add a vitest case for a reference prop.

**F02 - blocker - Leg page, "In plain words" - wrong unit in machine-checked prose.**
Steps: `#/leg/0022400001/1628369/pts` (Tatum, line 28.5). Text: "Recent results run 34 points above the model's number". The 34 is
the gap between a 70% last-10 hit rate and the model's 36%, in percentage points, not 34 basketball points. The footnote then
says "Each of the 12 numbers in this paragraph was matched to a figure on this page", so numguard certifies a misleading
sentence. Shot: `SH/tiles/A_leg_pts_m375l_01.png`.
Cause: `api/src/parlayhitters_api/dissect/packet.py:124` (`round(abs(gap) * 100)`) and `dissect/template.py:87`.
Fix: "He went over in 7 of his last 10 (70%); the model gives 36%." Name both rates and drop the derived gap. If a gap stays, write
"percentage points". Add a template test asserting no bare "points" for a rate gap.

**F03 - major - every page footer - "reviewed by a person" is not true of app pages.**
The footer (`components.tsx:90`) says "Written by an automated system and reviewed by a person." Slates, breakdowns and
dissections are generated nightly and nobody reviews them. The spec's disclosure line (APP_CONTENT_SPEC 0.7) was written for
social posts, which do go through the approval queue. Shot: any page, e.g. `SH/tiles/A_leg_pts_m375l_07.png`.
Fix: on app pages, use "Made by an automated system from stored data. Not reviewed by a person before it appears." Keep the
reviewed wording for posts only. This is outward-facing copy, so it is Devin's call (non-negotiable 6).

**F04 - major - My picks - wrong claim about blind picks.**
Steps: on Swipe, open "Stats and line slider", then pick, then go to My picks. The ledger row has `saw_model: true`, but the page still
says "Each pick is saved with ... a note that you never saw our forecast." No row shows which picks were blind.
Shot: `SH/flow_4_picks_after.png`. Cause: `pages/Picks.tsx:141` is static text.
Fix: tag each row "blind" or "saw forecast", and change the sentence to "...and whether you looked at our forecast first."

**F05 - major - Parlay page with `?date=` - two cards contradict each other.**
Steps: `http://127.0.0.1:4303/#/parlay?date=2025-01-27`. The top card says "No parlay today. We had no game forecasts stored for
this date. We looked at 210 open Kalshi markets", which is the 2026-10-11 engine state. It sits directly above "Most confident
tickets" for 2025-01-27. Shot: `SH/tiles/C_parlay_0127_d1280_00.png`. Cause: `ParlayCard` ignores the date
(`api.parlay()` takes none).
Fix: when `date` is set and differs from the parlay state's `report_date`, hide the card or title it "Parlay check for <its
date>". Better: have `/api/parlay-of-the-day?date=` return 404 for dates it does not hold.

**F06 - major - Swipe - the Over button is styled as the primary action.**
Over is the orange filled button and Under the dark one (`SH/tiles/A_swipe_m375l_00.png`). The deck exists to collect blind
over/under answers for leagues and the crowd-signal test (APP_ARCHITECTURE 9). A visual nudge toward Over biases that data
from the first pick, and "overs priced high" is the hypothesis BETTOR_FLOW wants to test.
Fix: give both buttons the same weight (neutral fill, arrow plus text). Keep orange for the drag stamp only.

**F07 - major - Today and Game (export data) - a big "49%" on a reference line means nothing.**
With no sportsbook line, the line is the middle of our own forecast, so P(over) is about 50% by construction. Every prop then
shows "49%" and "about half the time" as its headline number (`SH/tiles/B_today_m375l_01.png`). A fan reads that as information.
Fix: when `line_source === "reference"`, lead with the forecast ("typical night 23 points, half of nights 18 to 28") and drop the
percentage. Show percentages only against a posted line.

**F08 - observation for the modeler, not an app bug - model over-chance runs low on the fixture nights.**
On both fixture slates (494 props with a book price and an outcome), the mean model P(over) against the mean book P(over) and the
realised over rate was:

| Stat | Model | Book | Realised |
|---|---|---|---|
| pts | 44.8% | 49.9% | 48.7% |
| reb | 45.5% | 49.5% | 51.8% |
| ast | 45.8% | 50.1% | 48.3% |
| fg3m | 40.8% | 47.9% | 44.9% |

That is two nights with no clustered CI, so it is a lead, not a claim. I re-derived Tatum's 36% from his stored quantiles (interpolated at
28.5) and it matches, so the app's arithmetic is right. The UI amplifies the gap: stars show 13 to 20 point "disagreements" in big
type (Tatum 36 vs 49, Adebayo 32 vs 52), which reads as "take the under". Route to the modeler; in the app, see F13.

**F09 - minor - hindsight illustration - outcomes promised but not shown; no payout.**
The label says "Outcomes are attached after the fact", but no ticket or leg shows hit or miss, and no ticket shows the payout
multiple (spec 1.3 shows "Pays 62x"). Shot: `SH/tiles/C_parlay_0127_m375l_02.png`.
Fix: per leg, a Hit/Miss chip and the final number; per ticket, "Pays N.Nx" and "all hit" or "k of n hit". Keep the "selected after
the fact" footnote.

### Bugs and flows

**F10 - major - Game - "Back to the night" loses the night you came from.**
Steps: Today, tab "Wed, Nov 20", open a game, tap "Back to the night". You land on Tue, Nov 12. The log shows
`#/?date=2024-11-20 -> #/game/0022400252 -> #/`. Shot: `SH/flow_1_back_link_loses_night.png`.
Cause: `pages/Game.tsx` hardcodes `href="#/"`.
Fix: link to `#/?date=<slate date of the game>`. The game payload carries its date, or use `history.back()` when the referrer is the
slate.

**F11 - major - all pages - browser Back returns to the top.**
Steps: on a slate, scroll to y=2500, open a game, press Back. You are at y=0. Cause: `router.ts:53` calls `window.scrollTo(0,0)`
on every hashchange, including Back. Fix: keep a `Map<hash, scrollY>`. Restore on popstate after the view renders, and scroll to
top only on forward navigation.

**F12 - major - Swipe - arrow keys pick wherever focus is; ArrowDown also scrolls.**
Steps: focus the "Stats and line slider" summary and press ArrowRight. The card is answered Over (Herro, then Paul George in the
log). ArrowDown skips the card and also scrolls the page 40 px. Cause: `pages/Swipe.tsx` window keydown handler, which exempts only
INPUT/SELECT/TEXTAREA.
Fix: handle keys only when the card (`tabindex=0`) or the deck has focus, call `preventDefault()`, and ignore keys when focus is
on a button, summary or link. Use Space or S for skip and leave ArrowDown to scrolling.

**F13 - major - Leg page headline has no book number.**
The three headline tiles are "Last 10", "Last 5" and "Our model 36%". The book's 49% appears nowhere above the fold, although the game
page shows both. That comparison is the one a bettor needs, and its absence makes F08's gap look like a signal.
Shot: `SH/tiles/A_leg_pts_m375l_00.png`. Fix: a fourth tile, "Book 49% (DraftKings, de-vigged)", or a model-vs-book pair inside
the model tile.

**F14 - major - error state shows developer instructions to users.**
Steps: dead API (`http://127.0.0.1:4304/#/`). The panel reads "If you are running this on your own machine, start it with
`uv run uvicorn parlayhitters_api.main:app --app-dir src` in app/api". Shot: `SH/tiles/E_dead_api_m375l_00.png`. An
export refused by its manifest check (503 with a reason) gets the same "did not answer" text, which is wrong.
Fix: user copy ("We can't reach our data right now. Try again in a minute.") and the uvicorn hint only when
`import.meta.env.DEV`. For a 503 with `detail`, show "Today's data failed its checks, so we are not showing it" with the reason.

**F15 - major - PWA - the saved copy is used only when the network throws, not on 5xx.**
`public/sw.js` `apiNetworkFirst` returns any non-ok response as is. My 502 test passed only because Vite's proxy aborted the
connection. Behind Cloudflare, a down origin returns 502/522, and an export refusal returns 503, so users would get the error
panel instead of the last copy. That contradicts APP_ARCHITECTURE section 6 ("PWA serves its cached shell and last forecasts,
labelled stale"). A related gap: pages never visited online show the error panel offline (`SH/flow_6_offline_uncached_game.png`).
Fix: on `res.status >= 500`, serve the cached entry with `X-From-Cache: 1` when one exists, else the response. Warm the cache for the
slate's game pages after Today loads (about 9 KB gzip each). Keep the 503 reason visible when no copy exists.

**F16 - major - opening-night data coverage (export side, not app code).**
The rehearsal export has `box_scores: 0`, `injury_report: 0`, `prop_lines: 0` and `lookback_days: 7` (manifest). All 36 sampled
breakdowns have `prior_games = 0`, so the leg page is "No earlier games are loaded for this player" (`SH/tiles/B_leg_m375l_00.png`),
and the Swipe deck is empty ("No cards: this data holds no sportsbook lines", `SH/B_swipe_m375l.png`). If the 10-20 export looks
like this, the app's two best features (breakdown and Swipe) are blank on opening night.
Fix (export owner, after the 10-18 to 10-20 hands-off window): export last season's box rows for slate players (or a lookback of at
least 365 days for box_scores only) plus the injury report and prop lines. Before opening night, check the 10-19 export's
row_counts.

**F17 - minor - default night is inconsistent.** Export slates come newest-first (`2026-10-16, 2026-10-15`) and fixtures
oldest-first. On 10-15 the Today tab opens Oct 16. Fix: default to today's ET date if held, else the next upcoming one, and sort
the tabs chronologically.

**F18 - minor - Game - "Show the final score above to see how long he played"** appears for games with no final, where no such
button exists (`pages/Game.tsx` PlayerRow). Fix: show the sentence only when `g.final`.

**F19 - minor - console noise.** Every Today and Parlay load logs a red 404 for `/api/top-parlays/<date>`, which the UI treats as a
normal state. Fix: return 200 `{status: "missing"}`, or accept it. It hides real errors in the console.

**F20 - minor - no staleness alarm.** The banner prints "Data as of <time>" but never warns when the export is old (architecture
section 6: "banner shows its age"). Fix: if `data_as_of` is more than 30 h old, say so in the banner, e.g. "This data is 2 days
old".

### Clarity for a casual fan (5-second test)

**F21 - major - Today: the slate is below the fold, after two "Keep your money" panels.**
At 375 px the first screen is "Parlay of the day: No parlay today" plus "Most confident parlays: No tickets file for this
night. Keep your money." The first game starts after about 700 CSS px (`SH/tiles/A_today_m375l_00.png`). The page is about 7,700 CSS px
long. Each prop repeats the 3-line model label ("Model: out-of-fold forecast from a historical season (no model choice is recorded
in this data)"), 24 times per page.
Fix: merge the two parlay cards into one verdict strip ("No parlay tonight - keep your money. Why?"). State the model source once
per page (banner or "How we made this" link). On the game card, show one line per player: name, line, model %, book %, and a
lean arrow only when the gap passes a rule.

**F22 - major - jargon on user-facing pages.** Automated scan of `main` text, plus reading:

| Page | Terms found |
|---|---|
| Parlay (hindsight) | T490, T493, T495, `p_cal`, P(over), "AVG row", "Rule LINE_CALIBRATION (a5441242)", "month-block walk-forward", "greedy by rank", ">= 30 settled", "configured noise, not tested", "2024.." (double period) |
| Swipe slider | "alt-line test T494", "quantile grid (rougher)", "rung map", "out-of-fold" |
| Leg | "walk-forward out-of-fold" stamp, "about 1 in 27 carried over", "Expected extra beyond the forecast" |
| Today/Game (export) | "AVG (sequence + recent-role blend), October 2026 bundle", "availability model R" |
| Parlay (export) | "The parlay evaluation file for this date is missing", "No tickets file for this night" |

Fix: one plain sentence per concept, with the codes moved inside "Show the exact details":
- "calibrated (T494)" becomes "adjusted using how past lines like this one actually finished".
- "model only" becomes "straight from the model, not adjusted".
- "AVG ..." becomes "our main props model".
- "No tickets file" becomes "No tickets tonight yet".

**F23 - minor - the one answer per card is not stated.** Game and Today cards show P(over) but never say which side the model leans or
whether that lean is worth anything. Under the gate-closed rule the honest one-liner is "Model 36%, book 49% - the model leans under,
but it has no track record yet; keep your money." A per-card verdict line is the single biggest clarity win.

**F24 - minor - leg page length and order.** The leg page is about 6,400 CSS px at 375 px with 22 distinct font sizes
(9.9 px to 41.6 px). The "Full breakdown" CTA on the game page comes before the forecast sentence it summarises. Fix: on the leg page,
collapse splits, teammates and on/off under summaries showing their one-line conclusion. On the game page, put the CTA after the bar.

**F25 - minor - Top-parlay tickets lead with the hit chance, not the verdict.** Each ticket's big number is "55%" (chance all legs
hit). The losing EV (-$0.04, low bound -$0.38) is in small grey type below. Fix: lead with "Loses about 4 cents per $1 at
DraftKings' price" and keep the chance as the supporting number. Capitalise "draftkings" in the `why` text (it comes from the
producer).

### The "tech-enabled" feel

The visual primitives are good and honest: the win split bars, the quantile range bar with the line marked, the count histogram
under the slider, the last-10 hit/miss chips and sparkline, and the source chips. What makes it read generic rather than premium:

- **Charts are buried under prose.** Every chart is followed by 2 to 5 sentences restating it. Premium sports-data apps let the chart
  carry the claim and put the words behind a tap. Proposal: one hero visual per card, a model-vs-book dot plot on a 0 to 100%
  track (two dots and a gap). It shows the model's "thinking" (where it lands against the market) in one glance and works for
  win probability and props alike.
- **Too many bordered boxes.** Outlined buttons, outlined tags ("Steady, last 15 games", "65%+ LEG: RAN HIGH IN TESTS"), grey
  chips and grey ticket cards compete. Pick one container style per level: panel, then row, then chip.
- **Type scale.** 22 sizes on the leg page and 12 on Today. Collapse to about 6 steps (12/14/16/20/28/40) with tabular figures.
  Raise 9.9 px and 10.9 px labels to at least 12 px.
- **Number formats.** The same quantity appears as "36%", "about 4 times in 10" and "Scores 29 or more points about 4 times in 10",
  sometimes all on one card. Choose percent for numbers and frequency words for the one-line sentence, not both side by side. Line steps differ: ±0.5 on
  the leg page, ±1 on the swipe slider. Slider marks render as ambiguous ":" and "|" glyphs (`SH/tiles/A_swipe_detail_m375l_01.png`).
  Use labelled ticks.
- **Colour.** Orange carries brand, accent, CTA, "Over", alerts and "tonight" tags at once. Reserve it for one meaning, the model
  for example, and give the book a neutral tone. That also fixes F06.

## If we only do 5 things before opening night

1. **F01**: no "book line" on a line no book set. It is the one place the app currently mislabels a model number as a
   market number.
2. **F02**: the "34 points" sentence. It is wrong on every disagreeing leg and is certified as checked.
3. **F16**: confirm the opening-night export carries box history, the injury report and prop lines. Otherwise the breakdown
   and Swipe are empty however polished they are.
4. **F06 and F12**: neutral Over/Under styling and safe keys. The swipe ledger is research data from the first pick, and
   bias or accidental picks cannot be removed afterwards.
5. **F03 and F14**: the footer claim and the developer error text, the two outward-facing statements that are untrue or
   unprofessional on any day something goes wrong.
