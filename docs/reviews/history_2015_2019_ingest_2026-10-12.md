# History 2015-16 .. 2018-19: ingest, validation and data card (2026-10-12)

Spec: DECISIONS "2026-10-12 17:41 CDT — history extends back to 2015-16". Archivist run; no model was
fit or evaluated; the 2025 holdout and `nba.duckdb` were not touched.

## Verdict

* **Overlap check: PASS.** The downloaded 2019-20 / 2020-21 play-by-play carries every event our own fetched copy
  carries (541,168 and 538,444 event rows matched by `(game_id, event number)` in the 2,304 games both sides have;
  0 unmatched in either direction), with identical period, team, action type, sub type, shot result/value/distance,
  and 1 player id differing in each season. The files are not "row for row" in the literal sense (different
  endpoint: see "What differs" below); every difference is a documented class and none changes a parsed
  possession's outcome, points or lineup in a way that is not explained.
* **Four seasons loaded** (5,249 games: 2015-16 1,316; 2016-17 1,309; 2017-18 1,312; 2018-19 1,312; 1,230 regular-season games each; 111,568 box rows; 1,028,962 possessions with lineups; 279,796 stints; 904,550 shots), history DB only.
* **Derived box vs official box:** exact on all 14 counted stats over 48,930 player-games of the overlap seasons
  (pts, reb, ast, fg3m, fga, fta, fgm, ftm, tov, stl, blk, oreb, dreb, pf: 0 mismatches, after two fixes this very
  comparison found: double personal fouls, and "FLAGRANT.FOUL" matching a technical-foul pattern); minutes within 1.0 min for 99.4% / 98.7% (floor 0.91, target 0.95); starters agree 100% / 99.8%.
* **Independent box sample (step 3): queued, not yet run (see Step 3)**
* **Coverage: complete.** No missing regular-season games; playoff structure consistent (details below).

## Source, licence, handling

shufinskiy/nba_data, Apache-2.0. Archives are in `data/raw/nba_data_shufinskiy_2026-10-12/` with its `LICENSE` and
`list_data.txt` (write-once, not modified). They were extracted with Python `tarfile` (regular files only, no
absolute or `..` paths, `filter="data"`) into the sibling `data/raw/nba_data_shufinskiy_2026-10-12_extracted/`;
all reads run with `python -I`; nothing from the archives is executed. Files used: `nbastats_{2015..2020}`,
`nbastats_po_*`, `shotdetail_*`, `shotdetail_po_*`. The play-in games of 2019-20/2020-21 are not in these files
(not needed: there was no play-in before 2020-21).

distance comes from the text, and from `shotdetail` where 2015-17 text omits it (15-23k shots a season).

games. **Conclusion: trusted for 2015-16 .. 2018-19**, with the clock and assister classes noted below.

## Data card

* **Definition.** `games`, `player_game_stats`, `possessions`, `stints`, `shots`, `game_era_flags` rows for seasons
  2015..2018 in `data/history/nba_history.duckdb` only. `season` is the start year (2015 = 2015-16).
* **Sources.** Play-by-play and shot detail: shufinskiy/nba_data (stats.nba.com export, Apache-2.0). Box: **derived**,
  not fetched. Dates: shot-detail `GAME_DATE`. Final scores: last `SCORE` of the play-by-play.
* **Known-at.** All rows are post-game facts, as for the other history seasons: a feature for game G may use only games
  before G. Nothing here is pre-tip information; there is no injury report before 2018-12-20.
* **Era flags (`game_era_flags`, two columns added).** `no_injury_report` = game dated before 2018-12-20 (all of
  2015-16 .. 2017-18 and part of 2018-19), era-wide, never imputed. `box_derived` = TRUE for seasons before 2019. The
  covid flags are FALSE for these seasons. Rule-change flags (2018-19 14-second reset, 2023-24 participation policy)
  are not stored: they are a function of `season`/date and belong in the learning-curve rule.
* **DNP rows are absent.** The official box lists every rostered player (18.6% zero-stat DNP rows). A play-by-play box
  has a row only for a player who appears in an event or a substitution. Inactive and bench-unused players do not
  exist for 2015-18, so "games played / team games" and any DNP-rate or P(play) feature built from these rows is
  **not comparable** with 2019+. Consumers must read `box_derived` and either restrict to played rows (all of these
  rows with `minutes > 0` are played) or leave P(play) out of the 2015-18 training.
* **Minutes** come from the lineup tracker's stints (1-second clock resolution). 129-167 rows per season have events but
  no stint time (`minutes = 0.0`, stored as 0, not NULL); on the overlap seasons the same gap is 129 and 268 rows (0.5% and 1.1% of played player-games; official minutes mean
2.1 and 5.3, max 27.6 and 39.5 where the tracker desynced). The existing V3-based history has the same gap: 110 and 207
official-played players with no stint row (mean 1.8 and 1.7 min; 48 and 90 under one minute).
* **Imputation.** None. Structural missing (no DNP rows, no tracking/hustle/availability/national_tv/officials) stays
  NULL/absent; unknown missing is not filled.
* **Shots.** `shots` has one row per shotdetail row for the loaded games. Shot-detail is incomplete in 2015-16 and
  2016-17: shot rows equal box FGA in 95.3% and 94.2% of team-games (98 and 116 games differ), 100% in 2017-18 and
  2018-19. Do not use shot counts as FGA before 2017-18.
* **Final scores.** 22 team-games in 19 games of 2015-17 disagree between the feed's last score and the sum of event points
  (16 by +1, 2 by +3 in one game, and two 2017 games where the feed's score stops early: 85-89 and 103-124 against box
  sums of 116-125 and 120-128). `games.home_pts/away_pts` is the feed's last score and is not corrected; all 19 games
  are in the validation sample, which will say which side is right.
* **Attribution.** Contains data from shufinskiy/nba_data (Apache-2.0), derived from stats.nba.com; the licence text is
  kept at `data/raw/nba_data_shufinskiy_2026-10-12/LICENSE`.

## Playbook check

No model class is involved (data ingest and validation). Questions 1-5 applied to the data: information (nothing here is
pre-tip), enough data (4 seasons, 5,249 games), test validity (derived box tested against two independent official
boxes: the overlap and the sample), lineage (existing parser and tracker reused, mapping verified on the overlap, two
bugs it exposed were fixed before the load), scored against (our own fetched copy and the official box).

## No-signal note

Nothing about predictive value was tested. The overlap, the derived-box reconciliation and the coverage all came out
clean; the one structural cost is the missing DNP rows.
