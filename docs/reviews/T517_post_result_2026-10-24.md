## Inference
- **Baseline fairness.** MR and MM have the same legs, months, weights, ridge and training window. MR carries the main effect of every
  covariate that MM interacts with (edit 1). Fair: yes.
- **Multiplicity.** The primary is one pre-declared contrast, so it gets no correction. Secondary family of 10 under BH: smallest BH q 0.152, **no
  secondary test survives** (C_mod pts p 0.016 / q 0.152; reb 0.047 / 0.157). The family actually run also includes 25 descriptive slices,
  the DK direction check, Part L and the exchange slice. None carries a decision weight. My 30-odd attack contrasts are robustness reads,
  not new claims.
- **Power / MDE.** The binding se is about 0.000162 (player-season). The MDE at 80% power (two-sided 5%) is about 2.8 x 0.000162 = **0.00045**,
  above the observed 0.00033, so the detection sits below the design's reliable-detection size and is exposed to winner's curse. Averaging
  2023 and 2024 gives about -0.00025. **Stage 3 consequence:** at about 165 live dates with a date-clustered se of about 0.00015, a true
  C_mod of -0.00025 to -0.00033 has power of only about 0.3-0.6. A forward null would be "underpowered, cannot conclude", not "no effect". The
  Stage 3 gate must compute this before it is frozen.
- **Bootstrap.** Paired, clustered and seeded (seed 0), with the pinned `research.eval.model_clv_eval.Boot`. `nba/truth/bootstrap.py` does
  not exist at this base, as rule section 14 says. Flagged as a known deviation, minor. Percentile intervals with 30 to 35 clusters
  (team, week) are, if anything, anti-conservative, so the team-cluster crossing is not a small-sample artefact against the claim.
- **Too good to be true?** No. The gain is a third of the info floor. But it is **concentrated**: 41% in 5 of 414 player-seasons and 23% in one
  team. The house rule treats that as "presumed artefact until explained". The explanation here is sampling concentration, not leakage
  (checks 1-3 and 6).
- **Lineage.** With all three interactions dropped, C_mod is -0.00035 [-0.00061, -0.00009]. The claim reduces to the stat-specific `delta`
  weight, which is ODDS_HISTORY H2 / MARKET_SHRINK on the same two seasons. It is not independent evidence for either (rule section 2 says so).
  reb is again the most negative stat, and pts carries the 2024 gain but flips sign in 2023.

## Single most likely failure mode
**Sampling luck concentrated in a few 2024 player-season / team rotations**, not leakage. The true model increment is probably about
-0.0002 or smaller. The 2024 detection is the favourable tail: it relies on 5 player-seasons and one team, it crosses 0 under team clustering
and under 1 of 20 seeds, and the 2023 CI covers 0 with pts reversing sign.
