**Note on check 1.** A 50/50 pmf mixture is more dispersed than either member. When labels are shuffled within date, the outcome
becomes the date's marginal, and the more dispersed forecast wins by construction. So label-shuffling AVG against a single arm measures
the mixture, not the information. On the true labels that mixture effect is about zero: AVG_S - FULL -0.0007, AVG_P - FULL -0.0021,
AVG_perm - FULL +0.0007. The 2025 null is degenerate (sd 1.1e-6, so the floor sigma 0.0001 applies). By 2025 the re-learned gate puts
about zero weight on a permuted member (SEQc_perm - A0 = 1e-6), so AVG_perm is about 0.5 A0 + 0.5 FULL. It still answers "what if SEQ
carried no player-specific information". Future mixture rules should not list a raw label shuffle as a guard.

## Inference

* **Baseline fairness.** FULL is the frozen 73f3f4b0 construction, regenerated through the approved Stage-2 path. Gates A and B are
  exact on all four stats for 2024, and the T473 identity holds on 2025 to 1e-12. The rows are identical: 28,173 on every arm, all
  played. The walk-forward cadence is monthly on both sides. FULL trains on 2022+ while SEQ adds 2019-21. That is the tested
  difference ("SEQ adds to FULL"), not an unfairness. But it means part of the gain is data volume that FULL could also use (see risk).
* **CI correctness.** `ensemble_eval.clustered` calls `nba.props.metrics.paired_score_delta_ci` (cluster bootstrap by `game_id`, ratio
  of resampled sums, exact). My independent implementation agrees. `nba/truth/bootstrap.py` does not exist, so the CI code lives in
  `nba/props/metrics.py` (minor; the rule names "the MODEL_CLV bootstrap").
* **Multiplicity.** The decision family is primary m = 1. The 2025 contrasts actually computed in this run were primary, AVG-A0, 3
  margins, the null and 6 secondaries (12). Every p is the bootstrap floor 0.0005, so Bonferroni over 12 gives 0.006: the primary
  survives. Secondary BH m = 7: all p_BH 0.0005. These clear the -0.005 floor with CI upper < 0: pts AVG-A0, reb AVG-A0 -0.0077, ast
  AVG-A0 -0.0058. These do not: reb/ast/fg3m AVG-FULL (-0.0041 / -0.0038 / -0.0031) and fg3m AVG-A0 -0.0037.
* **Power.** CI half-width 0.0024 (MDE about 0.0024). The observed effect is 5.5 half-widths from 0 and 3.5 from the floor.
* **Too good to be true?** No. AVG-A0 -0.0249 is about one fifth of the context-residual production gain. SEQc alone beats FULL
  (3.1224 vs 3.1325). Coverage 80% is 0.792 and bias -0.15 pts. The gain is spread over months, teams and tips.
* **Where the gain sits** (it matches 2024): low outcomes (pts <= median -0.0218 vs above -0.0042; actual < FULL q10 -0.128), minutes
  5-20 (-0.0241), starters (-0.0181), p_play >= 0.9 (-0.0154 vs -0.0070). AVG loses on minutes < 5 (+0.0439 [+0.0360, +0.0518]) and the
  postseason (+0.0047 [-0.0026, +0.0120]).

## Playbook check (class: neural sequence model, as a 50/50 pmf mixture with a gradient-boosted residual model)

1. **Information or capacity?** Mostly data volume (2019-21 history), with no new pre-tip information. Real-tip tokens cost +0.0013 to
   +0.0017 on 2023/24. This is a capacity/data win, which our record says is rare, so this review treated it as presumed leak. Nothing
   was found.
2. **Enough data for this class?** W_ALL is six seasons, but there was no learning curve. Whether the history gain is still rising is
   unknown (the redo list's SEQ learning curve).
3. **Test valid?** Yes, apart from the process gap: real tips (clock verified for every season), played-row population identical across
   arms, integer support, a power margin, guards sized from a null. The label-shuffle form is invalid for mixtures (above).
4. **Lineage.** This is essentially ENSEMBLE E3 (T443), which the era-embedding probe (P-emb) disqualified. That guard was dropped at
   the freeze without being stated (DECISIONS 16:10, main-session miss). The post-verdict descriptive probe is committed but **not yet

5. **Scored against.** Whole-distribution integer CRPS vs FULL. It has not reached the line (T477; LINE_CALIBRATION is the lever).

## The single biggest risk if the claim is wrong
It is not a leak; I found none. The claim is narrower than "the sequence model is better". It is a points-CRPS gain from a GPU model
trained on three more seasons than FULL, measured in one season with one set of seeds (the Colab rerun is GPU noise on the same seeds,
not a replicate). Half of it sits in the top 50 players. Part of it may be data volume that a CPU model given the same 2019-21 history
would also capture. And the live report feed will be fresher than the 2022-24 archive the model trained on. Read as a production
case, the most likely way this disappoints is the live path: no Colab-free nightly path exists, so it cannot be served as tested.
