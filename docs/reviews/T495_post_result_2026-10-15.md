## Why the shuffle wounds T2's meaning (not its word)

Permuting leg outcomes within a date keeps each date's base rate and destroys every per-leg link between probability and
outcome. Under that null, CAL still beats raw by about 0.010-0.013 log loss per ticket. Raw is over-confident (raw gap
-0.043 to -0.049), and any less confident forecast wins log loss against random labels.

So "CAL HELPS THE JOINT" is real (it is log loss, and confidence matters), but it is a confidence correction. A constant
shrink of the raw legs would earn much of it. The evidence that CAL's ticket probabilities also carry correctly scaled
information is the slope: about 1 against about 0 under shuffle. The ticket-level information itself is small, consistent
with T493 (CAL leg log loss 0.6878 against a coin's 0.6931).

## What the TOP lean implies for the panel

1. **The number shown on a top pick is too high, by about 3 to 5 points for DraftKings and 4 to 10 points for Pinnacle.**
   The cause is CAL's high-q tail: q >= 0.65 legs are 3 to 9 points over-confident in both seasons and both books. A top-3
   ranking selects that tail by construction, so it is a winner's curse on the leg map, compounded across 2 or 3 legs.
2. **Ranking is fine; the level is not.** Within random tickets the p quintiles are calibrated, so ordering by
   `p_joint_cal` is reasonable. What breaks is the displayed level at the extreme.
3. **Do now, no rule needed** (a display caveat, Devin's call). Change the panel label from "legs calibrated; joint not yet
   tested" to something like: "On random 2023-24 parlays these chances were accurate. The top picks, which use the
   most-confident legs, came in about 3-5 points lower than shown." Keep "keep your money".
4. **Fix properly through a new pre-registered rule** (one follow-up, not variant-chasing). Choose one:
   - (a) A tail-aware leg map: CAL plus a monotone tail correction, or isotonic regression on the top decile, fit
     walk-forward and scored at q >= 0.60 on 2024. This fixes the cause and helps singles too.
   - (b) A TOP-specific joint shrink: J fitted on TOP tickets only, logit p = a + b logit p_joint_cal, walk-forward,
     confirmed on the live log.

   Claude's recommendation is (a), because the error is in the legs. Until then the live TOP slice (frozen LIVE words) is the
   test, and its -0.05 floor is about the size of this lean.
5. **Do not shift the displayed number by hand** (for example "minus 4 points"): that would be a rule change without a rule.

## The single most likely failure mode if T495 is read wrong

Reading "all four CALIBRATED, CAL helps" as "the panel's chances are honest". They are honest for an average random
parlay. The panel shows the opposite of average.
