## Inference
- **Multiplicity.** The decision family is m = 2 (A_m, E), with Bonferroni 97.5% as declared; that is correct for the family run. A_m p = 0.0005 survives. E is a bounded-equivalence word, not a test of a nonzero effect. The secondary S family is BH m = 6, as run: the three A_m rows survive, the three E rows do not. My extra slices are about 15 exploratory contrasts. None reverses A_m's sign, so none needs correcting to read.
- **Power and MDE.** The realised two-way se is 0.00128 (A_m) and 0.00120 (E), so the MDE at 80% power and 97.5% is about 0.0037-0.0039 (the rule stated 0.0034).
  - A_m = -0.0058 clears it.
  - E cannot see one season's worth of curve (about 0.001-0.002, T486) and is not meant to. FLAT here means "two more seasons do not give a gain of 0.0022 or more".
- **Bootstrap.** The bootstrap is clustered by game, two-way with seed resampling, and seeded (game seed 0, seed streams `[0, 20261013, arm]`). It is conservative for every contrast inside one window, because the cold nets are shared. **Minor:** the CI code is again a local replica (`seed_draw_rows`, `two_way_draws`), not `nba/truth/bootstrap.py` (still absent); flag it for the fold-in, as in T486.
- **Baseline fairness.** Both sides have the same rows, the same FULL, the same per-arm blend fit and the same S1 handling. Against DATA, M(8) sees the 2023-24 in-season rows about half as often per refit as U(4) does (U_4's step count spread over a 2x pool), so the matched construction is, if anything, conservative.

## Single most likely failure mode if the claim is wrong
**E = FLAT read as "zero".** The bound excludes the 6->8 slope but not a diminishing return of about 0.001-0.0015 per two seasons. Seed noise sets the bound (6 seeds), not game noise.

A_m being wrong would need a channel from 2015-18 rows into 2023-24 inputs, and none exists: inputs are hash-identical, pools are strictly earlier, S1 shows no missingness asymmetry, and the gain is largest for players S1 never saw. Steps cannot carry it either (paired step share +/-0.0013). The remaining open item for A_m is external validity: one select season, 2023-24, with no 2024-25 replication.
