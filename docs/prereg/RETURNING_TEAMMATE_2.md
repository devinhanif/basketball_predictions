## Hypothesis, prior evidence, forking paths (read first)
* **Hypothesis.** When a rotation teammate who missed at least N straight team games and was report-listed is back tonight (not OUT or doubtful on the T-60 report), the players who absorbed his minutes give them back; production, whose recency windows are full of absence games and whose `n_out_rot` is 0 tonight, over-forecasts them.
* **Descriptive evidence (T476; `reports/race_tanking_eda_2026-10-12.md`; 2023-24; stored A0; not a claim):** teammates' pts, actual minus A0 median, vs baseline: star (30+ min) back game 1 -0.81 [-1.06, -0.55]; 174 star-seasons; games 1-3 of the star's absence +0.47 [+0.30, +0.65]; FULL does not close it. The -0.81 is CONDITIONAL ON THE STAR HAVING PLAYED (post-hoc); at T-60 he plays 77% (gate).
* **Forking paths.** The idea, the -0.81 and the star = 30+ mpg definition were found on 2023-24 (the pooled test seasons here: option B waives the split). 2023 and 2024 are not clean for this question; a pass is a candidate and only the 2026-27 live log (or one logged 2025 touch, not proposed) can confirm. Choices made after seeing an outcome-free statistic and disclosed: N = 3 (T476's definition); `doubtful` returners excluded (v1's gate measured doubtful returners playing 1.0% of 204); `ret_min_listed` added (this gate shows unlisted returners play 38-45% against 49-88% for listed ones); the primary population is v1's "listed on the previous game's report" and NOT the brief's literal "OUT on the previous N reports", because the literal one halves n (4,208 / 4,520 affected rows, half-width 0.0098 / 0.0103) while raising the star play rate to 81%; the literal definition is kept as a declared SLICE (`out_run_N3`). No stat outcome under the new columns and no arm has been scored.

## Features (as-of; `research/eval/returning_teammate_2_features.py::build_returning`, unit-tested)
For player i of team T in game g (`k` = T's game order in the season by real tip, ties by `game_id`), a **returner** j != i satisfies, using information knowable at T-60 before g only:
* j played (minutes > 0) for T earlier this season, played none of T's last N = 3 games before g, and played for no other team since (traded players drop out);
* j is listed (any status) on the latest usable snapshot of T's PREVIOUS game (the report-listed absence). A game with no usable snapshot breaks the run: no returner, no imputation;
* g has a usable snapshot (`ret_known` = 1; else every column is 0 and `ret_known` = 0, the fact production stores as `has_report`), and j is neither `out` nor `doubtful` on it. A return missing from tonight's report (unlisted) still counts (he is not OUT) but contributes nothing to `ret_min_listed`.
Columns (0 when nobody returns): `ret_min` (sum over returners of j's mean minutes in his last 10 played games before g), `ret_min_star` (same, mean >= 30), `ret_min_listed` (same, only returners listed available / probable / questionable tonight), `n_ret`, `ret_use` (sum of j's mean usage share over those games, (fga + 0.44 fta + tov) / the team's total in that game: his usage share), `ret_missed` (longest streak among returners, cap 30), `ret_own_gain` (i's mean minutes in T's last min(5, ret_missed) games minus his mean over his 40 played games before that window; 0 if either empty). Sources: `player_game_stats` rows of earlier games, `games`, `player_availability` (source `nba_official_report`), nothing from g's box except the list of who gets a row. `plays_tonight` (descriptive) never feeds a column.
* **Slices of the affected rows** (declared, n >= 300, else not reported): star returner (`ret_min_star > 0`) vs rotation-only; games missed 3-4 / 5-9 / 10+; listed tonight vs unlisted; **`out_run_N3`**: the brief's literal definition (j listed `out` on each of the previous 3 snapshots, not out tonight), built by the same code with `prev_rule="out_all"`.
