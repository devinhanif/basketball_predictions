## Why this rule exists (and the arithmetic that limits it)
SEQ_LEARNING_CURVE (T485, wounded T486) and SEQ_LEARNING_CURVE_2 (T487, SURVIVES T488) found, on one select season (2023-24, pts integer CRPS of AVG = 0.5 SEQc + 0.5 FULL, n 27,619 / 1,318 games): going from 4 to 8 training seasons helps through data, not extra warm steps (A_m -0.0058 [-0.0087, -0.0029], 97.5%; step share +0.0000 [-0.0011, +0.0013]), and 10 vs 8 is FLAT as a bound (E +0.0007 [-0.0022, +0.0032]). Every window contrast was measured on 2023-24 only. The parent rule closed the curve and required "its own 2024-25 replication rule (select = this result, report season = 2024, scored once, pre-registered)" before any production-window change.
Three facts shape this draft, all measured, none a result of the new arms:
1. **The served window already grows.** AVG_SHADOW_LIVE_2 pins the W_ALL pool "to 2019-20 on". In the 2026-27 monthly refits that is 2019-20 .. 2025-26 (7 completed seasons) plus the current season's earlier months. The 8-season window of the brief (2018-19 .. 2025-26) therefore differs from the served pool by **one season (2018-19)**. Left alone, the served pool reaches 8 completed seasons in the 2027-28 refits. The production-relevant increment (7 vs 8) is expected to be about -0.0008 and cannot be detected (this rule's MDE is 0.0027); no rule on one report season can test it. What *can* be tested is the three-season analog on 2024-25 (5 vs 8 seasons: served pool 2019-23 vs 2016-23), which asks whether the T487 curve is a 2023-24 artefact. A PASS supports the curve; it does not measure the 7-to-8 step.
2. **The history gain fades as the current-era pool fills.** SEQ_FULL_MIX_RT run 1: the 2019-21 history step (W_ALL minus W22) was -0.0120 in 2023 and -0.0072 in 2024; the adversary's gain attack found the gain concentrated in October-November. The same fade should apply to 2016-18: the 5-vs-8 contrast read off T487's unmatched arms on 2023-24 is about -0.0044 (C_U(8) - C_U(6) -0.0030 plus half of C_U(6) - C_U(4) -0.0028); times the observed fade ratio 0.6 gives an expected -0.0026 on 2024-25, a hair above the floor below. The test is near its own floor on purpose and may well end INCONCLUSIVE.
3. **The deployable change is a bundle.** Pre-2019 rows only enter through the SEQ_LEARNING_CURVE construction (past-only normalizer, rule-flag and S1 era channels, S1 segment). The served model is the RAW construction (T478). So Devin would switch to normalizer + flags + S1 + the longer window together; the rule scores the bundle against the served arm and separates the window from the construction.

## Metric, contrasts, CIs (written before any arm is run)
* **Metric:** pts integer-grid CRPS on 2024-25, AVG = 0.5 SEQc + 0.5 FULL (the parent's blend: walk-forward softmax gate, 30-day blocks, 3,000-row minimum, strictly earlier rows of the test season, 0.5 / 0.5 before the first fit, weights fit once on the full 18-seed ensemble and held fixed in the bootstrap; postseason rows route to A0 in every arm and therefore add exactly 0 to every contrast, 6.5% of rows in 2023-24, disclosed, the regular-season figure is printed too). The job asserts identical row sets and identical test-input hashes across all five arms. `C_X` = CRPS of AVG built from arm X.
* **Primary: W = C(C8) - C(C5)** (the window effect at a fixed construction, three seasons added). Negative is better. Floor **-0.002** (derived, not tuned to the gate: the parent's floor -0.003 spans four added seasons, -0.00075 per season; three seasons give -0.00225; rounded to -0.002).
* **Co-condition B = C(C8) - C(S)** (what Devin would actually switch to, against the served arm). Floor -0.002, same CI rule.
* **Control G-S1 (direction only) = C(C8s) - C(C8)**: replacing the real S1 targets by permuted ones must make the model worse.
* **Descriptive, no family:** C6 - C5 (the 2018-19-only increment), C8 - C6, C5 - S (construction alone), cold best-validation loss by arm, reb / ast / fg3m for W and B (BH over those six, cannot change a verdict), calibration coverage q05..q95 for SEQc, month blocks, regular-season-only figure.
* **CI:** 2,000 two-way draws drawn once and shared by every contrast: resample `game_id` with replacement (seed 0) **and**, independently within each arm, resample its 18 seeds with replacement (the parent's `two_way_draws`; the CI code is a local replica of the not-yet-existing `nba/truth/bootstrap.py`, flagged for fold-in). **95% percentile intervals; the family is one (W), so no Bonferroni;** B and G-S1 are conjunctive conditions, not extra tests. A date-clustered variant (207 dates) is a robustness print, not a decision input.
* **Power, stated now (components measured on T487's results.json, run 20261014_114309):** the game-clustered paired se of a window contrast is 0.00064-0.00075 (U_8 - U_6 and U_6 - U_4 game-only 95% half-widths 0.00125 / 0.00130; a three-season span assumed 0.00075); the seed component of a two-arm contrast at 6 seeds per arm is sqrt(0.001261^2 - 0.00075^2) = 0.00101 (A_m two-way 95% half-width 0.002471), so at 18 seeds 0.00101 x sqrt(6/18) = 0.00058. Combined se for W about **0.00095, 95% half-width 0.0019, 80%-power MDE 0.0027**. Power of the W condition (CI upper < 0) is about 0.56 at a true -0.0020 (the floor) and 0.78 at the expected -0.0026. B crosses constructions (less paired): se about 0.0012, half-width 0.0023, fine against its expected -0.0045.

### Decision words (the gates below and the reproduction gate pass first, else nothing is read)
* **REPLICATED:** W <= -0.002 with 95% CI upper < 0, **and** B <= -0.002 with 95% CI upper < 0, **and** G-S1 holds (C(C8s) - C(C8) has a one-sided 90% lower bound > 0, point > 0), **and** no slice guard fires. (The G-S1 threshold is direction only and sized from the normal approximation, not from a permutation null: a 90-fit permutation null is out of reach; its false-fail rate is about 0.14 at the expected effect and its false-pass rate 0.10, both stated in the prior, and the adversary prints the seed spread.)
* **NOT_REPLICATED:** W 95% CI lower > -0.002 (a gain of the floor's size is excluded). **HURTS** is the subset with W lower > 0.
* **INCONCLUSIVE:** everything else, including W passing while B, G-S1 or a slice guard does not (the failing condition is named: W_ONLY, WINDOW_NOT_BUNDLE, S1_CONTENT_NOT_CONFIRMED, SLICE).
* **Slice guard:** for each of starters, bench, realised minutes < 5, `n_prior < 20`, Oct-Nov, Dec-Jan, Feb-Apr (n >= 300 each, fixed now), W's 95% lower bound must not exceed +0.003. (T479: AVG is worse than FULL at minutes < 5; the window must not make that worse.)
* Reproduction and construction gates: **S** (RAW, N = 5) AVG minus FULL on 2024-25 pts within **0.003 of -0.0155** (SEQ_FULL_MIX_RT run 1, W_ALL, 2024; seeds 0-2 there, a pooled 18 here; the band is about 3 sd of a 3-seed pool); FULL and A0 integer grids equal the stored arrays to 1e-6 (T467: FULL minus A0 pts 2024 -0.0083145); run-time era gate: on 100% of 2024-25 test rows the era vector equals 2023-24's (all `rule_*` = 1, the covid channels, S1 and report-dropped channels = 0); epoch-cap diagnostic: more than one third of the (arm, seed) cold fits at the 25-epoch cap with the best epoch in the last four makes the verdict INVALID (budget). S outside its band is **INCONCLUSIVE (construction)** (the parent's declared fallback; not a VOID, the harness is not re-run under this id); any other gate failing is VOID (gate): harness fixed, rule untouched, nothing read.

## What each choice protects against
* Moving the test to 2024: a curve that is an artefact of 2023-24 (one season's pool size, October-November concentration).
* A second construction arm (C5): crediting the window with the normalizer/flag gain (T485: NORM minus RAW -0.0023 at N=4).
* B as a co-condition: a window gain that is real at a fixed construction but is eaten by the construction change Devin would have to ship.
* C8s: a "window gain" that is really extra rows of anything (dilution, steps, era identification) rather than content of 2016-18; the one S1 attack T488 could not run.
* 18 seeds and the two-way CI: seed noise, which set E's interval in T487 (game-only half-width 0.0012 vs two-way 0.0027).
* A floor equal to the gate half-width: a point that clears the floor but whose CI crosses zero, and the reverse.
* No Bonferroni and no hierarchy: one primary; B, G-S1 and the slices are conjunctions, so they can only make the rule harder to pass.
* S as the served arm, not the 4-season arm: comparing the candidate to a window nobody serves.
