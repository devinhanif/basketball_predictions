Shallower ladders exist for the other stats: the share of BetRivers player-stat-games with >= 2 lines is rebounds 85% / assists 62% / threes 59% in 2023 and 26% / 24% / 18% in 2024 (the share with >= 3 lines is only 26%, 8%, 2% in 2023 and 8%, 4%, 1% in 2024). - The store has two captures per game, not prices "over the day": `t60` (62.6 to 79.4 minutes before tip in 2023, about 64.4 in 2024; this is BEFORE the model's report cutoff of tip - 60) and `t5` (9.3 to 9.4 minutes before tip; AT OR AFTER the cutoff).

## Part 1: calibration at P >= 0.70 (no prices)
**Question.** Among all half-point lines whose stored model P(over) is at least 0.70, does the hit rate equal the mean predicted P?

**Object and units.**
1. Forecast: `reports/odds_history/model_rows_{2023,2024}.parquet`, the production walk-forward context-residual forecast as stored (month blocks, trained strictly before each block, real-tip report gating, integer support; its A0 check equals the LOWER_TAIL production arm to 1e-6). `p_ge[k-1] = P(Y >= k)`, k = 1..K (K is the row length; pts 80-84, reb 35, ast 30, fg3m 18-19). Stats: pts, reb, ast, fg3m. No refit, no recalibration, no map.
2. Row universe: player-games with minutes > 0 (all stored rows; 0 rows have null or non-positive minutes). So P is conditional on playing, exactly as a sportsbook voids a DNP. Stated, not hidden.
3. Unit: a **line-row** = (player-game, stat, k) with line L = k - 0.5, P = P(Y >= k) on the 1/199 grid, hit = 1 if Y >= k. Eligible iff P >= 0.70 (tolerance 1e-12). Every k in the support is a line; no market is needed. Many lines have P = 1.0 (line 0.5 for a starter); they are counted and reported (pts 26,842 / 27,557 in 2023 / 2024), they sit in the 0.95+ bin.
4. Reliability bins: [0.70,0.75), [0.75,0.80), [0.80,0.85), [0.85,0.90), [0.90,0.95), [0.95,1.0], and the sub-row P = 1.0 inside the last.
5. **Headline band [0.70, 0.90).** Why: above 0.90 a posted price is -900 or shorter, where one point of probability is worth under 1.2% of ROI and the book's overround (8.4% to 9.2% at the eligible BetRivers rungs, gate) dominates; the 0.95+ bin is mostly lines no book posts. The six-bin table is still reported in full.
6. Distance from the main line (model ruler, defined from the forecast only so Part 1 stays price-free): the **main line** of a player-game-stat is the half-point line whose P is closest to 0.5 (ties to the lower line); distance d = (main line - L) / u with u = 2 points for pts and 1 for reb, ast, fg3m. Bins: (-inf,0], (0,1], (1,2], (2,4], (4,inf). Reported inside the headline band. A bin with < 1,000 line-rows or < 300 games is INSUFFICIENT (no word). The market ruler (distance from a book's main rung) is defined in Part 2 and applies only there.

**Statistic.** gap = (hits / n) - mean(P) over the line-rows of a cell, a ratio estimate weighting every line-row equally. Reported with n line-rows, n player-games, n games, hit rate, mean P, gap, and for every bin the break-even price at the claimed P (decimal 1/P, American equivalent) and at the realised hit rate (decimal 1/h): the price at which that rung type is fair under each belief.

**CI.** Game-clustered percentile bootstrap, B = 5,000, `numpy.random.default_rng(2026)`, resampling `game_id` with replacement, one resample matrix per season shared by every cell (lines within a player-game are nested, players within a game share the game, so the game is the right cluster). A date-clustered bootstrap (resample `game_date`, same B, `default_rng(2027)`) is run beside it. **The decision interval is the union: lower = min of the two lowers, upper = max of the two uppers.** Two-sided p for each clustering = 2 min(P(b <= 0), P(b >= 0)) with a +1 correction; **the p used for BH is max(p_game, p_date)**, so the p-value is never narrower than the interval it accompanies.

**Decision words (per stat x season headline cell; verbatim).**
- **CALIBRATED:** |gap| <= 0.02 AND the decision interval lies inside [-0.03, +0.03].
- **OVERCONFIDENT:** gap < -0.02 AND interval upper < 0 AND BH q < 0.05.
- **UNDERCONFIDENT:** gap > +0.02 AND interval lower > 0 AND BH q < 0.05.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else (including a wide interval).
BH is over the 8 headline cells (4 stats x 2 seasons). CALIBRATED is an equivalence statement and has no p-value. **Margin disclosure (review B2).** The +-0.03 margin and the 0.03 gate threshold were chosen after the conservative half-widths (0.0215 to 0.0225 in the headline band) were counted, and before any outcome was read. Under the margin of Devin's first sketch (+-0.02) the conservative-bound gate FAILS in 8 of 8 headline cells: the 2023-24 data cannot decide a +-0.02 equivalence on that bound. The bootstrap interval may be narrower, which is unknown until scoring. Devin chose +-0.03 on 2026-10-14 13:12. This differs from the sketch Devin gave ("within +-0.02 with the CI covering the mean"): a CI that must contain exactly 0 turns a practically irrelevant but detectable 0.8-point gap into "not calibrated" at n of 76,000 lines, and a wide CI that happens to contain 0 would pass; equivalence inside +-0.03 avoids both. 0.02 of probability is about 2.7% of ROI at the BetRivers median decimal price 1.36, under a third of the overround.
- **Stat word:** the same word in 2023 and 2024, else MIXED.
- **Model word: "calibrated at 70%+"** iff all four stat words are CALIBRATED. If any stat word is OVERCONFIDENT the model word is "OVERCONFIDENT at 70%+ for <stats>". Otherwise "NOT SHOWN calibrated" with the list.
- Bin-level and distance-level words use the same definitions, are descriptive, and carry a BH q over their own family (6 bins x 4 stats x 2 seasons = 48) only if anyone quotes one directional bin.

**Descriptive decomposition (no word).** The same gap for line-rows that BetRivers posted at the primary capture, `t5` (the Part 2 set) against the remainder: separates "the model is miscalibrated at 70%+" from "the lines a book posts near the main line are where the model is overconfident" (CALIBRATED_GRID: the grid is calibrated overall and overconfident exactly at the book's line). The decomposition is also split starter / bench (`starter10 >= 0.5`), because BetRivers posts a points quote for about 77-83% of starter player-games and 7-12% of bench ones (review N5): a Part 1 word does not automatically carry to the bet set.

**What Part 1 cannot say.** It is not a profit claim and not advice. It covers lines no book posts. A CALIBRATED word at 70%+ says nothing about whether a bettor beats a price.

## Part 2: profit at posted prices on $20 a week
**Scope.** Posted lines in the stored store, BetRivers as the fixed book. Because ladders are partial (see the correction above), the BetRivers set has two kinds of rung: ladder rungs (deep for points; two to three lines for the other stats) and single main lines (mostly reb, ast, fg3m). Its eligible rungs are 76% points in 2023 and 65% in 2024 (gate). The result is therefore mostly a points-ladder result plus main-line disagreements, and is reported by stat.

**Bet set A (primary).** In order; each exclusion is counted and reported:
1. Source: `odds_history`, `snapshot_kind = 't5'` (the primary capture, see the timing rule below), `book = 'betrivers'`, markets player_points / rebounds / assists / threes, `player_id` not null, seasons 2023 and 2024.
2. Two-sided: both over and under priced at the same half-point line in the same snapshot; whole-number lines and one-sided quotes are dropped (counted; no pushes can occur). De-duplication: a (book, game, player, stat, point) key quoted more than once on a side is dropped entirely and counted (no arbitrary survivor). Arm A asserts zero such keys (BetRivers has none in either season); in 2023 other books do (2,016 over rows at t5, all dropped).
3. **Timing rule (as-of, never before the model's cutoff).** The model's report cutoff is the real tip minus 60 minutes. The price snapshot must satisfy `tipoff_utc - 60 min <= snapshot_ts < tipoff_utc` for 100% of rows (asserted; real tip from `data/ops/model_clv_pairs`); any violation stops the run. **How the snapshot is chosen:** the store holds exactly two captures per game, `t60` (before the cutoff) and `t5` (after it); the rule takes the one that satisfies the inequality, which is `t5`. There is no per-row choice and no best-time selection. Why: a price older than the model's information lets the model know news the price did not (review N1: a report stamped at 11 AM or 5 PM ET can enter the model after a `t60` price was taken, 50 of 1,318 games in 2023 and 62 of 1,315 in 2024), and such a leak only pushes toward PROFITABLE. Cost, stated: the `t5` price (about 9 minutes before tip) already contains later news the T-60 model cannot see (for example lineups confirmed around T-30), so this tests the T-60 forecast against a price that is better informed than the forecast. The price can only hurt the word PROFITABLE. Which bets exist cannot be guaranteed the same way: it depends on lines still posted at T-9 (3.0-4.1% of played t60 units are pulled by t5, and 2,564 / 2,740 units appear only at t5); t60 units pulled by t5 are counted as an exclusion and checked by attack (viii). The `t60` capture is run as a **sensitivity** (descriptive, never decides), with attack (vii) below.
4. Join: to the stored model row on (game_id, player_id, stat) for the same season; no model row (DNP or unmatched) is counted as `void_or_unmatched` and excluded (a book voids a DNP; the model rows are played-only). P = P(Y >= point + 0.5).
5. Eligible iff P >= 0.70. One row per (game, player, stat, point).
6. **Side and amount:** the over price only; flat stake; decimal odds d from the American price; profit = d - 1 on a win, -1 on a loss (stake units). No pushes. No fee beyond the priced overround (as DECISION_SCORING: a sportsbook has no explicit fee, `fee = 0.0` and the source string says so). No promotions, boosts, taxes or state fees are modeled.

**Why BetRivers, fixed, and not "best price".** (a) It is the only book with ladders in both seasons; Unibet US is the same Kambi feed (94.9% identical prices) and counting it would double one source. (b) A fixed book is what a $20 bettor has; best-price shopping across 12 books picks outlier quotes, is a winner's-curse selection, and needs many accounts. (c) The brief asked for "one bet per player-game-stat-line"; with one book that is automatic. **Arm B (secondary, descriptive, never decides):** the same eligibility on the best over price across all books at that exact line (highest decimal, ties to the alphabetically first book). It exists so a reader can see what line-shopping would change.

**Staking and the $20 budget (the plan).**
- Stake **$2 flat per bet, at most 10 bets per week = $20 a week**, never more. The week is the Monday-start week of `game_date` (as stored, Eastern date). Unspent budget does not carry over. Winnings are not restaked.
- Why this size: at the median decimal price 1.36 a $2 win pays $0.72 and a loss costs $2.00; ten bets keep one week's worst case at -$20 and avoid concentrating the budget on one correlated slate. Larger stakes with fewer bets only change variance.
- Selection when more than 10 units are eligible in a week: a **unit** is (game, player, stat). Draw up to 10 units uniformly without replacement, then one rung uniformly within each unit, so nested rungs of one player are never stacked. Seeded `default_rng(2026)`, R = 2,000 independent plan draws per season. If a week has fewer than 10 units, bet all of them and spend less.

**Estimands and CIs.**
- **E1 (decision): rung ROI** = sum(profit) / n over ALL eligible rungs of arm A, each at one unit, pooled over stats and over the two seasons (resampling stratified by season, each season's clusters drawn within season). By stat and by season reported. E1 does not depend on the weekly cap; it is the property of the strategy "every eligible line". **E1u (descriptive, review N3):** the mean over units of each unit's mean rung profit, same CIs; it matches the plan, which draws one rung per unit (BetRivers has 1.28 rungs per unit in 2023 and 1.19 in 2024 at t5).
- CI: game-clustered and date-clustered percentile bootstrap, B = 5,000 each (`default_rng(2026)` and `default_rng(2027)`), ratio estimator on cluster totals. **Decision interval = union**, as in Part 1. Why both: lines within a game share the outcome, and a whole night shares injuries and a scoring environment, which game clustering ignores.
- **E2 (plan dollars, descriptive):** the dollar path of the $2 x 10 plan. Per season over the R plan draws: total staked, final profit (median, 5th and 95th percentile), P(final > 0), median and 95th-percentile max drawdown of the weekly cumulative path, share of weeks with a loss, longest run of losing weeks. These distributions reflect only which bets the plan picks; outcome uncertainty is carried by the E1 interval, and the plan's dollars are reported as ROI interval x total staked. Context fixed now, from model-claimed inputs only: with a per-bet sd about 0.59 (d sqrt(P(1-P)) at P 0.75, d 1.36), 300 bets a season (30 weeks x 10) give a season-profit sd of about $2 x 0.59 x sqrt(300) = $20, against an expected +$24 even if the model's claimed +4% ROI were true. A single season of $20 weeks cannot, by itself, tell skill from luck; that is why E1 pools every eligible rung.

**Decision words (pooled E1, both seasons; verbatim).** Let lo and hi be the decision interval of pooled ROI.
- **PROFITABLE:** pooled ROI >= +0.02 AND lo > 0 AND the point ROI is > 0 in each season separately.
- **NOT PROFITABLE:** hi < +0.02 (the data rule out the practical floor).
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** otherwise.
A PROFITABLE word makes arm A a candidate for the forward log only; money, staking and promotion are Devin's (CLAUDE.md non-negotiable 6). It is not advice. By-stat words are descriptive; a stat-level claim needs BH q < 0.05 over the 4 stat cells (q from max(p_game, p_date)) and the same rule. Arm B, the comparators and E2 never decide. Floor +0.02 (not 0): at $600 staked a season, 2% is $12, below the sd of one season's P&L, so it is the smallest ROI worth the word; the CI lower bound > 0 is Devin's example criterion and is kept.

**Break-even price at each P (reported, from Part 2's set).** Per P bin: n rungs, mean claimed P, realised hit rate with its interval, mean decimal price paid, break-even decimal at the claim (1/P) and at the realised rate (1/h) with American equivalents, ROI at the claim (mean of P d - 1, what the model says) beside realised ROI. This is the table that answers "at what price would 70% over be worth it": it shows the price needed against both the claimed and the realised hit rate.

**Comparators (same n, same CI method; descriptive, not gates).** C1 market lean: BetRivers over rungs whose own de-vigged over probability is >= 0.70, model ignored (does the model add anything over "bet the favourite rungs"); C2 random rung of the same count (recovers about minus the overround, the vig baseline). Market ruler: for each BetRivers player-stat-game, the main rung is the one whose de-vigged over probability is closest to 0.5 (ties to the lower line); ROI is also sliced by distance from it in the units of Part 1.

**Attacks, run only if the word is PROFITABLE and before anyone believes it:** (i) planted-future (shift each model P to a different player-game of the same stat; ROI must fall to the C2 level); (ii) shuffle P within (game, stat) among BetRivers rungs, 50 seeds, ROI must not clear the interval; (iii) leave out the best 5% of bets and the best week; (iv) arm B and the Unibet replicate must have the same sign (the replicate is not independent evidence); (v) a missingness audit on `last_update` and quote staleness; (vi) sensitivity to the 8.4-9.2% overround: ROI minus the C2 baseline; (vii) on the `t60` sensitivity capture, drop games with an official-report stamp in (snapshot_ts, tip - 60] and require the ROI sign to hold (the harness also stores every row's lead in minutes). (viii) units quoted at t60 and absent at t5 (played) are scored at their t60 price and added to arm A; the ROI sign must hold. Units that appear only at t5 are reported separately.

**What Part 2 cannot say.** One snapshot (T-9) per game; whether a real bettor could place that price at that minute, the limits, are unknown; BetRivers is not Devin's venue (he bets only on Kalshi and Polymarket), and whether a friend can get that price at T-9 is unknown; deep alternates and the "N+" ladders are not in the data; the 2025-26 season is untouched. It tests the stored posted lines, not "all alt lines".

## Secondary family (v3): four disjoint P bins, reported, none promoted
**What is added, plainly.** The primary question stays the single set P(over) >= 0.70 and its two parts. Devin also asked for the same two parts in four disjoint probability bins, labelled by midpoint: **55** = [0.50,0.60), **65** = [0.60,0.70), **75** = [0.70,0.80), **85** = [0.80,0.90). Bins 75 and 85 partition the primary headline band [0.70,0.90); bins 55 and 65 are below the primary threshold. **All four bins are reported in full. None is selected, ranked or promoted after the result. A profitable bin is a hypothesis for a new rule on the live log, not a claim, and it can never replace or soften the primary word.**

**Part 1S (calibration, no prices).** For each bin x stat x season (32 cells): the line-rows of every half-point line in the support whose stored model P(over) falls in the bin, hit = Y >= k, gap = hit rate - mean P. Same CI (game-clustered and date-clustered percentile bootstrap, B = 5,000, seeds 2026 / 2027, decision interval = union), same words (CALIBRATED: |gap| <= 0.02 and interval inside +-0.03; OVERCONFIDENT / UNDERCONFIDENT: |gap| > 0.02, interval beyond 0, BH q < 0.05), same p = max(p_game, p_date). **BH family = all 32 cells (bins x stats x seasons)**, separate from the primary family of 8. Bin word per stat: the same word in both seasons, else MIXED. Break-even prices at the claimed and the realised rate are reported per bin. No distance slices for the secondary bins (the primary family carries them).

**Part 2S (profit at posted prices, same staking).** For each bin, BetRivers arm A at the `t5` capture exactly as primary (timing rule, two-sided half-point over rungs, de-duplication, join to the stored model P), with rungs whose model P is in the bin. Each bin is one plan: $2 per bet, at most 10 bets a week, random unit-then-rung draw, R = 2,000 (the plans of different bins are alternatives, never added together, and the $20 is spent on one bin's plan at a time). E1 (all rungs of the bin, pooled stats and seasons), E1u, E2 and the break-even table per bin as in Part 2. Decision words per bin: PROFITABLE / NOT PROFITABLE / INCONCLUSIVE with the primary definitions (floor +0.02, decision interval = union of game- and date-clustered intervals, point ROI > 0 in each season) and for PROFITABLE additionally **BH q < 0.05 over the 4 bin cells** (q from the one-sided p for ROI <= 0, taking the larger of the two clusterings). A bin that fails its minimum-data gate gets no Part 2S word.

**Clustering.** The ladder rungs of one player-game span bins (a points ladder of 7 rungs can have rungs in 55, 65, 75 and 85), and a game holds many player-games. So the bootstrap draws GAMES (and, separately, DATES) once per season with **one resample matrix shared by every bin, stat and arm**; each cluster carries all of its rungs in all bins. Bins are therefore jointly resampled, and a cluster's rungs in different bins stay together.
