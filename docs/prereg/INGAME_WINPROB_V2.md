## Playbook check (MODEL_PLAYBOOK cross-cutting questions)
1. **Information or capacity.** Information: prior, possession, fouls, timeouts, lineup on the floor. Capacity is a logistic with a few time interactions; a GBM on identical inputs (A3) is the capacity control.
2. **Enough data.** 3,690 regular-season games per three seasons, 47 grid states each; cells in the gate below (smallest required cell 8,978 states / 1,054 games). Logistic class: low data appetite. Training for 2023 select is one season (2022); weak, disclosed.
3. **Test validity.** State rows use events with elapsed <= T only (truncation identity and planted-future identity are gate items of the run); same rows, same (no) recalibration for every arm; game-clustered paired bootstrap; a shuffled-information control; a synthetic dryrun before any real label is read; contrasts whose dryrun CI half-width exceeds half their floor are INCONCLUSIVE, not null.
4. **Lineage.** v1 (ride watcher: logistic on m/sqrt(t+1), m, m*log1p(t), fit on 2022-24 incl. the report seasons, so it is in-sample on 2023/2024: the arms below refit v1's form walk-forward; shipped v1 is reported descriptively and can only look better than V1r). T508 (in-game line model): its "win" was 94% baseline weakness (the attack row); hence the information-matched arms here and the rule that the binding comparator is the better of V1r and A1. T508's stint look-ahead disclosure (period openers) is inherited.
5. **Scored against.** V1r and A1 on our own holdout seasons; ESPN's live win probability and the Kalshi in-game mid only in the live shadow stage (no in-game Kalshi history exists for 2022-24).
Class checks (logistic/GBM): features as-of; none constant within a date (no era flag); GBM settings fixed (8 leaves, 200 trees, lr 0.05, min 200 rows/leaf, seed 0, n_jobs 1), monotone increasing in margin.

## The rule in twelve lines (as it would be frozen)
1. **Unit.** (game, state), states every 60 s of elapsed regulation (60..2820; 47 per game) plus every 60 s inside overtime; regular season only. State = last event with elapsed <= T, ordered by action number. Outcome = home team wins (final, including OT). Playoffs excluded (v2 does not run in the playoffs without its own rule).
2. **Arms** (every arm logistic unless stated; no recalibration for any arm; same rows). **V1r**: v1's feature form refit on the training seasons. **A1 = V1r + prior**: adds logit(prior) x {1, sqrt(left/48), left/48} (left = minutes). **A2 = v2 full**: A1 + possession (3-class) x {1, sqrt(left/48)}, own and opponent team fouls in period (capped at 6) x period-clock position, timeout difference and own remaining x 1{left <= 5}, on-floor strength difference L (sum of as-of ratings of the five), star difference S, foul-trouble count difference F, pace (shrunk) x left, OT flag; each also interacting with m/sqrt(t+1) where the structure demands it, listed in code before the run, not tuned. **A3 (capacity control)**: GBM on exactly A2's inputs. **C0 (information-matched control)**: A2 refit with every feature beyond V1r and the prior permuted across games within (season, time bin, margin bin of width 5): same marginals and structure, game-specific information destroyed. **Shipped v1** (descriptive; in-sample on 2023/24).
3. **Player rating (fixed now).** Hollinger game score per minute over the previous 40 games before the game date (any season in the window), shrunk with 10 pseudo-games at the league rate; fewer than 5 prior games -> league rate (the missingness share is reported by season).
4. **Split.** Train 2022 -> **select 2023**; train 2022-23 -> **report 2024**. 2025 is never loaded (game ids `00225`, playoffs `004`/`005` will be refused in the run code by a game-id guard like T508's `check_game_id`).
5. **Metric.** Per game, mean over its states of log loss; contrasts are paired differences of these game means (negative = the first arm is better), game-clustered bootstrap (2,000 draws, seed 0). Brier is descriptive. Slices (descriptive): Q1..Q4, last 5 min, last 2 min, OT, region R75 = {V1r leader probability >= 0.75}, R98 = {>= 0.98} (regions are defined by the reference arm, so the arm under test cannot pick its own region).
6. **Primary family** (report season 2024, BH at 0.05 over the three bootstrap p-values): **P1** A2 - V1r pooled <= -0.005. **P2** A2 - A1 pooled <= -0.002 (information beyond the prior). **P3** A2 - A1 in R75 <= -0.002. Each also needs the 2023 point to meet the same floor (else 2024 is not scored for that contrast) and the 2024 95% CI upper bound < 0.
7. **Guards (all must hold for any PASS).** (a) C0 - A1 pooled >= -0.001 (shuffled information adds nothing). (b) Planted-future identity and truncation identity: state rows built from events with elapsed <= T are identical when later events are deleted (first 30 games of each season). (c) Dryrun passed. (d) No feature group carries more than 60% of A2's gain over A1 (knockout, reported; a single group over the line is a red flag, CLAUDE.md). (e) A3 - A2 reported: if A3 beats A2 by more than 0.002 the structure, not the information, is the limit: noted, one follow-up at most, no promotion. (f) Lag robustness: A2's gain over A1 on states read at 0 / 60 / 120 / 180 s stale; if the gain at 120 s is below half the gain at 0 s, the feed latency kills the use and the verdict says so.
8. **Calibration and thresholds (2024; descriptive with a no-harm guard).** For A2: reliability in leader bins [0.95, 0.98), [0.98, 0.99), [0.99, 1.0] (observed leader-win rate vs mean prediction, game-clustered Wilson/bootstrap CI), and the comeback rate (leader loses) at the **first grid state where A2's leader probability >= 98%** and at **>= 75%**, one row per game, with n. Guard: the 98% comeback upper CI <= 3% (the 10-22 pre-registered reversal threshold) and the observed comeback rate not above A2's mean predicted comeback by more than 1 pp (75%: 5 pp). Expected n of comebacks at 98% is about 12 per season (1.2% of about 990 games): this slice cannot discriminate within 1 pp in one season and is pooled 2022-24 leave-one-season-out as a second, descriptive table. Compared side by side with V1r at the same thresholds, with the number of games where the two arms cross on different grid states.
9. **Dryrun (before any real label is read).** The harness runs on real feature rows with synthetic outcomes: (i) null, y ~ Bernoulli(V1r's probability), 20 seeds: each contrast's CI contains 0 in >= 18/20 seeds and the mean point within +-0.0005; (ii) positive control: y from a data-generating logit with planted prior weight, a planted possession worth 1.0 point, and a planted timeout effect: A2 recovers each within 25% and A2 - A1 is <= -0.002 with CI upper < 0 in every seed; (iii) shuffled-labels refit (labels permuted across games within season): every contrast CI contains 0; (iv) planted-future rows (events after minute 24 doubled): checkpoints <= 24 identical, later rows differ. A contrast whose dryrun CI half-width is > half its floor is **INCONCLUSIVE** (an invalid test is not a null). Verdict words: PASS, FAIL, INCONCLUSIVE.
10. **Live shadow stage (before any alert uses v2).** Starts only after a frozen-run PASS. From the 2026 capture (`data/raw/live_<date>/espn_summary`, `kalshi/KXNBAGAME`), every in-progress snapshot is scored with V1r, A1, A2 (prior = rung0), A2k (prior = Kalshi pre-tip mid, shadow only), against the ESPN live win probability (`winprobability`, newest play) and the Kalshi mid at the snapshot, using only information in the snapshot and earlier ones; snapshots staler than 180 s are excluded and counted. Scored at settlement on n >= 100 settled games (clustered by game): descriptive differences A2 - V1r, A2 - ESPN, A2 - Kalshi mid, reliability at 0.95+, comeback counts. Conditions for letting v2 touch an alert: frozen PASS, shadow n >= 100 games, A2 - V1r point <= 0 with CI upper <= +0.003, 0.95+ bin within 2 pp, snapshot cadence <= 60 s for the games used, and Devin's go. No condition involves beating ESPN or the Kalshi mid; we expect not to (markets beat us on game winners) and v2 is a cross-check for the ride, never a source of edge.
11. **What changes in production: nothing.** A PASS makes v2 a candidate for the shadow stage. No stake, no sizing.
12. **Cost.** Gate: about 2.5 min (done). Dryrun: under 5 min on the Mac. Frozen run: logistic arms in seconds, A3 and bootstrap about 5-8 min. All maintainer-run with the exact command in the freeze commit.

## What each choice protects against
V1r instead of shipped v1: v1 saw 2023/24 outcomes. A1 as binding comparator: the prior alone will beat v1 early in games and would otherwise be credited to possession and fouls. C0: a refit with more columns can win by capacity or by an era signal. Regions defined by V1r: selection on the arm under test. Truncation and planted-future identities: lineup stints are a look-ahead reconstruction and a missingness pattern once leaked through three experiments. Dryrun and INCONCLUSIVE: an invalid test is not a null. Counts, not `bonusState`/`timeoutsUsed`/`possession`: the ESPN flags that were checked either never fire or disagree with the plays. Knockout share: a single feature group carrying the gain is a red flag. Staleness arm: the live feed is 1-3 minutes behind and the game moves in that time.

## Kill criteria and forking paths
Gate FAIL: not freezable. 2023 floor not met for a contrast: 2024 not scored for it. Any guard fails: no PASS. Declared and fixed: grid, rating definition, features and their interactions, floors, GBM settings, seeds, bootstrap draws. Family: P1-P3. A null closes "does knowing prior, possession, fouls, timeouts and lineup improve in-game win probability beyond prior + clock" for these features; one pre-registered follow-up at most.

## Feed parser plan (live), as built
`nba/ride/feed_state.py` (pure functions; tests `tests/ride/test_feed_state.py`, fixture trimmed from the 10-21 capture). ESPN `summary` fields:
* possession: from `plays[]` in order: `type.text` + `team.id` (acting team; for a foul the committer; for Jumpball the team gaining possession) via `update_possession`. Made FG, made last FT, turnover, foul -> other team; defensive/offensive rebound -> rebounder; missed FG or missed last FT -> loose ball. Assignments, so an ESPN foul plus its separate turnover cannot toggle.
* team fouls: `header.competitions[0].competitors[].fouls.teamFoulsCurrent` (period). Player fouls: `boxscore.players[].statistics[0].athletes[].stats` at the `PF` index of `names`.
* timeouts: `competitors[].timeoutsRemaining` (cross-check: 7 minus `Full Timeout` plays by `team.id`).
* substitutions: `plays[]` with `type.text == "Substitution"`, `team.id`, `participants[0]` entering, `participants[1]` leaving; on-floor set is read from `athletes[].active` (5 per team), starters from `starter`.
* clock and period: `status.period`, `status.displayClock`; score: `competitors[].score`; ESPN benchmark: `winprobability[].homeWinPercentage` at the newest play id.
Parsed example (game 401909092, LAL home vs GS, snapshot 2026-10-22T03:52Z): `period 3, clock 6:41 (401 s), secs_left 1121, score 83-59, possession away` (LAL's last made free throw ended the possession, so GS has the ball), `timeouts_remaining 5 / 3`, `team_fouls_period 2 / 2`, `on_court` five ids per team, `player_fouls['4433137'] = 4`, `espn_home_wp 0.995`, `fouls_to_give 2`. Unrecognised bodies return None (never guessed).
Known parser limits: defensive goaltending and rare violations leave possession unchanged (internal consistency 98.2%, 54 disagreements of 2,975, mostly an offensive foul and its turnover listed separately); `Substitution` rows lag the box `active` flag by a snapshot (18 of 278 rows differ).
