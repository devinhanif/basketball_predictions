2. **Primary** (one test, no family): pts, conditional on playing (minutes > 0), integer CRPS, **FULL minus the lower-tail arm** (`props_context_residual_lt` pts grid, the latest row `made_at <= tipoff - 60 min`, = production plus the short-minutes mixture = the A0 of T467-T473), paired per row, one-sided (H1: FULL lower). **PASS at the final look iff all hold:** (a) delta <= **-0.005** (game-clustered); (b) the fit-noise-inflated one-sided 95% bound `delta + 1.645 * sqrt(se^2 + 0.00199^2) < 0` under BOTH the game-clustered and the date-clustered bootstrap; (c) FULL beats the past-only stale-adjustment placebo by <= -0.0025 with one-sided upper bound < 0; (d) the day-shuffled FULL is worse than the comparator (one-sided lower bound > 0); (e) no slice (bench, low p_play, second night; n >= 300) worse than the comparator by more than +0.005; (f) the integrity gates are clean. 3. **Secondary family (declared; BH over exactly these five, game-clustered one-sided p, m = 5):** conditional integer CRPS FULL minus production's own grid for reb, ast and fg3m (the whole package, mixture included, because no live mixture arm exists for these stats); and the two joint contrasts, pts: `crps_joint_full - crps_joint_rprod` (the grid swap with R held fixed; R cancels) and `crps_joint_full - crps_joint_prod` (the full proposal vs production). ## One departure from the brief, and why (read first)
The brief named "FULL minus production" as the primary. Production's primary grid has **no lower-tail mixture**; FULL is built on it for all four stats, and the pts mixture alone is worth about -0.011 integer CRPS (T136-T159, PROJECT_STATUS; its own live rule is FORWARD_PREREG_2026_27, shadow arm `props_context_residual_lt`, logged by `--log-lower-tail-variant` in the pretip job). T467-T473 measured FULL against **A0 = production + mixture** (-0.0083 / -0.0083 on 2023/24, -0.0116 on 2025), so a live contrast against bare production would clear the -0.005 floor on the mixture's gain with no recent-role information at all, and the verdict would not be about what FULL adds. The primary therefore compares FULL with the lower-tail arm (same stat, same cutoff); FULL minus production is computed on every look as `cond_vs_prod` (package) and for reb/ast/fg3m it IS the secondary contrast (no live mixture arm exists there, so those results cannot be attributed to the five columns; stated in the label). **Safe default taken: comparator = lower-tail arm. If Devin wants the package as the primary, say so before the freeze; the floor would then need re-basing (a package floor of about -0.016 would hold the same -0.005 for the columns), which I did not choose for him.** A pts row without a lower-tail arm row (production logged no `props_context_residual_lt` row) leaves the primary population and is counted (`played_rows_without_comparator`); it stays in `cond_vs_prod`.

## Why these choices (what each protects against)
* **Live log only, no history verdict.** 2025 is spent for FULL (T473) and for R; 2023/24 chose the five columns (forking paths in RECENT_ROLE_FULL). Forward rows are the only clean ones. FULL's own 2025 pts result (-0.0116 [-0.0159, -0.0076], n 28,173, with a shuffled-label guard that fired and was later shown to fire on ~19% of honest shuffles) is known to the rule's author and to Devin; the live log is the test that has not been seen.
* **Conditional integer CRPS on played rows for the primary.** FULL changes the conditional grid (heads and the short-minutes weight); P(play) does not enter, so the primary cannot move with the P(play) shadow. Integer grid for every arm (T114-T118, a continuous arm lost 92-126% of its gain to the support artifact).
* **Floor -0.005, one-sided, two clusterings.** A clear gain nobody can use; the hypothesis is directional. Games share a result and a pace (design effect 1.0-1.16 measured), dates share a slate; the bound must hold under both.
* **Fit-noise inflation in (b).** The bootstrap covers test-row sampling only; two gradient-boosted refits of the same data differ by more (permutation sd 0.0023, below). Adding the null sd in quadrature is the cheapest honest fix for "the guard's uncertainty ignored fit noise" (BEST_PRACTICES, "Shuffle guards must be sized from a permutation null").
* **Placebo (c).** Past-only stale adjustment: today's comparator grid plus the same player's PREVIOUS (FULL - comparator) quantile-wise integer adjustment. If FULL's gain were a persistent per-player offset and not today's information, this stale copy would match it. Disclosed weakness: the stale copy is itself much worse than the comparator (rehearsal below, +0.0148), so (c) is a weak discriminator by construction; the strong placebo evidence is offline (PL_FULL, T473: -0.0112) and a live noise-column refit is not built. It guards, it does not prove.
* **Day-shuffle (d).** FULL grids permuted across players of one day (same stat): a scorer that does not notice a mismatch is blind (non-inert check; unit-tested, and 3.47 worse in the rehearsal).
* **Slices (e):** bench = production `proj_minutes < 20` (latest `forward_pplay_shadow` row at the same cutoff; the FULL tables store no minutes), low availability = production `p_play < 0.9`, second night of a back-to-back (`games` table). A gain bought on starters while hurting near-DNP rows is what SEQ blends did (T442). Playoffs are excluded from the population (different minutes regime); slices do not enter BH.
* **Kill-only interim:** harm should stop early; success must not be declared on a half look (optional stopping).
* **Secondary family BH m = 5, labelled package:** reb/ast/fg3m effects are below the floor offline (-0.0025/-0.0036, -0.0020, -0.0011/-0.00004); they are asked as a direction question and cannot rescue or fail the primary.

## Joint scoring construction (frozen here)
The joint (unconditional) forecast is `(1 - p) delta_0 + (p/19) sum_k delta_{q_k}` (the 19 stored integer quantiles as equal-mass atoms, DNP rows scored as 0) and its score is the exact CRPS of that mixture (`nba/daily/full_shadow/settle.py: mixture_crps`). **This construction was chosen by the steward (commit ce59b41, Mon Oct 12 10:12 CDT 2026) before any live row existed (the tables were verified empty at 12:13 CDT) and is frozen here; it is not revisited after a result.** `crps_joint_prod` = production p_play x production grid; `crps_joint_full` = P(play) R x FULL grid; `crps_joint_rprod` = R x production grid. The 19-atom form is coarser than the 199-point grids of the offline tests, and joint levels are not comparable to conditional levels, only within a pair of joint columns. Rows with NULL `p_play_r` have NULL R columns and drop out of the joint contrasts. Identity (checked to 1e-9 on every scoring run): `(full - prod) = (full - rprod) + (rprod - prod)` per row.

## No double counting with PPLAY_INTO_PROPS_LIVE (frozen 3a305482) and FORWARD_PREREG
* PPLAY_INTO_PROPS_LIVE tests R in the unconditional forecast **on production's grid** (`rprod - prod`). This rule's conditional contrasts do not contain p at all (disjoint). `full - rprod` holds R fixed (R cancels), so it measures the grid only. `full - prod` is the sum of the two; its `rprod - prod` piece is that rule's and is printed here only as a check (populations differ: that rule scores every `p_play_r` row, this one the FULL shadow's context rows, so the numbers are not expected to match). **A package claim ("serve R and FULL together") requires `full - prod` to pass here AND the PPLAY rule's R verdict not to be FAIL or killed**; a FULL result never rescues R.
* FORWARD_PREREG_2026_27 owns the lower-tail arm vs production (pts). The primary here subtracts that arm out, so the two rules test different increments (mixture over production; FULL over mixture).

## Guards and integrity gates (any failure => no verdict for the affected stat, reported)
* **Stamp:** the row used is the latest with `made_at <= tipoff - 60 min`; the writer refuses `made_at >= tipoff`; the reader counts shadow rows after tip (must be 0) and (game, player, stat) triples whose every row is later than the cutoff (excluded). More than 2% of triples excluded => no verdict.
* **Shadow integrity:** for every `in_prod_log` shadow row the stored production `p_play_prod` (1e-12) and 19-quantile grid (1e-9) must equal the latest `props_context_residual` row of `forward_predictions` made at or before the shadow row (production is write-on-change, so same-run pairing is not required); mismatches must be 0. If nothing can be compared the gate is reported unverifiable and the steward's byte-identical production check stands in.
* **Scorer reproduces settle:** conditional CRPS recomputed from the stored grids equals `crps_prod` / `crps_full` to 1e-9.
* **Null-sized fit-noise term (b above), numbers.** Null = SHUF_FULL - SHUF_CTRL, pts integer CRPS, the frozen RECENT_ROLE_FULL shuffle construction re-run under permutation seeds on the select seasons 2023 and 2024 (`reports/shuffle_guard_diagnostic_2026-10-12.json`). First measurement K = 48 (24 seeds x 2 seasons): mean -0.00026, sd 0.00229. **Extended before the freeze to K = 100 (50 seeds x 2 seasons; Devin 2026-10-12, "a yes"; run 12:50-13:47 CDT, cache-resumed): pooled mean -0.00019 (se 0.00020), sd 0.0019879 (2023 0.00238, 2024 0.00151; range -0.0061 to +0.0068). Frozen term: sigma_N = 0.00199 (`pooled_both.sd`, rounded to 5 dp; also `NULL_SD_PTS`).** The noise-column control (five columns row-permuted, shuffled labels, 8 seeds per season) gave sd 0.0021 / 0.0018, the same dispersion. A real-label noise-column null was not measured (the cache holds shuffled-label runs only; real-label fit noise should be smaller, so the term is conservative). The fixed -0.001 threshold of RECENT_ROLE_FULL is NOT used.
* **What this does to the pass line at n_min:** bootstrap se is about 0.00304 when the one-sided half-width is 0.005, total sqrt(0.00304^2 + 0.00199^2) = 0.00363, so (b) needs delta <= **-0.00598** (the floor alone would allow -0.005). Normal approximation, P(PASS on (a)+(b)): true gain -0.0083 (2023/24 level) about 0.78; -0.0116 (2025 level) about 0.97; -0.005 (exactly the floor) about 0.37; zero about 0.03. This is the rule's power, stated now: a real gain of the 2023/24 size fails about 2 times in 10.
