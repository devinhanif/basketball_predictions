1. **Rows.** The A0 row set of ODDS_HISTORY: `reports/odds_history/model_rows_{2023,2024}_{pts,reb,ast,fg3m}.parquet` (production out-of-fold forecasts, played rows, career `n_prior >= 5`), 27,619 / 27,583 rows per stat, identical row sets across the four stats (measured). Seasons: select 2023, report 2024. **Season 2025 is never loaded by this harness**: every SQL carries `season <= 2024`, the loader raises on any season >= 2025, and a planted-2025-row test proves it is not returned. Season 2022 is read once, for `kappa.json` (line 4), and for nothing else.
2. **Affected slice.** `games_with_team` g = the player's prior played (minutes > 0) regular-season games for the row's team in the same season. **Mover** = has a regular-season game in S-1, his last regular-season team in S-1 differs from his first regular-season team in S, and the row's team is that first team. **Affected rows (the verdict slice): movers with g < K, K = 10.** K = 5 and K = 20 are pre-declared sensitivity, reported, never selected between. **Rookies** (no game of any kind in held seasons 2013..S-1) are a separate declared slice, descriptive only (gate: fails at K = 10 in 2023, below). Returners after a gap, and in-season arrivals (row team differs from the season's first team), are reported counts only: no preseason evidence for that team exists.
3. **Preseason evidence.** From `preseason_player_game_stats` joined to `preseason_games` (season S, `game_id` prefix `001`, minutes > 0, the row's team): n_pre games, m_pre total minutes, per-game minutes mpg = m_pre / n_pre, per-minute rates rho_s = stat_total / m_pre for pts, reb, ast, fg3m. A row is **covered** when n_pre >= 2; an uncovered row is served exactly as A0 (weight 0). The primary slice is ALL affected rows (covered or not), because that is what production would face; the covered subset is a reported slice.
4. **Fixed blend (no fit on 2023/2024).** Weights are functions of preseason minutes, declared here: `w_m = m_pre / (m_pre + 60)` for minutes (Devin's example constant) and `w_r = m_pre / (m_pre + 120)` for rates, both times the fade `(1 - g/K)` so the blend is continuous at K and vanishes by game K. Preseason minutes are not regular-season minutes (starters sit): `mpg_adj = kappa_c * mpg`, with two constants `kappa_starter`, `kappa_bench` (c = the player's preseason starter flag, any preseason start) frozen from a ratio on **season 2022 only** (for players whose first 2022 regular-season team is the preseason team, with n_pre >= 2 and >= 10 regular games: the sum of regular-season minutes in the first <= 20 games over the sum of preseason minutes per game x the same number of games, pooled by starter flag), computed once by `calibrate` (n = 244 starters, 165 bench), **kappa_starter = 1.2435134417606934, kappa_bench = 0.9402396015339128**, written to `reports/prereg_preseason_role_prior/calibrate.json` (`KAPPA_JSON_SHA256`), hashed into the rule before any 2023 score exists. Minutes target: `min_blend = (1 - w_m') * min10 + w_m' * mpg_adj`, `w_m' = w_m (1 - g/K)`, where `min10` is production's half-life-10-game exponentially weighted mean of the player's minutes (see 'What min10 is' below). Rates: `rho_pre_shrunk = (m_pre * rho_pre + 100 * rho_prev) / (m_pre + 100)` with `rho_prev` the player's S-1 regular-season per-minute rate (set to A0's own rate when he had < 300 S-1 minutes), then `rho_blend = (1 - w_r') * rho_A0 + w_r' * rho_pre_shrunk` with `rho_A0 = m_recency / max(min10, 1)` (A0's own rate: the production feature `rate_s`, see 'What min10 is'), `w_r' = w_r (1 - g/K)`.
5. **Arms.** All arms are a rescaling of A0's stored quantile grid by a multiplier lambda per row, re-served on the integer grid `ceil(q - 0.5)` exactly as production (the grid is rebuilt from the stored `c`, `s` and the recovered `zq`, as in LINE_CALIBRATION); lambda = 1 reproduces A0 to the bit (a `check` identity test).
   * **A0** production as stored (`mean_prod`, `p_ge`); reproduced as in LINE_CALIBRATION check 3 and WOWY_STRUCT's A0 equality: the harness recomputes integer CRPS per cell and equals the stored `model_rows_{S}_{stat}.a0.json` `crps_int` to 1e-9 (8 of 8 cells), else a label-free STOP.
   * **A1 minutes-only:** lambda = `min_blend / min10` (clipped to [0.5, 2]); rates unchanged. Rows with `min10 < 1` (none in 2023/24) are served as A0.
   * **A2 minutes + rates (the claim):** lambda = `(min_blend / min10) * (rho_blend / rho_A0)` (clipped to [0.5, 2]).
   * **A3 placebo (the player's own S-1 last 10 regular-season games in place of preseason):** same weights per row as A2 (taken from the real preseason `m_pre` so the information content is matched), values `mpg`, `rho` from his last 10 regular-season games of S-1 (rows with fewer than 5 such games or zero minutes are served as A0). **Stated limit:** A0's `min10` and recent rate (half-life-10 weighted means over all his past games) already hold those games at the start of the season, so A3 is close to the identity by construction; it guards "any blending helps" weakly, and the next arm is the real guard.
   * **A4 permuted-identity placebo (added; the real capacity guard):** A2 with the covered affected players' preseason vectors permuted within season and within starter flag (seed 0, one draw; no other draw is made or reported). If a generic shrink toward a typical preseason line gains as much as the right player's own, the gain is not role information.
6. **Metric and verdict.** Integer-support CRPS (199-point grid, `ceil(q - 0.5)`) for every arm; paired difference A2 - A0 per stat on the affected slice; 95% percentile bootstrap clustered by `game_id` (2000 draws, seed 0, `nba/props/metrics.py::_clustered_boot_means` as pinned at freeze); one-sided p = (1 + #{draws >= 0}) / 2001, BH at q = 0.05 over the 4 stats within a season; date clustering reported, never decides. **A stat clears a season** when its point <= -floor, CI upper < 0 and BH p <= 0.05. **Floors (declared, CRPS units): pts 0.005, reb 0.003, ast 0.002, fg3m 0.001** (each about 0.1-0.2% of that stat's A0 CRPS on the slice; practical, not statistical). The same contrast on ALL rows of the A0 row set is reported (n = 27,619 / 27,583).
7. **Words.** (`evaluate` returns exactly one overall word.) Per stat per season the cell clears (rule line 6). `both` = stats that clear in 2023 and 2024. A stat in `both` is **good** when, in both seasons, (P) A4's gain is < 50% of A2's gain, (G1) A2 - A4 <= -0.5 x floor, (G3) coverage of A2 at tau 0.10 and 0.90 is within 0.02 of A0 on the affected slice (the rescale must not break the interval), and globally (G2) no stat of the four has an A2 - A0 CI lower bound above 0 in either season (nothing is significantly harmed). **PASS**: pts is good. **PASS_OTHER_STAT**: pts is not good, another stat is good (a candidate for that stat only; it was not the headline). **PLACEBO_TOO**: no stat is good, `both` is non-empty and some stat in `both` fails (P) in either season (the generic-shrink reading; it takes precedence over FAIL_GUARD). **FAIL_GUARD**: no stat is good, `both` is non-empty, no (P) failure (G1, G2 or G3 fails). **FAIL_ONE_SEASON**: `both` is empty and some stat clears in exactly one season. **FAIL_NO_GAIN**: no stat clears in any season. (G4) planted-future and NULL-rule tests are a STOP before any score, never a word: every preseason game used is dated before the season's first scored row, the context built with later regular-season rows removed gives identical lambdas, and lambda is a function that takes no outcome argument.

PASS or PASS_OTHER_STAT leads to a separate promotion decision by Devin, a live shadow in the 2026-27 forward log (movers of 2026 are identifiable from the first game; 2026 preseason is already loaded) and, only on Devin's words, one logged 2025 touch. ## Hypothesis
**H1.** For a mover in his first K = 10 games with the new team, preseason minutes and per-minute production are evidence about the new role that production's recency-average-plus-residual cannot see, because its inputs at the start of a season are last season's games for another team. Blending them with the fixed weights above lowers integer CRPS of pts by at least 0.005 on the affected slice (and by the declared floors for reb, ast, fg3m) in 2023 and 2024.
**Mechanism and an alternative reading.** (a) Minutes: the new team's coach gives a new role (more or fewer minutes). (b) Rates: a new usage and a new teammate set change shots and passes per minute; the 2026 opening-night numbers say this is where the error sits. The alternative reading, which the arms are built to separate: exhibition minutes are garbage-time heavy and noisy, so any benefit may be a shrink toward a typical line (A4) or only minutes (A1 vs A2).

## What min10 is (production code path read 2026-10-21; resolves the freeze blocker)
**`min10` is NOT the minutes input behind `mean_prod`.** In `nba/props/context_residual.py`: `player_states` computes, over the player's PLAYED games (all teams, all seasons held), exponentially weighted means with half-lives 5, 10, 40 and 100 games; `min10` is the half-life-10 weighted mean of his minutes (not "the last 10 games"), and `m` (stored as `m_recency`) is the half-life-10 weighted mean of the stat (`m10_s`). The served centre is `c = m + mu` where `mu` is a LightGBM residual (features include `min10`, `min5`, `min40`, `starter10`, team and report features) and the scale is `s` (a second LightGBM head), grid `q_j = max.acc(clip(c + s zq_j, 0))`. So minutes enter production in two places: through `m10_s` (which is itself rate x minutes by construction: `rate_s = m10_s / max(min10, 1)` is a production feature) and through the tree's `mu`, which has no explicit minutes projection to replace. **Consequence for A1 and A2.** There is no minutes projection behind `mean_prod` to substitute. The rule therefore states what it does: it treats `min10` as the minutes level of the base `m` and rescales the WHOLE served grid (centre and scale, hence the residual and the spread too) by `lambda = min_blend / min10` (A1) and by that times `rho_blend / rho_A0` (A2), where `rho_A0 = m_recency / max(min10, 1)` is production's own `rate_s`. This is an approximation (the residual `mu` is assumed to scale with minutes) and is a pre-declared one; the alternative of scaling only `m` and leaving `mu` and `s` untouched is NOT run (variant-chasing). lambda = 1 reproduces A0 exactly (tested). Stored `mean_prod` is not used by any arm.
