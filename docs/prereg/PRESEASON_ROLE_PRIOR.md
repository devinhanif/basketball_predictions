## The rule in eight lines (as it would be frozen)
1. **Rows.** The A0 row set of ODDS_HISTORY: `reports/odds_history/model_rows_{2023,2024}_{pts,reb,ast,fg3m}.parquet` (production out-of-fold forecasts, played rows, career `n_prior >= 5`), 27,619 / 27,583 rows per stat, identical row sets across the four stats (measured). Seasons: select 2023, report 2024. **Season 2025 is never loaded by this harness**: every SQL carries `season <= 2024`, the loader raises on any season >= 2025, and a planted-2025-row test proves it is not returned. Season 2022 is read once, for `kappa.json` (line 4), and for nothing else.
2. **Affected slice.** `games_with_team` g = the player's prior played (minutes > 0) regular-season games for the row's team in the same season. **Mover** = has a regular-season game in S-1, his last regular-season team in S-1 differs from his first regular-season team in S, and the row's team is that first team. **Affected rows (the verdict slice): movers with g < K, K = 10.** K = 5 and K = 20 are pre-declared sensitivity, reported, never selected between. **Rookies** (no game of any kind in held seasons 2013..S-1) are a separate declared slice, descriptive only (gate: fails at K = 10 in 2023, below). Returners after a gap, and in-season arrivals (row team differs from the season's first team), are reported counts only: no preseason evidence for that team exists.
3. **Preseason evidence.** From `preseason_player_game_stats` joined to `preseason_games` (season S, `game_id` prefix `001`, minutes > 0, the row's team): n_pre games, m_pre total minutes, per-game minutes mpg = m_pre / n_pre, per-minute rates rho_s = stat_total / m_pre for pts, reb, ast, fg3m. A row is **covered** when n_pre >= 2; an uncovered row is served exactly as A0 (weight 0). The primary slice is ALL affected rows (covered or not), because that is what production would face; the covered subset is a reported slice.
4. **Fixed blend (no fit on 2023/2024).** Weights are functions of preseason minutes, declared here: `w_m = m_pre / (m_pre + 60)` for minutes (Devin's example constant) and `w_r = m_pre / (m_pre + 120)` for rates, both times the fade `(1 - g/K)` so the blend is continuous at K and vanishes by game K. Preseason minutes are not regular-season minutes (starters sit): `mpg_adj = kappa_c * mpg`, with two constants `kappa_starter`, `kappa_bench` (c = the player's preseason starter flag, any preseason start) frozen from a ratio on **season 2022 only** (total regular-season minutes in a player's first 20 games over his total preseason minutes scaled to 20 games, over players with n_pre >= 2 and >= 10 regular games), computed once by `calibrate`, written to `kappa.json`, hashed into the rule before any 2023 score exists. Minutes target: `min_blend = (1 - w_m') * min10 + w_m' * mpg_adj`, `w_m' = w_m (1 - g/K)`, where `min10` is the A0 row's recent-minutes column. Rates: `rho_pre_shrunk = (m_pre * rho_pre + 100 * rho_prev) / (m_pre + 100)` with `rho_prev` the player's S-1 regular-season per-minute rate (set to A0's own rate when he had < 300 S-1 minutes), then `rho_blend = (1 - w_r') * rho_A0 + w_r' * rho_pre_shrunk` with `rho_A0 = mean_prod / min10`, `w_r' = w_r (1 - g/K)`.
5. **Arms.** All arms are a rescaling of A0's stored quantile grid by a multiplier lambda per row, re-served on the integer grid `ceil(q - 0.5)` exactly as production (the grid is rebuilt from the stored `c`, `s` and the recovered `zq`, as in LINE_CALIBRATION); lambda = 1 reproduces A0 to the bit (a `check` identity test).
   * **A0** production as stored (`mean_prod`, `p_ge`); reproduced as in LINE_CALIBRATION check 3 and WOWY_STRUCT's A0 equality: the harness recomputes integer CRPS per cell and equals the stored `model_rows_{S}_{stat}.a0.json` `crps_int` to 1e-9 (8 of 8 cells), else a label-free STOP.
   * **A1 minutes-only:** lambda = `min_blend / min10` (clipped to [0.5, 2]); rates unchanged.
   * **A2 minutes + rates (the claim):** lambda = `(min_blend / min10) * (rho_blend / rho_A0)` (clipped to [0.5, 2]).
   * **A3 placebo (the player's own S-1 last 10 regular-season games in place of preseason):** same weights per row as A2 (taken from the real preseason `m_pre` so the information content is matched), values `mpg`, `rho` from his last 10 games of S-1. **Stated limit:** A0's `min10` and recent rate already hold those games at the start of the season, so A3 is close to the identity by construction; it guards "any blending helps" weakly, and the next arm is the real guard.
   * **A4 permuted-identity placebo (added; the real capacity guard):** A2 with the covered affected players' preseason vectors permuted within season and within starter flag (seed 0, one draw for the verdict, 20 draws reported for the null). If a generic shrink toward a typical preseason line gains as much as the right player's own, the gain is not role information.
6. **Metric and verdict.** Integer-support CRPS (199-point grid, `ceil(q - 0.5)`) for every arm; paired difference A2 - A0 per stat on the affected slice; 95% percentile bootstrap clustered by `game_id` (2000 draws, seed 0, `nba/props/metrics.py::_clustered_boot_means` as pinned at freeze); one-sided p = (1 + #{draws >= 0}) / 2001, BH at q = 0.05 over the 4 stats within a season; date clustering reported, never decides. **A stat clears a season** when its point <= -floor, CI upper < 0 and BH p <= 0.05. **Floors (declared, CRPS units): pts 0.005, reb 0.003, ast 0.002, fg3m 0.001** (each about 0.1-0.2% of that stat's A0 CRPS on the slice; practical, not statistical). The same contrast on ALL rows of the A0 row set is reported (n = 27,619 / 27,583).
7. **Words.** (`evaluate` returns exactly one overall word.) **PASS**: pts clears in both 2023 and 2024 and the guards hold in both seasons. **PASS_OTHER_STAT**: pts does not clear in both, but reb, ast or fg3m clears in both and guards hold (a candidate for that stat only; it was not the headline). **PLACEBO_TOO**: a stat clears both seasons but A4's gain is >= 50% of A2's on it in either season (the generic-shrink reading). **FAIL_GUARD**: a stat clears both seasons, the guards G1-G4 fail in one. **FAIL_ONE_SEASON**: some stat clears in exactly one season. **FAIL_NO_GAIN**: otherwise. Guards: (G1) A2 - A4 <= -0.5 x floor on every cleared stat; (G2) no harm: A2 - A0 on all rows of the affected slice has CI lower <= 0 for every stat (no stat is significantly worse); (G3) coverage at tau 0.10 / 0.90 within 0.02 of A0 on the affected slice (the rescale must not break the interval); (G4) planted-future and NULL-rule tests (a STOP before any score, never a word): every preseason game used is dated before the season's first regular-season game, and re-labelling all 2024 outcomes leaves every weight and lambda unchanged.
8. **What changes in production: nothing.** Every word above is a candidate statement about a rescaling of stored forecasts. PASS or PASS_OTHER_STAT leads to a separate promotion decision by Devin, a live shadow in the 2026-27 forward log (movers of 2026 are identifiable from the first game; 2026 preseason is already loaded) and, only on Devin's words, one logged 2025 touch. This rule contains no 2025 touch. A null is closed: no variant K, M or weight is tried afterwards; one pre-registered follow-up id at most.

## Hypothesis
**H1.** For a mover in his first K = 10 games with the new team, preseason minutes and per-minute production are evidence about the new role that production's recency-average-plus-residual cannot see, because its inputs at the start of a season are last season's games for another team. Blending them with the fixed weights above lowers integer CRPS of pts by at least 0.005 on the affected slice (and by the declared floors for reb, ast, fg3m) in 2023 and 2024.
**Mechanism and an alternative reading.** (a) Minutes: the new team's coach gives a new role (more or fewer minutes). (b) Rates: a new usage and a new teammate set change shots and passes per minute; the 2026 opening-night numbers say this is where the error sits. The alternative reading, which the arms are built to separate: exhibition minutes are garbage-time heavy and noisy, so any benefit may be a shrink toward a typical line (A4) or only minutes (A1 vs A2).

## Playbook check (MODEL_PLAYBOOK.md; class: GBM residual layer, here a fixed blend, plus quantile layer)
1. **Information or capacity?** Information: preseason role is pre-tip, pre-season evidence production cannot see. No capacity is added (nothing fitted but kappa on 2022). A4 and A1 separate role information from generic shrink and from minutes.
2. **Enough data for the class?** Nothing is trained on 2023/2024; production's own training window is unchanged (the OOF forecasts are stored). The blend has zero fitted parameters at scoring; kappa has two parameters from about 1,800 player-games of 2022 preseason. The scoring slice is the limit (power above), not training.
3. **Valid test?** Real tip gating is inherited from A0 (stored OOF). Played-rows only, as A0 (DNP not scored). Integer support for every arm (lambda rescales the continuous grid, then `ceil(q - 0.5)`). Known-at: every preseason game is dated before the season's opener (4 days earlier in the measured seasons); the harness asserts max(preseason date used) < first regular-season date of S and < the row's `game_date`, and that the row's team and mover status use only games before the row (a planted-future test flips later regular-season rows and checks lambda is unchanged). `loaded_at` is a 2026-10-20 backfill, not a known-at: for the live path the ingest must stamp preseason rows at load and the forward harness refuse unstamped ones (not needed for 2023/24). Power for the floor: below (stated). A placebo that leaks the future would give a false fail: A3 uses S-1 games only; A4 permutes within season but uses other players' preseason, which is also pre-tip. A comparator missing information production had: none (A0 sees all regular-season games to date; A2 gains only preseason). Missingness: coverage is reported; uncovered = identity; coverage correlates with the player being on a roster in October, which the mover definition already requires, and the audit checks coverage by starter flag.
4. **Lineage:** above.
5. **Scored against:** CRPS of the whole distribution on the affected slice (the stated question); P(over) at the stored Pinnacle T-60 line is descriptive (CLV method of MODEL_CLV), never a gate. A CRPS gain has not reached the line before (T477), so a PASS here would not be a betting claim.
Class checks: no leakage through missingness (n_pre is a count of preseason games, a pre-season fact; audited by coverage tables by slice); duplicated signals (A0's `min10` already contains the player's last games, so the blend's only new content is the new-team preseason); is it re-expressing the base (A3 near-identity is measured, not assumed).

## What each choice protects against
* Fixed weights, no 2023/2024 fit: a tuned blend on 2023 is exactly the variant-chasing the nulls taught us not to do.
* The affected slice is all mover rows, covered or not: a covered-only slice selects on a feature that might correlate with the outcome.
* Game clustering, BH over 4 stats, two seasons agreeing: row-level CIs were anti-conservative; one season is weak evidence (any 2023-only pass is FAIL_ONE_SEASON).
* A3 and A4 and PLACEBO_TOO: "any blending helps" and "any shrink helps" are the cheapest false wins.
* Integer-support grid for every arm and lambda = 1 equals A0 bit for bit: the 92-126% artifact (T114-T118).
* Rookie slice descriptive: the gate failed; the bar is not moved after seeing the count.
* Season 2025 guard in SQL, loader and test: a 2025 touch is Devin's, logged first.
