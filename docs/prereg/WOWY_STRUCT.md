## The rule in seven lines (as it would be frozen)
1. **Feature (`nba/props/struct_alloc.py`).** For player i in game g: `V_s` = the as-of per-game stat s of tonight's OUT rotation players (the T-60 real-tip report, production's rotation line); `struct_s(i) = V_s x w_i / sum w` over the as-of rotation pool minus the OUT set plus i if absent from the pool; `w` = the as-of per-game stat (RATE) for ast, and FGA + 0.44 FTA + TOV (USAGE) for pts. The columns used are exactly **`struct_u_pts` for pts and `struct_d_ast` for ast** (`struct_s` below means these two); `struct_d_pts` (the RATE version for pts) is NOT used by any arm. No player's own with/without history, no tonight's box, no `assister_id`.
2. **Comparator is warm.** `A0_warm` = production `context_residual` stat heads on features built over history + `nba.duckdb` in ONE `build_features` (every season warm), the E7 training window (rows dated 2019-10-01 or later; production's own window is 2022-10 on), the lower-tail mixture with `pi` / `w_q` refitted inside each block on that window (E7 consequence b), seed 0; integer-support CRPS for every arm.
3. **Arms** (each = A0_warm + columns): **ROLE** (`role_share_s`, the player's as-of share of the whole rotation's stat with no OUT information), **S+R** (ROLE + `struct_s`, i.e. `struct_u_pts` for pts / `struct_d_ast` for ast; the claim), **P+R** (ROLE + `struct_s` permuted within player-season; one draw, `numpy.random.default_rng(0)` per stat and season fit, training rows permuted first and test rows second, groups in `np.unique` key order of `player_id_season`, as `wowy_struct_crps_null.py::perm_player_season`), STRUCT alone (descriptive), and the reported placebo-OUT diagnostic (E5, arms table).
4. **Primary contrast: S+R minus ROLE**, i.e. STRUCT beyond role share, so the claim is strictly redistribution. Metric: integer CRPS per cell, paired, 95% percentile bootstrap clustered by `game_id` (2000 draws, seed 0), computed by `nba/props/metrics.py::paired_score_delta_ci` (draws from `nba/props/metrics.py::_clustered_boot_means`) **as that code stands at the freeze commit** (the house bootstrap `nba/truth/bootstrap.py` does not exist today; it is used only if it exists at the freeze commit, else never); one-sided p = (1 + #{draws with the mean delta >= 0}) / (1 + 2000) from the same 2000 draws, the BH input; game clustering governs every verdict, date clustering is reported and never decides (including when it crosses 0); floor **-0.005**, CI upper < 0.
5. **Cells (the family, m = 3; all others dropped at the gate):** pts x leader-out, pts x top 25% of `|struct_u_pts|` at the fixed cut-off 5.04 (inside VAC24), ast x leader-out. Leader out = the team's as-of per-game leader in the stat is on the OUT list. BH at q = 0.05 over m = 3 within each season; select 2023, report 2024; a cell passes only if it passes both.
6. **Guards (each cell, each season):** (G1) S+R minus P+R <= -t_c, per cell and per season, with t_c frozen from the 2020-22 permutation null (`struct_null`): pts leader out 0.0050, pts top 25% fixed 0.0063, ast leader out 0.0030 (floor-bound; measured 2 sd 0.0016); values rounded toward stricter; (G2) all-rows no-harm: S+R minus A0_warm <= +0.001 on all rows of the A0 row set (the warm-frame rebuild refit by the harness on the E7 window; the stored production arrays are a reproduction check only, never the comparator); (G3) coverage tau 0.10 / 0.20 / 0.80 / 0.90 within 0.01 of A0_warm; (G4) planted-future and NULL-rule tests; (G5) slices with n >= 300 in the cell-season (bench / starter, n_out_rot = 1 / >= 2, star out, first 15 team games, playoffs) not worse by > +0.01, S+R minus ROLE. **Definitions (chosen before scoring):** bench / starter = the as-of `last_starter` column (1 if he started his previous game, 0 if not; NaN rows are in neither slice); `n_out_rot` = the production column of that name; star out = `V_pts >= 45.0087` for the pts cells and `V_ast >= 10.4241` for the ast cell (the 0.75 quantile of `V_s` among 46,183 rows of 2020-21 with a usable report and a non-empty rotation OUT set, features only, `research/eval/wowy_struct_star_cuts.py`, fixed, never re-cut on 2022-24; `V_s` = the as-of per-game stat of tonight's OUT rotation players, the production `vac_s`); first 15 team games = `team_game_no < 15` (0-based, `context_residual.py`); playoffs = `game_id` prefix `004`. **A slice with n < 300 in a cell-season is reported (n, delta, interval) and NOT gated.** At review (features only, no outcome) the playoffs slice was below 300 in every cell-season (21 to 115 rows) and `n_out_rot = 1` was below 300 for pts top 25% (66 / 75 rows), so those slices are reported, not gated; star out and `n_out_rot >= 2` hold 90-99% of their cells and nearly duplicate the cell.

## Hypothesis
H1: the share of tonight's vacated points that a player's as-of production predicts he will absorb among the REMAINING rotation, scaled by what is vacated, lowers integer CRPS of his points and assists beyond (a) the production features (which carry the team totals `vac_*`) and (b) his plain role share of the team's production. Null: no change. Claimed information: who is out tonight (the report) combined with who is left (the as-of pool); not claimed: anything about a player's own past with and without a teammate (the WOWY columns, parked: `WOWY_REDISTRIBUTION.md`).

## Features (as-of; `nba/props/struct_alloc.py`, tests `tests/props/test_struct_alloc.py`)
* **State:** exponentially weighted (half-life 10 games) means of minutes, pts, reb, ast, fg3m, FGA, FTA, TOV over the player's games strictly before the game date, across teams and seasons (history + `nba.duckdb` box).
* **OUT set:** `flagged_from_availability` (status out, latest snapshot with `as_of <= real tip - 60`) restricted to rotation players (as-of `min40 >= 12`, >= 5 prior games), exactly `vacated_features`; 2 of 12,010 OUT entries played that night (audit): OUT is the report, not the box.
* **Pool:** players with >= 5 appearances in the team's previous 10 games and a mean of >= 12 minutes, strictly earlier games, minus the OUT set, plus the scored player if absent (8.4-8.7% of pool members did not play that night and stay in the denominator: pre-tip, as production).
* **Columns:** `struct_d_{pts,ast}` (RATE), `struct_u_pts` (USAGE), `role_share_s` = the player's as-of stat / (pool stat + V + his own stat if absent from the pool) with the OUT players' stat IN the denominator and nothing allocated, `leader_out_s`, `V_s`.
* **NULL rule (B4):** reported game and nobody rotation-OUT -> explicit 0; no usable report -> NULL, never "nobody out" (`attach_struct`, same as `attach_wowy` and production `vac_*`; unit test `test_attach_struct_null_rule_and_role_share`). The earlier gate's `fill_null(0)` on every row is replaced; the number was unaffected (the GBM drops no-report rows).
* Not used: `possessions.assister_id` (D15), potential assists (no column), per-100-possession rates (per-minute rate x minutes is the as-of equivalent), position, tracking (those belong to PROP).

## Arms (E7: every arm trains on rows dated 2019-10-01 or later, test seasons 2023 and 2024; rows before 2019-10-01 warm features only; production month blocks; seed 0; production LightGBM settings; `pi`, `w_q` refitted in each block on the same window for every arm, pending the choice in E7 (b))

| **S+R** | `role_share_s`, `struct_u_pts` (pts) / `struct_d_ast` (ast) | **the claim** (contrast S+R minus ROLE) |
| P+R | `role_share_s`, `struct_u_pts` / `struct_d_ast` permuted within player-season (train and test permuted separately; one draw, `default_rng(0)` per stat and season fit) | capacity / player-level guard (G1) |
| STRUCT alone | `struct_u_pts` / `struct_d_ast` | descriptive |
| PLACEBO-OUT (E5) | `role_share_s` plus `struct_s` recomputed from the team's PREVIOUS-game OUT list (the last earlier game of that team with a usable report) in place of tonight's | reported diagnostic, no verdict, no gate |

**Why P+R and ROLE stay the guards and PLACEBO-OUT is only reported (E5).** The playbook's fair test for trees names a past-only placebo of the new information. P+R tests whether the column gains through player-level information with the wrong row; ROLE tests whether the gain is a role share; PLACEBO-OUT tests whether any OUT list of the same size and kind, but not tonight's, gains the same, which is the narrowest 'it is the report' control. Its column needs new code in the harness, built after the freeze, so it is a diagnostic: its failure to be worse than S+R is reported beside the verdict and never rescues or kills a cell.

**Comparator and training window.** `A0_warm` is not the stored production arrays: production's own 2022 rows are built from `nba.duckdb` alone (32.6% of 2022 rows have a different `n_out_rot` than a history-inclusive build, adversary F1), and the stored 2024 arrays differ from a rebuild on 79.9% of rows (F2 neutral-site restoration). The comparator is the same production code on the warm frame and **the E7 window (rows dated 2019-10-01 or later)**, the same window as every other arm, refit by the harness on the data version of the run; the stored arrays are a reproduction check (2023 to 1e-6 on shared rows where the OUT sets agree; 2024 within |delta CRPS| <= 0.0002) and the stored `pi` / `w_q` (fitted on the cold frame) are used only in that descriptive reproduction run; the verdict arms follow E7 (b).

## E7: the 2019+ training window (Devin's decision, 2026-10-14; added after the pre-freeze review, before any 2023/24 arm exists)
**Rule.** Every arm (A0_warm, ROLE, S+R, P+R, STRUCT alone, and the PLACEBO-OUT diagnostic) is refitted on the warm frame from rows dated **2019-10-01** on, instead of 2022-10-01. Same production month-block walk-forward refits, same seeds and settings, same A0 row set. Test seasons stay 2023 and 2024; a cell must pass both. 2019-10-01 is the floor because the history injury reports start in 2019-20, so OUT sets are unknown before that (rows before it have no report: they warm features only and are not training rows). The comparator is the same code with the same window as the arms, so the contrast S+R minus ROLE is unchanged in construction. **What it protects against:** the draft's one-season training window starved the tree of rows for a weak column (the review's playbook check 2) and made a 2023-only fail weak evidence; **what it costs:** A0_warm is no longer production.

**Consequences, stated, not silently chosen.**
* **(a) Comparator reproduction.** The stored production arrays (window 2022-10 on) reproduce only the 2022-10 window. That run is kept as a **descriptive reproduction check** (the 2023 reproduction to 1e-6 and 2024 to 0.0002 above) and as a descriptive 2022-window contrast (verdicts do not use it). A0_warm is now **production code on a longer window**, not production; a pass says nothing about the deployed model until production adopts the window (see e).
* **(b) Stored mixture `pi` / `w_q` (Devin's decision, 2026-10-14: refit).** The stored `pi` / `w_q` were fitted on the cold 2022+ frame and are NOT used by any verdict arm. **Rule:** inside every month block, for every arm, `pi` (short-minutes classifier on `COMMON_FEATURES`) and `w_q` are refitted on that block's 2019+ training rows (what `ContextResidualModel(lower_tail="mixture")` does when it fits). `pi` uses only `COMMON_FEATURES`, so it is identical across arms in a block and cannot carry the contrast; the heads' calibration `z` and the mixture's short-minutes component come from the same rows. Cost: A0_warm differs from stored production in `pi` as well, which is already stated in (a).
* **(c) Bubble season 2019-20 (and the short 2020-21 season).** No special casing: bubble rows (restart 2020-07-30, neutral sites, empty arenas) and 2020-21 rows are ordinary training rows, with their `has_report` flags as recorded; no row is dropped or weighted. Reason: no evidence that they are worse for a tree that takes `has_report`, rest and home flags as features; special-casing would be a fork with no data behind it. A year-by-year descriptive split of the S+R minus ROLE contrast is not part of the rule.
* **(d) Gate, non-negotiable 8.** The test rows and games of 2023 and 2024 do not change (cells 4,301 / 5,667, 3,560 / 4,472, 4,926 / 4,409 rows; 388 / 493, 811 / 903, 440 / 387 games), so the row-count part of the gate stands. **The power numbers do NOT carry over untouched**: they use forecasts of the stored 2022-window A0 (second moments of the forecast grids), and a 2019+ A0_warm changes those forecasts slightly; they are not re-measured here (re-measuring needs a 2019+ A0_warm fit on 2023 / 2024 forecast rows, forecasts only; it is a freeze-blocker item below, run before the freeze, outcome-free in version B).
