## Hypothesis
H1: the share of tonight's vacated points that a player's as-of production predicts he will absorb among the REMAINING rotation, scaled by what is vacated, lowers integer CRPS of his points and assists beyond (a) the production features (which carry the team totals `vac_*`) and (b) his plain role share of the team's production. Null: no change. Claimed information: who is out tonight (the report) combined with who is left (the as-of pool); not claimed: anything about a player's own past with and without a teammate (the WOWY columns, parked: `WOWY_REDISTRIBUTION.md`).

**H2 (the twist; secondary, not in the verdict family).** When a star sits, his possessions go to a fill-in who produces at about replacement level. A teammate's RAW on/off delta mixes 'teammates absorb the star's usage' with 'the fill-in produces at replacement level'. Setting each teammate's on/off delta per possession against the change expected from a replacement fill-in, and shrinking the noisy per-pair history toward that league-wide expectation (instead of toward zero, as `wowy.py` did), should give a more stable per-pair signal than the raw history that died on small-sample noise, and should beat the plain allocation S_R. Null: T_R is no better than S_R (and RAW). **Stated against it:** (i) the baseline half of the twist (`rb`, replacement-subtracted vacancy) is the construction REPLACEMENT_DELTA closed at its gate (its subtraction lost to its own unsubtracted twin in all three dev designs; the trees already see `vac_*`); (ii) the only new content is the on/off excess `rx`, which is the parked WOWY pair-history family (own-history columns: mostly noise); (iii) the dev GBM check below has T_R behind S+R in all 12 cell-designs (the three declared cells plus the dropped ast top-25% cell, three designs each).

## Features (as-of; S: `nba/props/struct_alloc.py`, tests `tests/props/test_struct_alloc.py`; T: `nba/props/wowy_repl.py`, tests `tests/props/test_wowy_repl.py`)

* **OUT set:** `flagged_from_availability` (status out, latest snapshot with `as_of <= real tip - 60`) restricted to rotation players (as-of `min40 >= 12`, >= 5 prior games), exactly `vacated_features`; 2 of 12,010 OUT entries played that night (audit): OUT is the report, not the box.
* **Pool:** players with >= 5 appearances in the team's previous 10 games and a mean of >= 12 minutes, strictly earlier games, minus the OUT set, plus the scored player if absent (8.4-8.7% of pool members did not play that night and stay in the denominator: pre-tip, as production).
* **Columns:** `struct_d_{pts,ast}` (RATE), `struct_u_pts` (USAGE), `role_share_s` = the player's as-of stat / (pool stat + V + his own stat if absent from the pool) with the OUT players' stat IN the denominator and nothing allocated, `leader_out_s`, `V_s`.
* **NULL rule (B4):** reported game and nobody rotation-OUT -> explicit 0; no usable report -> NULL, never "nobody out" (`attach_struct`, same as `attach_wowy` and production `vac_*`; unit test `test_attach_struct_null_rule_and_role_share`). * Not used by S_R: `possessions.assister_id` (D15), potential assists (no column), position, tracking (those belong to PROP). The T columns below do use per-possession rates from the box.
* **T columns (STRUCT_REPL).** Box inputs and the replacement definition are `nba/props/repl_delta.py`'s, unchanged: a player's possessions on the floor `P` = game possessions x minutes / (team minutes / 5) (game possessions = mean of both teams' FGA + 0.44 FTA - OREB + TOV); usage numerator = FGA + 0.44 FTA + TOV; the replacement rate of season S = the pooled per-possession rate of FRINGE player-seasons (rank above 9 by total minutes within the team-season) of seasons S-2 and S-1 (season S never enters; unit-tested; 2024: pts 0.1805, ast 0.0412, usage 0.1864); the player rate is the empirical-Bayes `r = (S + k repl) / (Q + k)` over ALL earlier games (strictly before the game date) with k from method of moments on 2015-2021 player-seasons (pts 103.3, ast 64.3, usage 49.7 possessions; `PRIORS_SHA256` pins the rates and k). For tonight's game g, scored player i and OUT rotation teammate j (production's OUT set, `flagged_from_availability` restricted to the rotation line, parity with production's `vac_min` and `n_out_rot` unit-tested in REPLACEMENT_DELTA): `d_ij(s)` = i's stat per possession in his past games WITHOUT j minus WITH j (j's current stint with the team, the team's previous 100 games, only games i played; `wowy.py`'s rules); `lam_ij = n_wo / (n_wo + 6)`, zero when either side is empty; `V_j = (u_j - u_repl) x P_exp_j` (j's shrunk usage above replacement x his expected possessions, production `min10` x the team's as-of pace); `den` = the pool's usage-weight sum (plus i's own when absent from the pool); `E_ij(s) = r_is x V_j / den` (the change in i's per-possession rate if the vacated usage above replacement is handed to the pool by usage weight and converted at i's own efficiency). Columns, in stat units per game (x `P_exp_i`, i's expected possessions): `rb_s = sum_j E_ij P_exp_i`, `rx_s = sum_j lam_ij (d_ij - E_ij) P_exp_i`, `rr_s = sum_j lam_ij d_ij P_exp_i` (so `rr = rx + sum lam E P`; unit-tested), `ro_conf = sum_j lam_ij`. Stats: pts and ast only (the declared cells). NULL rule as production: no usable report -> NULL, reported with nobody rotation-OUT -> explicit 0; `rb` / `rx` / `rr` are also NULL where the team's pace (fewer than 3 earlier games) or the pool (fewer than 6 earlier team games) is undefined (audited below). **Identity placebo `pq_*`:** the same code with each OUT rotation player's identity replaced by a uniformly drawn same-team as-of pool member who is NOT out tonight (his own rates, usage and pair history; the OUT player's expected possessions kept), seed 0, deterministic per (game, team).
* **How T differs from REPLACEMENT_DELTA's `rd`:** `rd` hands out j's stat above replacement by usage share; `rb` hands out j's usage above replacement and converts at the receiver's own efficiency (stat per usage possession), and T adds the pair-history excess `rx`. `rb` is nevertheless in the family REPLACEMENT_DELTA closed (corr with the STRUCT column 0.56 to 0.85 in the cells).
