3. **Era tokens.** Three ctx channels `era_bubble`, `era_nofans`, `era_limited` from `game_era_flags` (the 2020 bubble restart, the no-fans and limited-fans periods of 2020-21). * **(a) Per-row export** (`rows.parquet`, `rows.csv`), every scored 2025 row for each of pts, reb, ast, fg3m: game_id, date, player_id, player name (from the odds table's `player_name`; blank if the player has no quote), team_id and opp_id (numeric ids), stat, actual, minutes, starter (as-of `starter10 >= 0.5`) and `starter10`, `p_play`, and per arm: q05, q10, q25, q50, q75, q90, q95 (quantile tau of the 199-point equal-mass integer grid), mean, integer CRPS, randomised PIT (seed 0). `p_play` is the P(play) of the RECENT_ROLE_HOLDOUT model R from its cached 2025 predictions (`data/ops/recent_role_holdout_cache/run/blocks`, rostered not-OUT rows only); blank where absent. Prices: for Pinnacle and DK the stored T-60 main line, both American prices, the multiplicative de-vigged P(over), and each arm's P(over line) = P(Y > line) on the integer grid. Lines that are not x.5 are dropped (counted). Second-night-of-back-to-back (the team played the previous calendar day) and "teammate returns" flags are in the per-row table.
* **(b) Confusion matrices**, per stat and for all stats, per arm and book (Pinnacle, DK separately): (i) over/under at the stored line (predicted over if the arm's P(over line) > 0.5) against the outcome, with the market's de-vigged P(over) > 0.5 as a comparator row; (ii) the direction implied by the arm's own median (median above the line = over) against the outcome; (iii) by confidence bucket |P - 0.5| in [0, 0.05), [0.05, 0.10), [0.10, 0.20), [0.20, 0.5], each arm and the market (bucketed by its own |P - 0.5|). 'Over' is the positive class. Every cell has n and a game-clustered 95% CI for accuracy / hit rate (2000 draws, seed 0, the MODEL_CLV bootstrap). Definitions follow `market_ou_descriptive.py`.
* **(c) Spot-check pack** (`spot_check.md`): per stat, the 10 rows where AVG beat FULL most and the 10 where it lost most (by integer CRPS), and the same for AVG against A0; the same lists inside slices all, starters, bench, `p_play < 0.9`, second night of a back-to-back, and "a teammate who averaged >= 30 minutes over his last 10 appearances plays after >= 3 missed team games" (an ex-post flag: it needs who played). Each row shows date, name, team vs opponent ids, line, actual, minutes, and the q10 / q50 / q90 of both arms.
* **(d) User simulation** (`simulation.md/json`, `sim_bankroll_paths.csv`). Personas fixed now. **P0** keep your money (no bets). **P1** flat $20 on every leg where the arm's P(side) minus the de-vigged market P(side) is >= 0.03, at the stored vigged T-60 price. **P2** the same with >= 0.06. **P3** quarter-Kelly on a $500 bankroll, max $20 per bet: f = (b p - (1 - p)) / b with the arm's p and the vigged decimal price, the better side if f > 0, stake = min($20, 0.25 f bankroll at the start of the night), nightly stakes scaled down proportionally if they would exceed that bankroll. **P4** one 2-leg same-night parlay per night from the two highest-edge legs (edge > 0) on different games, $10, legs assumed independent (the price is the product of the legs' prices; this is an assumption, disclosed, and the CI clusters on the first leg's game). Books: Pinnacle and DK separately; one bet per (player, stat, night, book) at the T-60 price; the side is the arm's side of the market's de-vigged P. Reported per season x book x arm x persona: bets, staked, P&L, ROI = P&L / staked with a game-clustered 95% CI (2000 draws, seed 0), maximum drawdown of the nightly cumulative P&L, longest losing streak (bets in time order), the nightly bankroll path (CSV), and the share of months (with a bet) in profit. The same simulation is run on 2023 and 2024 from the same run's arrays and **flagged already-seen**: the construction was chosen on them (their SEQ member is the Colab rerun, not the stored E2). **This is descriptive, not a test of edge. Hit rates and ROI on one season are noisy; no persona is a recommendation.** Prices are the stored snapshots; no fee, limit or line-shopping model; the games-clustered CI ignores that bets within a night share a bankroll path (P3) and that a parlay spans two games (P4).
* **(e) Holdout exposure.** This step reads season-2025 **prop prices** from `data/odds/odds_history.duckdb`, the 2025 box scores and schedule from `nba.duckdb` (names are not stored there), and the cached 2025 P(play) predictions. The holdout row names the prop prices as touched by the descriptive step.


## Kill criteria
* A run-time gate fails: stop, nothing is scored; the fix is a new id (and, if the touch marker exists, the touch is consumed).
* Any E2P, E2S, E2 or AVG forecast scored on 2025 before the freeze commit and the committed row: the rule is void.
* The Colab job does not complete all three programs with 3 seeds and full budgets: no 2025 scoring; rerun the Colab job (nothing was scored); never cut an arm.
* Not a pass: closed. No other weights, members, gates, blends or seeds; no re-run on 2025; at most ONE pre-registered follow-up (new id), only after a pass.
