## The rule in ten lines (as it would be frozen if the gate allows it)
1. **Rows and stats.** The A0 rows of ODDS_HISTORY, `reports/odds_history/model_rows_{2023,2024}_{pts,reb,ast,fg3m}.parquet` (production out-of-fold forecasts, `n_prior >= 5`), **regular-season game ids only (prefix `002`)**, restricted to the **intersection** of the four stats' (game_id, player_id) sets so each player-game is scored in all four; the number dropped per stat is reported (measured so far: 0). If a stat's stored A0 rows did not exist the stat would be stopped, not rebuilt; all eight files exist. Select 2023, report 2024; season 2025 never loaded. **A0 = production props_context_residual as stored**, integer-grid CRPS (199-point grid, `ceil(q - 0.5)`) for EVERY arm on the same rows.
2. **Features and arm M: T506's, unchanged** (the six features; `off_arch` categorical incl. unknown; `e_usual, delta_rate, shift_l1, retained, def_unknown_share`; archetypes k = 6/6 from the PRIOR season; lift clip; roster window 10 games / 30 days; MIN_W 5; decay 0.97; LightGBM 4 leaves, 150 trees, lr 0.05, min 20 rows per leaf, seed 0). **Arm M for each stat = A0 + these features in that stat's own layer**; no cross-stat features, no tuning.
3. **PRIMARY metric, the only claim: the composite CM.** CM = mean over the four stats s of [ (mean over rows of the paired integer-CRPS delta, arm M minus A0, for stat s) / floor_s ], in floor units, rows being the same player-games in every stat. **Floors: pts 0.005, reb 0.003, ast 0.002, fg3m 0.001** (`FLOORS` in `archetype_matchup_all_rule.py`; pts/ast/fg3m equal T505/T506's, reb from `role_prior_salary_rule`). Negative CM is a gain; CM = -1 means the average stat improved by one floor.
4. **Inference.** The composite is computed per row (the mean over stats of delta_s / floor_s), so a bootstrap of that vector resamples the SAME clusters in all four stats (a joint block bootstrap, 2000 draws, seed 0); clusters: game, player-season, ISO week; the binding CI is the widest. No clustering of the four stats separately.
5. **Pass (2024; 2023 selects).** All must hold: (a) 2023 CM point <= -1.0 (otherwise 2024 is not scored); (b) 2024 CM <= -1.0 floor units; (c) the binding 95% CI upper bound < 0; (d) **no stat's own delta point estimate exceeds +1.0 floor units** (no stat may be hurt to buy another); (e) the top-5 player-season share of the composite gain <= 0.5; (f) the shuffled control (defender archetype labels permuted within team-game, `permute_within`, seed 0, the whole of arm M refit identically in all four stats) keeps strictly less than half of the composite gain. One test, no BH family. Code: `archetype_matchup_all_rule.primary_verdict`.
6. **Per-stat deltas and CIs are descriptive only**: no per-stat claim, no per-stat pass, whatever they show. **Each stat's share of CM is reported (`stat_shares`) in every table**, so the composite cannot be carried silently by one stat (the floors differ by 5x, so a stat with a small floor moves CM the most per unit of CRPS; this is by construction of the metric, and the share table shows it).
7. **Controls and audits.** (a) Positive control: a planted per-player-season shift in all four stats, oracle arm, must clear (point <= -1.0 floor units, binding CI upper bound < 0) in the gate. (b) Shuffled control as in 5(f). (c) Planted-future identity on the real table (T506's, reused unchanged). (d) Missingness audit of every new column by `min10` bucket (T506's; in the eval also against realised minutes). (e) Knockouts, descriptive: unknown category dropped, shift features dropped, `off_arch` dropped.
8. **Slices, descriptive only:** by stat, by `min10` bucket, by the 90th-percentile `shift_l1` slice of 2023 (T506's T2 definition, as a slice, not a test). Nothing is claimed from a slice.
9. **Explanations.** Reason strings (`reason_string`) never feed a score and are not evaluated here.
10. **What changes in production: nothing.** A PASS is a candidate for a live shadow in the 2026-27 forward log and, on Devin's word, one logged 2025 touch (this rule contains none). A null closes the matchup idea for good.

## Known before the gate (said plainly)
T506's single-seed noise half-widths, in floor units, were roughly pts 1.5 / 1.1, reb unknown, ast 0.8 / 0.7, fg3m 0.6 / 0.5 (2023 / 2024). With independent noise per stat on common clusters the composite half-width is about sqrt(sum of squares) / 4, i.e. about 0.4 to 0.5, so **the gate is expected to PASS**. That is a statement about power, not about an effect. The rule's difficulty is the claim: CM <= -1.0 needs the average stat to gain one full floor (pts 0.005, reb 0.003, ast 0.002, fg3m 0.001 of CRPS) from a six-feature categorical layer in a forecast that already holds recency, usage and the report, and no stat may lose more than one floor. If the gate passes and the frozen test does not, the closing statement is the honest one: "tested at adequate power; no composite gain".

## What each choice protects against
* **One composite, in floor units, one test:** four stats × two tests with BH (T506) is a family where a null is cheap to dodge and a lucky stat is cheap to claim; the composite is one pre-declared number.
* **Floor units:** CRPS deltas of pts, reb, ast, fg3m are on different scales; dividing by each stat's practical floor makes "one floor" the common currency (and exposes the 5x differences via the share table).
* **No-stat-hurt guard (+1.0 floor):** a composite can be bought by sacrificing one stat; the guard stops it.
* **Shares of CM reported:** the composite cannot be dominated silently by one stat; it is visible, and descriptive.
* **Intersection rows:** the same player-games in all four stats keep the joint bootstrap honest (the same clusters, the same exposure).
* **Joint (not per-stat) block bootstrap:** the four deltas of one player-game are correlated; resampling per stat would understate the composite's variance.
* **Shuffled control within team-game; top-5 guard; binding = widest of three clusterings:** inherited from T506/T500 (generic residual shrink; five players carrying the gain; anti-conservative game-only CIs).
* **20-seed mean detectable effect:** one seed moves by tens of percent (issue #25).
* **Select 2023 then report 2024:** the only way 2024 stays a report season.
* **Reb floor 0.003, from the project module, fixed here:** not tuned after seeing reb's power.
* **Gate before freeze, thresholds in code:** F9 and F12 could not pass their own gates.

## Kill criteria
* Gate FAIL: not freezable as drafted; recorded; the matchup idea stays closed.
* 2023 CM point not <= -1.0: 2024 is not scored (reported as a null).
* Any of: planted-future identity fails; shuffled control keeps >= 50% of the composite gain; top-5 share > 0.5; any stat's delta > +1.0 floor; a flagged missingness column that predicts realised minutes AND carries the gain in its knockout. Each voids the claim.
* 2024 CI upper bound < 0 only under game clustering (not under the binding): a null for claim purposes.
