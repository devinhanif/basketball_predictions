## 1. The rule in nine lines
1. **Stage 1 (depth model, ridge, alpha 10, standardised inputs):** team-game depth `D` = players with >= 8 minutes (Devin's scratch definition) regressed on as-of team-game inputs: own and opponent `pace10` (mean box-possession pace of the team's previous 10 games), own and opponent `win20` (win share over the previous 20 games), `abs_exp_margin` (the Elo expected margin already in R19), rest days and back-to-back flags, `n_out_rot` (rotation players OUT on the latest report stamped <= real tip - 60 min, production's cut), `elig_n` (as-of count of non-OUT players with `min10 >= 5`), and `game_no` (team games into the season, capped at 30). Walk-forward by calendar month, strictly earlier regular-season team-games, 2022 as warm-up. Output `z` = (D_hat - as-of mean of fitted D_hat) / as-of sd.
2. **Stage 2 (the layer, two slopes, rotation-edge rows only):** `logit p_B = logit p_base + g_p * z` (one slope, no intercept, fitted by logistic regression on strictly earlier edge rows) and conditional-grid scale `exp(g_m * z)` on the stored conditional quantiles (one slope, no intercept, fitted by OLS of `log(minutes / min10)` on `z` over strictly earlier played edge rows). Non-edge rows are byte-identical to the base arm.
3. **Edge rows (as-of):** eligible rows of the team-game ranked 8 to 12 by `min10` (descending, ties by player id) and not a usual starter (`starter10 < 0.5`); regular-season games only (`game_id` begins `002`); population = the stored Cell-P rows of PPLAY_MINUTES_VALID (rostered, `n_played_prior >= 5`, not OUT at T-60).
4. **Arms (same rows, paired):** **A0** = production as served (P0 and the stored A0 conditional integer grids); **A0c** = P0 with its walk-forward Platt map; **B1** = A0c + layer (primary); **B2** = A0 + layer (secondary); **R19** and **R19+layer** (secondary: does the layer add to the strongest stored P(play) model); **PL** = B1 with the pace and win columns of stage 1 permuted across the teams of the same date at predict time (placebo); **KO** = B1 with `pace10` and `win20` (own and opponent) removed from stage 1 and re-fitted (knockout: what the pace and quality information adds beyond rest, injuries, roster size and game number).
5. **Primary, report season 2024, edge rows:** unconditional integer CRPS, **B1 minus A0c**, per stat, floor **-0.005**, paired, 95% CI = the envelope of game, team-season and ISO-week cluster bootstraps (B = 5000, seed 2026). Unconditional distribution = point mass `1 - p` at 0 plus the conditional integer grid with weight `p` (DNP rows take the conditional grid of that player's latest earlier played row, the PPLAY_INTO_PROPS_LIVE convention, identical across arms). Every arm is scored on the same integer grid (`ceil(q - 0.5)`, floor 0, running max; T114-T118).
6. **Stats that carry a vote: pts only** (section 9: reb, ast and fg3m cannot reach -0.005 even if the planted effect is exactly right; they are reported, outside the family, with their ceilings). BH over the voting family (m = 1 here, so BH is the plain one-sided p; the family is declared so that a stat added later cannot enter silently).
7. **Mechanism condition (necessary for any PASS, no floor):** B1 minus A0c edge-row P(play) log loss has point < 0 and binding CI upper < 0 in 2024. The house floor (-0.010) is unreachable here (section 9) and is not used; the condition says the CRPS gain must come through who plays.
8. **PASS (pts)** needs all of: point <= -0.005 with binding CI upper < 0; BH p <= 0.05; mechanism condition; 2023 point negative and `g_p > 0` at the last 2023 block; B1 minus PL <= -0.0025 with CI upper < 0; B1 minus KO <= -0.0025 (pace and quality carry it); non-edge rows byte-identical; no slice (n >= 300) worse by more than +0.01; mid-coverage at tau 0.10/0.20/0.80/0.90 within 0.01 of A0c; stage-1 walk-forward out-of-sample R^2 >= 0.01 in 2024. **CLOSED** = point > -0.002. Else INCONCLUSIVE; a guard failure with the first three holding = GUARD_FAIL.
9. Select 2023 / report 2024; **no 2025 touch is earned or requested**; one pass, no reruns, no variants; a PASS is a proposal to Devin; a new question gets a new id. The live 2026-27 log (full official roster, true T-60) is the confirming venue.

## 2. Hypothesis, null, and what a result would mean
- **H1 (Devin, FAN_KNOWLEDGE 2026-10-23).** "They run a rotation based on who has energy; if the pace is taxing they play more players; if the team sucks they also play more players." Scratch evidence, 2022-24 close games (|margin| <= 8), as-of prior-10 pace and prior win%: +1 SD pace -> +0.167 [+0.141, +0.201] players >= 8 min; +10 pts prior win% -> -0.072 [-0.092, -0.052]; bad teams (<.35) 9.29 vs 8.88 (n 481 / 483). Both directions hold; the size is 0.2 to 0.4 players per team-game. (Quoted from the FAN_KNOWLEDGE entry; not reproduced here.)
- **What it predicts for the rows this rule touches.** About 4.5 edge rows per team-game (section 9). If one SD of pace moves depth by 0.167 players, each edge player's P(play) moves about +0.04 (planted rms 0.055 with the quality term). That is the largest effect Devin's numbers support, and it is a ceiling for this test: a fitted model recovers part of it.
- **H0.** Production's recency play rates, R19's game context (rest, back-to-back, Elo margin, tanking incentive, games into season) and the T-60 report already carry whatever pace and quality tell about who plays, or the pace and quality signal is too small against the noise in who plays (an edge player's P(play) has per-row sd about 0.45).
- **If PASS:** the first evidence that team-level rotation size, not just each player's own history, is a P(play) input; a proposal for a production layer (Devin's call). **If not:** the expected answer, recorded; the live log is the venue (section 9, option D).

## 4. Features and arms (as-of; `research/eval/rotation_depth_pplay_gate.py` builds the first set, unit-tested)
- `pace_game` = (own box possessions + opponent box possessions) / 2 per game; box possessions = FGA + 0.44 FTA - OREB + TOV summed over the team's box rows. `pace10` / `win20` are rolling means over the team's strictly earlier games (shifted by one before rolling, minimum 3 games), across seasons. Planted-future tests (`tests/research/rotation_depth_pplay/`, 3 passing): the as-of values equal a manual strictly-earlier mean, and editing every box row from game 10 on leaves the features of games 0-10 byte-identical.
- `min10`: mean minutes over the player's last 10 PLAYED games dated strictly before the row's date (day-shifted as-of join), NULL never occurs on edge rows (0.0%).
- `n_out_rot` / `elig_n` / `abs_exp_margin` / rest and back-to-back: production's own builders (`vacated_features`, the Elo config `configs/mov_elo_tuned.yaml`, the schedule with real tips, `tip_source="real"`). **Not built or tied in the gate** (open item for the harness: the OUT tie to production's `n_out_rot`, 0 differences, as QUESTIONABLE_CASCADE did).
- Missingness: every new column has a NaN rule written before use (windows need >= 3 games; a game with no usable report snapshot is NaN in `n_out_rot`, never imputed as zero) and an outcome-bucket audit (G7 of the harness; the QC audit showed the NaN pattern of the report is flat in realised minutes).

## 5. What each choice protects against
- **Edge rows only, starters untouched.** TEAM_BUDGET showed a team-wide constraint hurts the players who do play. Starters are a built-in negative control: their rows must equal A0 byte for byte.
- **A0c as the primary comparator, not A0.** R19 - P0 is dominated by calibration and current-form information (T484: no report group still -0.075; the gain is DNP streaks and recent minutes). A depth layer measured against uncalibrated P0 would be credited with calibration (D2 minus D1 in PPLAY_MINUTES_VALID). B2 (vs A0) is secondary for that reason.
- **Two slopes, no intercept.** One parameter per channel keeps this information, not capacity (playbook question 1). The layer cannot repair the live level miss (an intercept would); that is deliberate: the level miss is a population problem (section 8).
- **Unconditional integer CRPS for the vote, log loss as a mechanism condition.** P(play) only matters on rows that can fail to play; the conditional-on-playing metrics cannot move with p. CRPS is where the floor is expressed (CLAUDE.md); log loss has no reachable house floor here, so it is a necessary condition, not a threshold.
- **Pts only votes.** A floor a stat cannot reach is a guaranteed CLOSED, not a test (non-negotiable 8). The other three are reported with their ceilings so the reader sees why.
- **Envelope of game, team-season and week clustering.** `pace10` and `win20` are persistent within a team-season (30 team-seasons a year), coach habits are persistent, and calendar shocks hit a week; the widest binds (as QUESTIONABLE_CASCADE). The gate's simulated draws have independent labels, so their team-season and week design effects (0.7 to 2.1) say nothing about real residual persistence; sizing uses the measured P(play) design effect range 1.3 to 2.0.
- **PL (cross-team permutation within a date, as-of inputs only).** The ADVANCED_RATES placebo carried future information (T459); this one cannot. A gain from "any extra team-level column" or from the date's mix shows up in PL. **KO** says whether pace and quality, not rest and roster size, carry it. The permutation-null size of the PL guard (K = 50) must be written into the harness before the freeze (BEST_PRACTICES 2026-10-12); the run refuses until then.
- **Select 2023 / report 2024, no pooling in the base design.** A pooled design drops the sign check and leaks the fit into the report season (option B below).
- **Regular season only.** Playoff rotations tighten for a different reason; including them put 7% NaN windows in the first gate run (found and removed before any number was kept).

- **C is the only place rotation depth could live**, and Devin's own scratch bounds depth at 0.2 to 0.4 players per team-game (about 3 to 5% of 7.35). C also holds fringe/two-way players with history; its CI is wide.
- **Answer.** Part of the live miss is a bug-like roster-construction issue (A, 58%) and part a regime/population defect in the P(play) default and the play-rate definition (B, 19%, with the box-listing change behind it); a rotation-depth model addresses at most a few percent of it.
