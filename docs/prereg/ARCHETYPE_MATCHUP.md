## The rule in ten lines (as it would be frozen if the gate allows it)
1. **Rows.** The A0 row set of ODDS_HISTORY: `reports/odds_history/model_rows_{2023,2024}_{pts,ast,fg3m}.parquet` (production out-of-fold forecasts, career `n_prior >= 5`), **restricted to regular-season game ids (prefix `002`; Amendment 1: the stored files also hold playoff `004` and play-in `005` rows, which T505's description of them as "regular season" missed)**, identical row sets across stats (checked in the gate). Select 2023, report 2024; season 2025 never loaded; every SQL carries `season <= 2024`. Primary stat pts; ast and fg3m secondary. **A0 = production props_context_residual as stored**, integer-grid CRPS (199-point grid, `ceil(q - 0.5)`) for EVERY arm on the same rows.
2. **Categories (fixed, no tuning).** Offensive archetype of every scorer (6 clusters) and defensive archetype of every defender (6 clusters) from `research.coldstart.archetypes.fit_archetypes` (k-means, seed 0) fitted on the PRIOR season only (>= 300 minutes; the T505 features). **"Unknown" (anyone not clustered: new, below the minutes bar, no static data) is its own 7th category on both sides**, never a missing value; T505 found archetype-unknown tracks role (64-68% of `min10 < 10` rows), so the unknown category is audited and has a knockout.
3. **Utilization, both parts, from the prior season's matchup table only.** (i) Assignment: `A[a, b]` = the share of offensive archetype a's matchup minutes taken by defensive archetype b; `pi[b]` = b's share of all matchup minutes; `lift[a, b] = clip(A / pi, 0.1, 10)` (how much more than its base rate archetype b guards archetype a). (ii) Defender utilization: each opposing defender's normal defensive minutes per game played, decayed 0.97 per own game, strictly before tonight, current plus previous season (`decayed_asof`).
4. **Tonight's opposing roster.** Defenders who defended for the opposing team in its previous 10 games within 30 days before tonight, whose last known team is that team, and whose normal minutes are >= 5. **Usual mix** of scorer i (archetype a): sum over the roster of `w_j * lift[a, b_j]`, summed by b_j, normalised. **Tonight's mix**: the same with the players on the pre-tip official-report OUT set (`flagged_from_availability`, `tip_source="real"`, `max_season=2024`) removed and renormalised over those who remain (all OUT: tonight = usual). A team's first game of a season (no roster within 30 days) is `no_roster`: features neutral, served A0's layer inputs at zero.
5. **Features (six, per row).** `off_arch` (categorical 0..6, 6 = unknown); `e_usual` = expected allowed points per partial possession over the usual mix (`R[a, b]` from the prior season's matchups, cells under 300 partial possessions use the league rate) minus the league rate; `delta_rate` = same over tonight's mix minus over the usual mix; `shift_l1` = L1 distance between tonight's and the usual mix (0 to 2); `retained` = share of the roster's weight still available; `def_unknown_share` = share of tonight's mix in the unknown defensive category. **Arm M** = A0 plus these in the layer: LightGBM, 4 leaves, 150 trees, learning rate 0.05, min 20 rows per leaf, seed 0, target = actual minus A0's mean, trained on rows of strictly earlier months only (2023 rows are in the pool for 2024); the served grid is rescaled by `lambda = (mean + prediction) / mean`, clipped to [0.5, 2] and re-served on the integer grid. No tuning.
6. **Tests.** **T1** = all rows of the season (primary). **T2** = the large-shift slice: `shift_l1 >= max(cut, 0.05)`, `cut` = the 0.90 quantile of `shift_l1` over the SELECT season's (2023) rows, fixed from 2023 and frozen before 2024 is scored (the 0.05 floor stops a tie mass at zero from being the "large shift" slice). The complement of T2 is the guard.
7. **Metric and pass (2024).** Paired integer-grid CRPS, M minus A0. **PASS for a test = pts delta <= -0.005, 95% CI excluding 0 under game, player-season and ISO-week clustering (the binding CI is the widest), BH q <= 0.10 across the declared family {T1, T2} x {pts, ast, fg3m} = 6 tests, AND top-5 player-season share of the gain <= 0.5, AND the shuffled control keeps < 50% of the gain, AND (T2 only) the complement is not degraded by more than +0.002 (point estimate; its binding upper bound is reported).** Floors ast 0.002, fg3m 0.001 secondary. The select season picks what is scored: a test whose pts point estimate on 2023 is not <= -0.005 is not scored on 2024.
8. **Controls and audits.** (a) Positive control: a planted shift on A0's stored grid, oracle arm, must clear pts in T1 under the binding CI (measured in the gate). (b) Shuffled control: defender archetype labels permuted within team-game (`permute_within`, seed 0), everything else identical. (c) Planted-future identity on the real table (the gate scales every matchup event on or after a cut day and requires the six features of earlier rows unchanged). (d) Missingness audit of every new column by `min10` bucket (the 10-08 `opp_adjusted_ridge` leak signature was a NULL pattern tracking tonight's minutes); in the eval also against tonight's realised minutes. (e) Knockouts, descriptive: drop the unknown category, drop the three shift features (usual-only), drop `off_arch`.
9. **Explanations.** Each scored row can carry a short reason string, built by `archetype_matchup_rule.reason_string` from numbers already computed; reasons never feed any score and are not evaluated here.
10. **What changes in production: nothing.** A PASS is a candidate for a live shadow in the 2026-27 forward log and, on Devin's word, one logged 2025 touch (this rule contains none). A null closes the matchup idea (see 'Relation to T505').

## Known-at
* `player_game_matchups` is **post-game** data of its own game. A game's row enters a feature of a LATER game only: `decayed_asof` uses events strictly before the query day; `roster_candidates` uses the team's previous games strictly before the day; the prior-season tables use the prior season only. Unit test with a planted future, repeated on the real table in the gate.
* OUT flags: the usable pre-tip official-report set as of 60 minutes before the real scheduled tip (`tip_source="real"`); box-score absence is never used; a flagged player counts only if his last known team before tonight is tonight's opponent.
* Archetypes, `A`, `pi`, `R`, league rate: prior season only. Position and height from `players_static`.
* Missingness: every feature is defined for every row (explicit unknown category, explicit `no_roster` neutral values); no NaN cell is allowed (a gate check). Each neutral value is a function of games BEFORE tonight and of the report, never of tonight's minutes.

* **Detectable effect** = the binding (widest of game, player-season, week) 95% half-width of the null-noise contrast (an arm that shifts lambda by an independent exp(0.05 z) per player-season while truth equals A0; `role_prior_salary_eval.noise_arm`, reused unchanged, 2000 draws, seed 0) on the real test rows of A0's stored grid; **bar: <= 0.005 for pts**, the T505 bar and the practical floor. The 80%-power minimum detectable effect (half-width x 2.8/1.96) is reported, descriptive. Half-recovery and oracle simulations at sd 0.10 and 0.20 reported for pts; noise arm for ast and fg3m.

* Also reported: planted-future identity; positive control; row sets identical across stats; no NaN cell; the missingness audit (neutral share of each new column by `min10` bucket; a gap >= 0.30 between `min10 < 10` and `min10 >= 28` is flagged); `no_roster` share; share of games with a usable report; distribution of `shift_l1`.
* **Data capture audit (non-negotiable 8, second half).** Same as T505 (docs/prereg/MATCHUP_AVAILABILITY.md, 'Data capture audit'): matchups 2022, 2023, 2024 in `nba.duckdb`; `data/history/nba_history.duckdb` holds none for 2019-2021 (counted again in this gate's `capture_audit`); 2025 and later excluded by rule. Repairable gap and its cost: backfill 2019-2021 matchups (about 1,230 requests per season, hours, endpoint availability unverified); it lengthens the history for archetypes but adds no 2023/2024 rows, so it cannot rescue a power failure on those seasons. ## Playbook check (docs/MODEL_PLAYBOOK.md; class: GBM residual layer, plus a categorical table input)
1. **Information or capacity?** Information in principle (who is OUT on the opposing side is a pre-tip fact the production model does not use for defenders); capacity is small (4 leaves, 6 features). Honest reading: A0 already holds the scorer's recency and usage, team-level defense was null (T434-T435, T439), most prop error is minutes, and a 5-to-7-category mix averages away exactly the identity detail a primary-defender story depends on. Expected information gain near zero.
2. **Enough data for the class?** A 4-leaf tree on ~27k rows is fine for fitting; the limit is the number of player-seasons (~500) the clustered CI is made of, which is why the gate is a power gate. k-means with k = 6 on 300-500 players per season is thin but unsupervised and fixed.
3. **Valid test?** Real-tip gating inherited from A0 and the OUT set; integer support for every arm; planted-future identity (unit and real data); missingness audited; shuffled control (a gain that survives permuted defender archetypes is a generic residual shrink, not matchups); positive control and power stated before any null; same rows, calibration and window as A0.
4. **Lineage.** T505 (stopped at the gate, same data); M3A archetype matchup (T056-T059; never properly tested, no CI) and lineup archetype mix T042 (real but tiny); team-level DEFENSE_CONTEXT null (T434-T435, T439); `opp_adjusted_ridge` leaked through a NULL pattern (hence the explicit unknown category and the audit); RAPM / on-off and usage redistribution tied or lost; T500 taught the three-way clustering, top-5 guard and positive-control habits, inherited. **New here:** the categorical expected-matchup mix on all rows, defender utilization as weights.
5. **Scored against:** CRPS of the whole distribution on all rows and on the large-shift slice, not a line; a CRPS gain has not reached a betting line before (T477). Any line analysis is a new id.

## What each choice protects against
* **Categorical mix on all rows (T1):** per-identity primary-defender slices had a few hundred to a couple of thousand rows and no power (T505). The categorical layer is Devin's answer: every row has a defined expected matchup.
* **Unknown as its own category + `no_roster` neutral values:** a NULL that tracks role is the 10-08 leak fingerprint; an explicit category can be audited and knocked out.
* **Prior-season-only tables and clusters; strictly-before decayed minutes:** the tables built on the target season, or a defender's minutes including tonight, would be the outcome.
* **Lift (A / pi) instead of raw A:** raw A mixes "who exists" with "who guards whom"; weighting by the opponent's roster already supplies "who exists".
* **Roster window (10 games, 30 days) and MIN_W 5:** a defender traded away, on a long injury or in garbage time is not tonight's defender; fixed, not tuned.
* **T2 cut from the select season, floor 0.05:** a cut set on the report season would be tuned to its outcomes; a zero-tie mass would make the "large shift" slice the no-shift rows.
* **Shuffled control within team-game:** if permuting defender archetypes keeps the gain, the gain is not about who defends.
* **Three-way clustering, binding = widest; top-5 guard:** game-clustered CIs were anti-conservative in T500; five players carrying the gain was T500's failure.
* **Gate before freeze, thresholds in code:** F9 and F12 could not pass their own gates.

## Kill criteria
* Gate FAIL: not freezable as drafted; recorded; the matchup idea is closed (one follow-up used).
* Select season (2023): a test whose pts point estimate is not <= -0.005 is dropped before 2024 is scored.
* Any of: planted-future identity fails; shuffled control keeps >= 50% of the gain; top-5 share > 0.5; a missingness pattern (a flagged column) that predicts tonight's minutes AND carries the gain in the knockout; T2 complement degraded > +0.002. Each voids the test, not just the claim.
* 2024 clears only under game clustering: FAIL_CLUSTER (a null for claim purposes).
