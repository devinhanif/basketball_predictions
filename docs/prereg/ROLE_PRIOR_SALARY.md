## The rule in ten lines (as it would be frozen if the gate allowed it)
1. **Rows.** The A0 row set of ODDS_HISTORY: `reports/odds_history/model_rows_{2023,2024}_{pts,reb,ast,fg3m}.parquet` (production out-of-fold forecasts, played rows, career `n_prior >= 5`), 27,619 / 27,583 rows per stat, identical row sets across stats (T500 gate). Select 2023, report 2024; season 2025 is never loaded; every SQL carries `season <= 2024`.
2. **Cohort (movers, not rookies).** A **mover** has a regular-season game in S-1, his last regular-season team of S-1 differs from his first regular-season team of S, and the row's team is that first team; **affected rows** are movers' games with `games_with_team < K`, **K = 10**; K = 5 reported, never selected between. Rookies are NOT in the cohort: a rookie on a rookie-scale deal has a salary set by his draft slot, which the existing draft-slot prior already uses (`nba/ingest/players_static.py`), so salary adds nothing for him. Returners after a gap and in-season arrivals are excluded (as T500).
3. **Salary facts.** Basketball-Reference team-season pages (`data/raw/bref_salaries_2026-10-21/teams`, research use only, never committed): one row per (team, season, player) with the LARGEST dollar value among his lines on that page (10-day and repeated lines are separate contracts); `alive` = he is on the same page's roster table; **dead money** = a dollar row for a player not on that roster table. Blank dollar lines are kept as a category `unpriced` (see 'Blank is information'). Season map: BR end-year = repo season + 1 (repo 2023 = BR 2024).
4. **Features (all as-of; none reads a game).** `cap_share = salary / that season's cap`; `payroll_share = salary / team payroll` (all lines incl. dead money); `salary_rank` within the team among alive rows (1 = highest); `tax_state` in {under_tax, over_tax, over_apron1, over_apron2} from the team payroll against that season's tax line and aprons; `over_tax_by_cap_share`; `contract_class` (categorical, inferred: minimum, rookie_scale, low_exception, mid_exception, rotation_salary, near_max, max_range, from cap-share bands and years of service; thresholds in `role_prior_salary_rule.py::contract_class`, declared, never tuned); `age` at 1 October; prior-season minutes per game. Cap, tax and apron constants: table in 'Cap, tax and apron constants'.
5. **Role model (S1's source).** LightGBM, 4 leaves, 150 trees, learning rate 0.05, min 20 rows per leaf, seed 0, `tax_state` and `contract_class` as categorical; the data decide interactions with age and prior-season minutes (Devin's dead-weight request): one tree model with all features, not a linear salary term. Targets: the player-season's minutes per game and per-minute rates of pts, reb, ast, fg3m. Fit on **non-mover** player-seasons (first team of S = last team of S-1, alive salary rows, >= 10 games), so the prior is not a fit on the cohort. **Which season it is fit on** is an open decision for Devin (see 'Decisions'): the brief says the select season (2023); the 2023 fit applied to 2023 movers uses a full season of non-mover outcomes to score October games (a mild future-information exposure E1, shared by the placebo); the report season 2024 is scored with the frozen 2023 fit (clean). A lagged-fit sensitivity (fit on S-1 non-movers) is reported only.
6. **Arms.** **A0** production as stored (reproduced to 1e-9: 8 of 8 cells, measured). **S1** = A0's grid rescaled by `lambda = lam_blend(role_value, base_value, g, K)`: `((1 - w') base + w' role) / base`, `w' = 0.30 (1 - g/K)`, clipped to [0.5, 2], applied to minutes (base `min10`, role = predicted mpg) and then to rates (base A0's rate, role = predicted per-minute rate), re-served on the integer grid `ceil(q - 0.5)`; rows with `g >= K`, no salary line, or non-finite inputs are served exactly as A0 (identity); a row whose only contract information is on ANOTHER team's page carries an `other_page` flag and uses the cap share only (rank and payroll share set to the neutral class). The constant 0.30 is declared, not tuned. **S2** = T500's A2 (preseason minutes and rates) applied first, then the S1 blend on the remainder; reported against A0 and against A2 alone (does salary add to the preseason evidence). **PL** (placebo) = S1 with salary shares and ranks permuted within team-season among alive rows (seed 0, one draw). 
7. **Metric and verdict.** Integer-support CRPS (199-point grid) for every arm; primary contrast **S1 - A0 on the cohort rows with a usable contract line, per stat, BH over the 4 stats within a season**; floors (CRPS units) pts 0.005, reb 0.003, ast 0.002, fg3m 0.001. CI: 95% percentile bootstrap (2000 draws, seed 0, `nba/props/metrics.py::_clustered_boot_means`) clustered by **game, by player-season and by week; the binding CI is the widest of the three**. A stat clears a season when point <= -floor, binding-CI upper < 0, BH p (binding clustering) <= 0.05, **and the top-5 player-share guard holds** (no pass if five player-seasons carry more than 50% of the gain). Words as T500: PASS (pts clears both seasons and the guards), PASS_OTHER_STAT, PLACEBO_TOO (PL keeps >= 50% of S1's gain), FAIL_GUARD (top-5, harm, or a stat harmed with CI lower > 0), FAIL_ONE_SEASON, FAIL_NO_GAIN (`research/eval/role_prior_salary_eval.py::verdict`, tested).
8. **Positive control (an invalid test is not a null).** Before any null is recorded the harness must show it can move: a planted per-player-season role shift (sd of log lambda 0.20) in outcomes drawn from the stored A0 grid, with the oracle arm, must clear pts under the binding CI on the real cohort rows (measured in the gate: it does in both seasons, point -0.19 / -0.20, so the harness sees a very large shift). The smallest effect the CIs exclude is stated beside the floor (MDE below). A "salary adds nothing" reading is allowed only with: coverage and join checked (done in the gate), the control passed, and the whole-class knockout (S1 with the contract features zeroed = identity) equal to A0 to the bit.
9. **Secondary, no verdict word.** S1 - A0 on non-cohort rows (stayers' first K games; if stayers gain as much, the mechanism is not the new team); S2 - A2; K = 5; the `other_page` rows; slices by `tax_state` and `contract_class`; the lagged fit; S1 on the dead-weight set (movers whose salary rank and prior-season minutes rank disagree by more than 5 places).
10. **What changes in production: nothing.** A PASS is a candidate for a live shadow in the 2026-27 forward log and, on Devin's word, one logged 2025 touch (this rule contains none). A null is closed: no variant K, band, weight or season is tried afterwards; one pre-registered follow-up id at most.

## Known-at
* **Cap and tax lines** are published before the season (July 1). **Salary lines** are contract facts known at signing. The BR page carries no signing date, so a line's stamp is the player's **first regular-season game with that team in that season** (`known_at`), a conservative upper bound (the contract exists before he plays); a row may use a line only at or after its stamp (`known_at_ok`, tested; violations in the gate: 0 and 0).
* **Team payroll and tax state are the END-OF-SEASON page as pulled on 2026-10-21, not the payroll at tip of the row's game.** Players traded away mid-season are not on the page; players acquired later are. This is exposure E2, listed and not repaired: a mid-season trade changes the team's payroll and tax state after the cohort's first games. Repair, with cost: NBA.com transaction logs (free) would give signing and trade dates, about 150-300 requests, a separate source and ToS check.
* A pulled salary can differ from the figure at signing only by restructuring or conversion (two-way to standard); unmeasured.
* Exposure E1 (same-season fit of the role model) is the one place where a fit uses outcomes later than the scored rows; listed under 'Decisions'.

## Dead money and dead weight (Devin, ~12:05 CDT)
* **Dead money.** A dollar row for a player not on the same page's roster table (waived, stretched, bought out, or traded after the season per BR's table). Counted in the team payroll and its tax/apron state (the CBA counts it), excluded from the within-team ranks and from the role model's fit, given no role. Measured per BR year: 59 / 62 / 52 rows (2023 / 2024 / 2025), 313.7M / 258.2M / 178.6M dollars, 7.0% / 5.3% / 3.4% of league dollars.
* **Dead-weight contracts.** The tree model sees salary share jointly with age and prior-season minutes, so the data decide how much a big contract means for an old or barely-used player; no hand-set discount. Measured descriptively on non-movers (the only place a role is observed): Spearman of -salary rank vs minutes per game 0.72 overall, 0.57 where prior-season minutes are below the median, 0.89 for age >= 31 (n = 36; the old players on big deals ARE the starters in that season: the dead-weight case is rare at this n, not common). Movers whose salary rank and prior-season minutes rank (within the new team's alive roster) disagree by more than 5 places: **13 of 94 (2023), 8 of 79 (2024)**; movers who were OUT the whole prior season: **0 and 0** (a mover has a prior-season game by definition); alive salary rows league-wide OUT the whole prior season (non-rookie, zero S-1 games): 14 (2023) and 15 (2024), 24.0M and 45.4M dollars. The dead-weight slice is therefore about 10 players a season: far below any power.

## Blank is information (a missingness finding)
BR prints a blank dollar value for two-way, exhibit-10 and unpriced players (about 150 lines a year, 110-155 players). On non-movers the median minutes per game are 9.2 (2023) and 10.5 (2024) for blank-listed players against 25.6 and 24.5 for dollar-listed: **a blank is a fringe-role marker, not missing at random**, so it is a category (`unpriced`), and cohort coverage is reported both ways. This is the NULL-pattern class of leak (BEST_PRACTICES), so the missingness audit is part of the rule: the identity-served rows (no contract line at all) are reported with their share.

## Playbook check (MODEL_PLAYBOOK.md; class: GBM residual layer as a fixed blend, plus quantile layer)
1. **Information or capacity?** Information: contract facts known at signing that production cannot see directly (production's inputs for a mover are his games for another team). No capacity is added beyond a 4-leaf role model on non-mover rows; the placebo (permuted shares) and the whole-class knockout separate role information from a generic shrink. The honest alternative reading: the contract is priced off the player's production, which A0 already holds, so the incremental information may be near zero.
2. **Enough data for the class?** The tree is fit on about 250 non-mover player-seasons (Spearman set: 251 / 264) with two categorical features of 4 and 7 levels and interactions: thin for a tree (this is why it has 4 leaves). The scoring slice is the limit (power above), not the fit.
3. **Valid test?** Real-tip gating and played-rows handling are inherited from A0 (stored OOF); integer support for every arm; known-at stamp per line (violations 0); planted-future: the features are a pure function of the salary table and roster (tested), and the one outcome-later input is E1; missingness audited (blank is a fringe marker, other-page rows flagged, identity rows reported); a placebo that cannot leak the future (permutation within team-season uses the same page); a comparator missing information production had: none; power for the floor: FAILS (stated); positive control: passes at a 20% shift only (stated).
4. **Lineage.** PRESEASON_ROLE_PRIOR (T500): same cohort and K, a blend of projected minutes and rates, A0 comparator; FAIL_ONE_SEASON, then the post-result attack found 5 of 115 players carried 98.6% of the pts gain and game-clustered CIs were anti-conservative. **Carried forward:** the cohort, K = 10 with K = 5, integer CRPS, A0 reproduction, the placebo idea, no 2025 touch. **Fixed:** player-season and week clustering with the widest binding, the top-5 share guard, a positive control, a power statement under all three clusterings, the missingness audit. F9b (draft-slot rookie prior, T186-T192): not tied or beaten for rookies, which is why rookies are out. RECENT_ROLE / PI_RECENT_ROLE / MINUTES_HAZARD: adding role information to a fitted tree tied; not re-run. USAGE redistribution and sim props: lost.
5. **Scored against:** CRPS of the whole distribution on the cohort, not a line; any line analysis is a new id. A CRPS gain has not reached the line before (T477).

| cohort = movers, not rookies | rookie-scale salary is set by draft slot, which the slot prior already uses; salary would add a duplicate, not information |
| shares of the season's cap and of payroll, plus rank, not raw dollars | the cap moving 123.7M to 140.6M turns one dollar figure into three different roles; a model learning the calendar |
| tax state and apron state as a category | the CBA changes who a roster spot can go to (a minimum signing on a second-apron team is a fringe role); treating a 5M and a 40M payroll slack alike |
| contract class as a categorical inferred from bands | forcing a linear dollar effect where the structure is categorical (minimum / exception / max) |
| dead money counted in payroll, excluded from ranks and the fit | dead money inflating payroll ranks and giving a waived player a role |
| tree with salary x age x prior-season minutes, not a hand-set discount | a bench veteran's big contract pushing his projection up (dead weight); a discount tuned by eye |
| blank dollar line kept as a category | the NULL-pattern leak: dropping blanks removes exactly the fringe players, and the remaining sample looks easier |
| line known-at = first game with the team | using a contract before it existed |
| fit on non-mover rows | a prior fit on the cohort is a fit, not a prior |
| fixed weight 0.30, faded over K | a tuned blend on 2023 is the variant-chasing the nulls taught us not to do |
| three clusterings, widest binds | the T500 anti-conservative game-clustered CIs (an effect constant within a player) |
| top-5 player-share guard | five players carrying the gain (T500: 98.6%) |
| placebo with permuted shares; whole-class knockout | "any shrink helps" and a test that could not have moved |
| positive control | recording a null from a harness that could not see an effect |
| 2025 never queried; salary 2025-26/2026-27 counted only | touching the frozen holdout |
| floor not moved | a post-hoc floor chosen to be reachable |
