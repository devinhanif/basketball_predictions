## Hypothesis
Running the same T-30 logic with the information cutoff moved to tip minus 15 minutes keeps most of the T-30 information gain (starter status drives
minutes) while covering many more games, so the conditional gain times the logged share is larger than the T-30 arm's. Nothing about the model changes.

## Fixed design (what "same" means)
1. **Model and features.** Identical to `props_context_residual_t30`: same `t30_*` features (`nba.props.lineup_features.T30_FEATURES`), same fitted
   model cache (training does not depend on the cutoff, so no second fit), same roster rule (`official` before 2026-11-04, `recent` after), same
   `slate_report_outs` (a newer injury report is bundled in exactly as for T-30; the contrast is "everything knowable at T-15 vs at T-60").
   `model_name = props_context_residual_t15`, `version = ctxres-v1-t15`, `comparison_only = true`. Frozen like any shadow arm (FORWARD_PREREG rule 9).
2. **Lineup rule.** The one in `nba/lineups/store.py::dedup_snapshot_rows`, DECISIONS 2026-10-11 20:32: a team is Confirmed if any of its rows is
   Confirmed; a starter only from winning-status rows; the gate is both teams Confirmed with exactly 5 starters each, else skip with the same reason strings.
3. **Cutoff.** The snapshot used is the latest one with `fetched_at` strictly before tip minus 15 min (T-30: minus 30). The decision `made_at` must be
   before tip. Real tip times only (`game_tips.source = status_text`/real). With a 5-minute tick the effective information age is 15-20 minutes.
4. **Table.** `forward_t15_decisions` (same columns as `forward_t30_decisions`; `cutoff` = tip minus 15). One row per game, idempotent. Reasons are
   the same strings plus `run_after_tipoff` (counted, see rule 9), so a missed deadline is never confused with "not confirmed".
5. **Population and pairing.** FORWARD_PREREG section 1 rules 1-3 apply verbatim (regular season 2026, unit `(stat, game_id, player_id)`, key
   intersection with DNP counted, comparator = latest production `props_context_residual` row with `made_at` <= tip - 60). The T-15 eligibility analogue
   of the 2026-10-09 amendment: a `_t15` row is eligible iff `made_at < tip` AND `snapshot_fetched_at < tip - 15 min`.
6. **Stats and metrics.** Same as the T-30 arm: pts, reb, ast, fg3m, primary = paired integer-support CRPS delta (arm minus comparator, negative better),
   pinball19 secondary and labelled, floor <= -0.005, date-clustered paired percentile bootstrap, B = 10,000, `default_rng(2026)`, ratio of sums,
   two-sided p floored at 1/(B+1), the same guards (a)-(d) with (d) read as snapshot `fetched_at < tip - 15 min` for 100% of rows, the same slices,
   and the same direction check (an estimate more negative than the T-30 backtest upper end: pts < -0.041, reb < -0.018, ast < -0.0089 = suspected leak,
   audit first).
7. **Timing measurement (descriptive, rule-fixed, as FORWARD_PREREG 2.1).** At every checkpoint: lead minutes of both-fives-Confirmed (deciles, n,
   poll interval beside it), shares confirmed before T-30 and before T-15 with Wilson CIs, game-level open-decision share, announced-vs-box skew.
8. **Logged share.** `logged / (logged + skipped)` over games with a decision, Wilson 95%. Operational gate for any promote-candidate: Wilson lower
   bound >= 0.70 (same constant as T-30). Reasons reported: not_confirmed, starters_not_5, starters_block_expected, no_snapshot, no_model_rows,
   run_after_tipoff.
9. **Deadline risk is part of the arm.** The T-15 run has half the slack of T-30 (it may wait up to 600 s for `nba.duckdb`). A game whose decision
   starts at or after tip is `run_after_tipoff`: it counts as skipped (denominator and numerator of the share), never dropped.

## Market comparator (the T-15 price)
Comparator price: `live_book_odds` rows with `snapshot_kind = 't15'` (window [12.5, 17.5] min before tip, `configs/odds_live.yaml`, `received_at <
tipoff` by construction), per book as stored, main two-sided half-point line (point, over and under both present), `implied_prob` (proportional
de-vig) of the over. Our probability at that line: `P(Y >= ceil(point))` from the T-15 row's stored `p_ge_full` (integer support, same convention as the
CRPS). Leg metric: log loss of the over event, paired (our T-15 vs price) on the same legs, date-clustered bootstrap as rule 6, outcome = over event,
pushes impossible on half-points, players OUT at decision time dropped and counted (the book voids them). This is DESCRIPTIVE with a TRIPWIRE only:
no efficacy claim, no bet, no edge statement; our T-60 model and the price are both published beside it. Reading rule: the price is captured up to
2.5 minutes after our cutoff and the book may know more than our snapshot (sub-poll-gap news); a price win is therefore expected and uninformative.
The one reading that matters: the T-15 minus T-60 improvement in our log loss against the same t15 price (paired, same legs), is it negative with a
CI above the floor of -0.005? Reported only; no decision. If `t15` is dropped from the active schedule (`extended: true` entries drop when the
monthly estimate exceeds half the plan) this section is voided and says so; it does not change the CRPS rule.

## Looks, family, minimum n (the gate, checked against real data)
- **Family.** m = 4 tests: T15 x {pts, reb, ast, fg3m}, BH within the family, per-look two-sided alpha 0.0182 (Pocock constant for K = 4, kept
  although only 3 efficacy looks exist: conservative, stated). The T-30 family (m = 9) is not changed and not merged: the two arms share nearly all
  inputs, so claims about both are not independent and a joint claim is never made. The head-to-head T-15 minus T-30 on games logged by BOTH (paired,
  same rows) is descriptive, no decision weight, reported beside every look.
- **Looks, in distinct dates with a paired scored row for the arm-stat (rule 11 of FORWARD_PREREG).** L14 and L30 are kill-only interims (below). Efficacy
  looks L60, L120, END; same minimums as the T-30 arm: pts and reb 60 dates and 5,000 pairs (first look L60); ast and fg3m 120 dates and 10,000 pairs
  (first look L120). Outcomes at an eligible look exactly as FORWARD_PREREG 5.5 (promote-candidate, DROP, keep shadowing), with the logged-share
  gate in rule 8. A promote-candidate is a recommendation for red-team review plus Devin's decision, nothing more. A stat that fails at END is not retested.
- **Where the minimum n lands.** About 165 regular-season dates over about 175 calendar days (FORWARD_PREREG section 6), 7.5 games per date, so:
  L14 about 2026-11-03; L30 about 2026-11-20; L60 about 2026-12-22 (the week of 12-21); L120 about 2027-02-24; END within 7 days of the regular-season end.
  Pairs check (rule: the gate must be passable): backtest density 27,583 pairs / 212 dates = 130 per date; at L60 with logged share s the expected pairs
  are 60 x 130 x s = 7,800 s, which is >= 5,000 only if s >= 0.64. If s is below 0.64 the pair condition binds later than L60 and the look is "recorded,
  keep shadowing" until it holds (the same behavior as the T-30 table). At L120 the 10,000-pair minimum needs s >= 0.64 as well. A share this low
  would also fail the 0.70 operational gate, so the arm would be dropped anyway; the gate is consistent. Power: the T-30 SE table inflated for s = 0.85
  (pts .0064 / reb .0029 / ast .0018 / fg3m .0009 at 60 / 60 / 120 / 120 dates) applies unchanged for s near 0.85, better than T-30's own planning share;
  planning effects are the T-30 values (pts -0.023, reb -0.009, ast -0.0045), not haircut: T-15 reads a later snapshot than T-30 and later GTD resolution if anything raises the gain.
- **Share gate on the sample, reached early.** Wilson lower bound >= 0.70 needs about n = 100 games at a true share of 0.80 (n = 61 gives LB 0.669, n =
  100 gives 0.711), i.e. about the first two weeks, L14 (about 2026-11-03).

## Kill-only interim (no efficacy claim before L60)
- **L14 (monitoring):** pipeline fidelity (100% `made_at < tip`, 100% `snapshot_fetched_at < tip - 15`), direction check, logged share, deadline misses.
- **L30 (kill-only):** the arm is DROPPED (stop logging, record the result) iff any of: (a) a leak or guard failure not fixed by a documented,
  result-blind repair; (b) the point delta > +0.005 with the 98.18% date-clustered CI lower bound > 0 on any stat with >= 25 dates (harm);
  (c) the logged-share Wilson UPPER bound < 0.50 at >= 60 decided games (the arm is not buying coverage over T-30, so it is not worth the slot);
  (d) `run_after_tipoff` > 10% of decisions (the deadline is not reachable on this hardware). No L30 outcome can be a promote-candidate,
  and no outcome other than these can drop the arm.
- A T-15 result cannot trigger a change to the T-30 arm, and vice versa. If both arms reach a promote-candidate at the same look, the choice between
  them is a Devin decision made with the head-to-head and the logged shares; it is not made by this rule.

## What each choice protects against
- New arm id, new table, new counters: the frozen T-30 rule cannot be edited; a changed rule is a new arm (its own header), so no retroactive tuning.
- Same model cache and features: any T-15 vs T-30 difference is information timing only, not a refit or a feature choice.
- Strict `fetched_at < cutoff`: the only leak path in a timing arm is a snapshot newer than the stated cutoff.
- Counting `run_after_tipoff` as skipped: otherwise a slow box inflates the logged share by dropping the games it could not do.
- Separate family of 4 and no joint claim: two near-duplicate arms must not buy extra power or extra chances.
- Kill-only interim: the opening weeks are the most variable (preseason showed a 778-minute collector gap); stopping early is allowed, claiming early is not.
- Price comparison descriptive: the book prices up to 2.5 min after our cutoff and its edge on post-cutoff news is real, so a win or loss against it is not skill.
