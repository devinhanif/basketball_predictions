## What the gate found, before any rule text (the facts that shaped it)
1. **The tail is thin, and a word is reachable at q >= 0.60 but only a weak one at q >= 0.65.** After the map's own fit-size rule, DraftKings has **7,405 scored tail legs (q_cal >= 0.60), 1,746 of them at q >= 0.65**, pooled over 2023 (3,499 / 893) and 2024 (3,906 / 853). The synthetic-label half-width of a gap is **0.0116** at q >= 0.60 (n 7,405) and **0.0215** at q >= 0.65 (n 1,746), before real clustering; the only real clustered width we have (T495, DK 2024, n 853) is 0.034 against a binomial 0.031, so the projected real half-width at q >= 0.65 is about **0.024** and at q >= 0.60 about **0.013**. Pinnacle has 3,060 scored tail legs and 565 at q >= 0.65 (half-width 0.038 at q >= 0.65): direction only.
2. **CAL alone passes a gap check at q >= 0.60 about a third to a half of the time under the T495 truth** (31% under the smooth truth, 48% under the step truth), so "q >= 0.60 gap within the margin" cannot be the only gate: it does not separate a repaired tail from the original. The discriminating test is the q >= 0.65 gap, where CAL's interval excludes 0 in 96% of draws under the smooth truth (94% step) and only 3% under a calibrated truth.
3. **The log-loss co-primary is under-powered, and the rule says so.** The expected log-loss gain of a perfect map over CAL is **0.0015 per tail leg at q >= 0.60** (0.0054 at q >= 0.65; 0.00013 per leg over all legs in the fitted blocks). The paired 95% half-width is about 0.0013 (q >= 0.60) and 0.005 (q >= 0.65). Against the floor -0.0005 with p < 0.025, a perfect map passes 64% of draws and the fitted hinge 55% (smooth truth), 46% / 17% under the step truth. So the superiority half of the log-loss test is reachable about half the time even if the correction is right; its failure is read as "not shown", and a non-inferiority half (no harm) carries the pass.
4. **The tail is not "all stats".** Scored DraftKings tail legs by stat: fg3m 5,639, reb 1,335, ast 425, **pts 6**; by side: **under 5,615, over 1,790**. Points legs almost never reach q >= 0.60, so nothing here says anything about points; stat words are possible for fg3m and reb only (ast 425 is just above the 400-leg testing bar). This matters for MEDIAN_BIAS (below).
5. **Why one season does not decide, and which season is exposed.** The CAL map is fit by month on every earlier leg, so 2023 legs before January 2024 are identity (DraftKings December 2023: 1,178 tail legs dropped; Pinnacle December to March: 1,439 dropped). The two seasons are both A0- and CAL-exposed (T495 reported the tail in both). See "Seasons and exposure".

## The rule in ten lines
1. **Legs.** The CAL-served legs exactly as PARLAY_JOINT_CAL built them: DraftKings and Pinnacle T-60 main-line legs of seasons 2023 and 2024, played, with both prices posted, outside CAL's identity blocks; the served side is the side with `p_cal >= 0.5`, `q = max(p_cal, 1 - p_cal)` (`parlay_joint_cal_eval.load_cal` and `book_legs`, which call LINE_CALIBRATION's frozen `walk_forward`, CAL a5441242). The tail is `q >= 0.60` (**KNEE**); the high tail is `q >= 0.65` (**HIGH**).
2. **A0** is raw production on the same leg: the shown A0 probability is the probability A0 assigns to CAL's served side (`p_model` if that side is over, `1 - p_model` if under), clipped to [0.5/199, 1 - 0.5/199]. **CAL** shows `q`.
3. **TAIL_H (the one arm that can issue a word).** Per calendar-month block of `game_date`, fit on every earlier tail leg of the same book (pooled over stats and sides), weight `0.5 ** (age_days / 365)`: an offset logistic regression `logit P(hit) = logit(q) + beta * max(0, logit(q) - logit(0.60))`, ridge 1.0 on beta, beta clipped to [-0.95, +1.0]. Shown probability `q' = sigmoid(logit(q) + beta * max(0, logit(q) - logit(0.60)))` for `q >= 0.60`, and `q' = q` below. Identity (`q' = q`) when the block has fewer than **1,000** earlier tail legs. The map is continuous at the knee and monotone, so it never reverses the order of two legs. It has one learned number.
4. **TAIL_I (label only).** The same blocks and weights: weighted pool-adjacent-violators isotonic regression of hit on `q` over the earlier tail legs, linear interpolation between knots, floored at 0.60 (`q' = max(iso(q), 0.60)`), identity below the knee and in identity blocks. It answers "what if the shape is free"; it issues no word (it is much noisier at 1,000 to 8,000 fit legs; see the rehearsal).
5. **References, no word:** SHIFT (`q + g`, g the weighted mean of hit minus q over the fit tail legs) and CONST (the weighted tail hit rate), the strong simple baselines a one-parameter hinge must be read against.
6. **Rows scored** are the tail legs (`q >= 0.60`) in blocks that are not identity for TAIL_H, the same rows for every arm. Rows dropped are counted, never hidden. Legs below the knee are unchanged by construction (asserted every run).
7. **Primary metrics (Devin).** (a) **Calibration gap at q >= 0.60:** `G60 = mean(hit) - mean(shown)` over the scored tail legs; (b) **log loss** co-primary: `L60 = mean log loss(TAIL_H) - mean log loss(CAL)` on the same rows, plus the same two on the `q >= 0.65` band (**G65, L65**). Comparators are CAL (frozen) and A0. DraftKings is the primary book; Pinnacle is a direction check.
8. **CI and clustering.** Paired 95% percentile bootstrap, B = 5,000, **game** resamples (seed 2026) with the **date** resamples (seed 2027) reported beside it; the decision interval is the union (min of the lowers, max of the uppers) and the p-value is the larger of the two; seasons are strata, one matrix per season shared by every statistic (`research.eval.alt_harness_common.make_boot`); the estimator is the ratio of resampled per-cluster sums.
9. **Walk-forward, one pooled pass over 2023-24**, every block fit on strictly earlier blocks only; a per-season replication check (P4).
10. **The confirmatory test is the 2026-27 live log**, under a separate rule id (below); no 2025 touch, ever, under this id.

## Checks and words
Let X = TAIL_H. All DraftKings, scored tail legs, unless stated.
- **P0 (the problem exists on these rows; a precondition):** CAL's own G65 has its decision-interval upper bound below 0. If not, the word is **NO_TAIL_PROBLEM** and no repair is judged. (Under a calibrated truth P0 fails 97% of the time; under the T495 truths it passes 94% to 96%.)
- **P1 (gap at q >= 0.60):** `|G60(X)| <= 0.015` and the decision interval inside [-0.03, +0.03] (LINE_CALIBRATION's P4 margin and ALT_LADDER_HIST's CALIBRATED margin).
- **P2 (log loss, two halves).** **P2n (no harm, required):** `L60 <= 0` (point) and the interval upper bound `<= +0.0005`. **P2s (superiority, decides the strong word):** `L60 <= -0.0005` with one-sided p < 0.025 (larger of the two clusterings).
- **P3 (the high tail is honest):** the decision interval of `G65(X)` contains 0 and `-0.02 <= G65(X) <= +0.03` (point). This is the T495 finding turned into a test, and the same test flags CAL at 96% power under the smooth truth; absence of detection is informative at that power, and it is a weak word (it cannot show the gap is small, only that it is not detectably negative at a half-width of about 0.024).
- **P4 (both seasons):** in each of 2023 and 2024, `L60 <= 0` (point), `|G60| <= 0.03` (point) and `G65 >= -0.03` (point). P4 is a sign-and-band replication, not a held-out one: the 2024 fits use 2023 legs.
- **P5 (label, never a pass): shuffled-label null.** SH_k (k = 0..49) is TAIL_H refit after permuting the hit labels among the fit tail legs of each block (`default_rng([1000 + k, block])`). It is reported with its mean and sd and the calibration slope of hit on shown probability (game-clustered). **It is inert as a guard** in the rehearsal: a correct fix and a flat tail both match their shuffled arm (smooth truth: -0.00096 against shuffled -0.00107; flat: -0.00804 against -0.00807), because any shrink toward the base rate improves an over-confident tail (the T339 and T495 lesson). So P5 carries no information claim; the information evidence is the slope, reported, and nothing in the word says "adds information".
- **P6 (a second book agrees):** Pinnacle (its own tail fit): `L60 <= 0` (point) and `|G60| <= 0.03` (point).
- **P7 (no stat or side harmed):** over the stats with at least 400 scored tail legs (fg3m, reb, ast; pts has 6), `TAIL_H - CAL` log loss has BH-adjusted (m = number tested) one-sided "worse" q >= 0.05 for every stat; and over legs and under legs each have `L60 <= +0.0005` (point).
- **Words, in this order:** **NO_TAIL_PROBLEM** if P0 fails. **TAIL_FIXES** iff P0, P1, P2n, P2s, P3, P4, P6, P7 all pass. **TAIL_GAP_FIXED_LL_NOT_SHOWN** iff P0, P1, P2n, P3, P4, P6, P7 pass and P2s fails (the tail gap is repaired in the historical rows; the log-loss improvement is not shown, and the gate says that is the likely reading even for a correct fix). **NOT_FIXED** otherwise.

**Multiplicity.** One word-bearing arm (TAIL_H) and one primary comparison per metric; the isotonic, SHIFT and CONST arms are labels, so there is no arm family to correct. BH (m = tested stats) covers the per-stat do-no-harm check in P7; the stat, side, season, month and band slices are labels. The words are one fixed-order classification of pre-declared checks; the checks are not each corrected (their conjunction is the conservative direction for a positive word). **TAIL_FIXES is a historical screen, not a finding**: see the next section.

## Seasons and exposure: what can be claimed
- **Both seasons are exposed.** 2024 is LINE_CALIBRATION's select season and the T495 attack already measured the tail in both seasons (DraftKings q >= 0.65: 2024 -0.047 [-0.082, -0.014] n 853, 2023 -0.034 [-0.062, -0.007] n 1,246; Pinnacle -0.089 and -0.064). The knee (0.60), the band (0.65), the choice of a hinge on the logit and the one-parameter form were all chosen **after** that tail was known. There is no clean historical season for this question; "select on one season, report on the next" is not available (CLAUDE.md), and the pooled walk-forward replaces it.
- **What a historical TAIL_FIXES says:** a one-parameter correction, fit only on earlier months, removes a gap that the same data defined. That is evidence the correction is estimable and stable month to month (P4); it is **not** evidence the correction generalises, and it is not an out-of-sample claim. The strongest honest sentence is "on the 2023-24 legs, a correction learned only from earlier legs would have left the confident legs within sampling error of calibrated". It is never "the panel's numbers are now accurate".
- **What a historical failure says:** more than a success, because the data were chosen to show the problem: NOT_FIXED means a hinge cannot even fit the gap it was written to fit.
- **The 2026-27 live log is the confirmatory test** (separate rule below). The historical pass earns only the right to freeze that live rule; it earns no change to the panel, which is Devin's.
- **Degrees of freedom spent after seeing the tail** (declared, so the reader can discount): the knee, HIGH, the family (hinge), beta's clip, MIN_TAIL = 1,000 and the margins. Not spent: every threshold in the checks was fixed from the gate's power numbers (labels drawn from declared truths), not from a real-label result.

## Hypothesis, null and priors
- **H1.** A walk-forward hinge correction on CAL's own legs brings the DraftKings confident-leg gap to within the margins (P1, P3) without harm (P2n, P4, P6, P7). **Null.** The hinge leaves the q >= 0.65 gap detectably negative, or fixes it by hurting something else.
- **Why I expect partial success, reasoned not measured:** T495 shows the gap rising with q (0.60 to 0.65 about -0.005, >= 0.65 about -0.04), which a hinge on the logit can follow if the gap is smooth and not if it is a step; a one-parameter map fit on 1,000 to 8,000 tail legs has little to overfit, and the 2024 fits borrow 2023 legs on the same mechanism.
- **Binary priors (stated before any result; not blind: I know the T495 gaps and the gate's power numbers).** Events are on the pooled 2023-24 scored DraftKings tail legs. P(P0 passes, CAL's G65 upper < 0) **0.85**. P(P1) **0.85**. P(P2n) **0.70**. P(P2s) **0.35**. P(P3) **0.55**. P(P4) **0.55**. P(P6, Pinnacle) **0.75**. P(P7) **0.80**. Words: NO_TAIL_PROBLEM **0.12**, NOT_FIXED **0.48**, TAIL_GAP_FIXED_LL_NOT_SHOWN **0.18**, TAIL_FIXES **0.22** (sum 1.00). Expected values with 80% intervals: CAL G65 **-0.040** (-0.060 to -0.020); TAIL_H G65 **-0.015** (-0.040 to +0.010); TAIL_H L60 **-0.0006** (-0.0015 to +0.0002); TAIL_H L65 **-0.0020** (-0.0060 to +0.0010); fitted beta (late blocks) **-0.35** (-0.7 to -0.1). P(TAIL_I L60 worse than TAIL_H, point) **0.85**. P(TOP 2-leg DraftKings ticket gap of TAIL_H closer to 0 than CAL's, point) **0.80**. ## Playbook check (docs/MODEL_PLAYBOOK.md; classes: quantile and distribution layers, linear and logistic models)
1. **Information or capacity?** Neither. It adds no pre-tip information; it re-expresses CAL's own probability on its confident side. The answer is allowed to be "this is a calibration patch on a shape error upstream" (MEDIAN_BIAS).
2. **Enough data for this class?** One learned parameter on 1,178 to 8,557 earlier tail legs per block: yes for the hinge; the isotonic arm is data-hungry and is read as a label for that reason. The class check asks for volume per slice: pooled q >= 0.60 yes (7,405), q >= 0.65 thin (1,746 pooled, 853 per season), pts none (6), ast just (425).
3. **Valid test?** Real tip gating (minimum lead 62.55 minutes), played legs only (stated limit: a sportsbook voids a DNP), integer-grid x.5 lines (binary events, no pushes), walk-forward with strictly earlier fit rows (asserted), planted-future test, game- and date-clustered CIs, comparators on identical rows. Guards that can fire or pass by chance: P2s fires only about half the time even for a correct fix (so it is not required), the shuffled null is inert (so it is a label), and the discriminating test is P3 plus the P0 precondition, which a calibrated world fails 97% of the time. Risks listed: both seasons exposed, the form chosen after the tail was seen, legs of one game and player are correlated (design effect about 1.1 measured by T495).
4. **Lineage:** above.
5. **What it scores against:** calibration and log loss of a leg's served probability, not CRPS and not money. No claim of profit; the market beats us on winners and the props edge is unproven.
Class checks: the reliability table is at the point that matters (the tail, six bins from 0.60); slices fixed in advance (stat, side, season, band).

## Kill criteria and what a result opens
- **Any word is a finding.** No re-cut of the knee, band, margins, floors, family or block rule after a result; a different map (a stat- or side-specific tail, a free isotonic, a hinge on the probability scale) is a new id.
- **One allowed follow-up:** `TAIL_LEG_CAL_LIVE` on the 2026-27 log (own id, frozen before its first scored game, gate measured on real forward counts, width-gated look date; upstream stamp per the MEDIAN_BIAS clause).
- **If TAIL_FIXES:** attacks first (shuffled labels, planted future, knockout by stat and side and month, replication by season and on Pinnacle), then the live rule. Any panel wording change ("the top picks came in about 3-5 points lower than shown") is Devin's decision, not this rule's; no number is shifted by hand. **If TAIL_GAP_FIXED_LL_NOT_SHOWN:** the same, with the log-loss gap stated. **If NOT_FIXED:** recorded; the next candidate is the root cause (MEDIAN_BIAS, a count-shape head for fg3m) or option (b) of the T495 attack (a TOP-specific joint shrink), under a new id. **If NO_TAIL_PROBLEM:** the T495 tail finding does not reproduce on the scored rows; that is itself a finding about T495.

## Unstated details fixed before any scored number
- **Block** = calendar month of `game_date` (pooled across stats and sides within a book); fit rows are earlier legs of the same book with `q >= 0.60`, from either season.
- **Leg order** is (game_date, game_id, player_id, stat), fixed by `book_legs`; ties in q (isotonic knots) are broken by index; `logit` clips at 1e-6.
- **Hinge IRLS:** Newton on beta, start 0, at most 100 iterations, tolerance 1e-12, ridge 1.0 on the sum-of-losses scale, beta clipped to [-0.95, +1.0].
- **Log loss** clips shown probabilities to [1e-6, 1 - 1e-6]; the A0 shown probability is clipped to [0.5/199, 1 - 0.5/199] first.
- **Scored rows** per block: tail legs in blocks with at least 1,000 earlier tail legs of the book; the stat test bar is 400 scored tail legs; the P7 side bar is +0.0005 (point).
- **TOP slice:** the cross-game candidate set of `parlay_joint_cal_gate.top_candidates` (each game's 3 highest-q legs), all 2- and 3-game subsets, ranked by the arm's own product, 3 per date, restricted to legs in scored blocks; independent-product tickets only; date-clustered.
- **Slopes:** OLS of hit on shown probability over the scored tail legs, game-clustered, reported for CAL and TAIL_H.
- **Scale:** light (numpy over about 9,000 tail legs, B = 5,000 by about 1,300); progress lines per book and per ten shuffled draws; no heavy.lock; no GPU.
