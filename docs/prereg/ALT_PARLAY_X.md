## Question
1. **Joint calibration and independence.** If legs from different games on one slate date are treated as independent, does the hit rate of a parlay equal the product of its legs' model P?
2. **Profit.** With the parlay priced as the product of the legs' decimal odds (Devin's stated assumption), what is the ROI at $2 per parlay, up to 10 parlays a week ($20 a week)?
3. **Break-even.** At what price multiple does a parlay of this kind break even against the claimed and the realised joint probability?

**What this can and cannot answer (review answer 6).** PROFITABLE needs a realised ROI of roughly +0.05 to +0.13 in the best cell (the reviewer's model-only simulation gives pooled ROI half-widths of 0.036 to 0.096 in the gated cells, 0.055 for 2-leg `ge70`, which is the smallest ROI the interval can resolve; the conservative date-cluster widths are 0.12 to 0.19). It is reachable only if the model's true edge exceeds the compounded margin. The likely informative outputs are the T1 gap (OVERCONFIDENT or not) and the compounded-margin break-even table, not a profit word.

## Construction (fixed now, seeded, no hand selection)
1. **One leg per unit.** A unit is (game, player, stat). Within a pool, a unit's rungs that fall in the pool are reduced to ONE rung by a seeded uniform draw (rungs sorted by (game, player, stat, point) first; `numpy.random.default_rng([2026, season, pool_index])`). Two rungs of one player-game-stat are never both legs.
2. **Cross-game, same slate date.** A parlay's legs come from distinct games with the same `game_date` (the date as stored). So no two legs share a game, and no leg is from a different slate date.
3. **Sizes 2 and 3.** For each (season, pool, size m, date): up to **20** draws; a draw picks m distinct games uniformly without replacement among that date's games with legs, then one leg uniformly within each picked game (`default_rng([2026, season, pool_index, m])`). A draw whose leg set already appeared that date is dropped (no refill), so a date yields at most 20 parlays. Dates with fewer than m games with legs yield none. Why not all combinations: a busy date has about 90 legs in the 55 pool, which gives thousands of pairs; 20 seeded draws per date cap busy dates (a date contributes min(20, the number of distinct sets it allows), so thin dates weigh less). Sampling games first and then a leg weights games equally, not legs; this is part of the rule.
4. **Price and joint probability.** D = product of the legs' decimal odds (Devin: "assume it's just odds multiplying"). Predicted joint P = product of the legs' model P. This is an **assumption of independence across games**, tested below, not a fact. A real book may price a parlay below the product or add boosts; neither is modeled.
5. **Settlement.** The parlay wins iff every leg hits (profit D - 1 per unit staked), else loses the unit (-1). No pushes (half-point lines). A void leg would shrink the parlay in practice; the construction excludes legs whose player did not play (void) before any parlay is built, which uses the played-only universe of the model rows (the same convention as ALT_LINE_70), stated as an approximation.
6. **Plan ($20 a week).** $2 per parlay, at most 10 parlays a week = $20, Monday-start week of `game_date`, no carry, winnings not restaked. Each week the plan draws up to 10 of that week's constructed parlays uniformly without replacement, R = 2,000 plan draws per season (`default_rng(2026)`). One (pool, size) cell is one plan; plans are not combined.

## Estimands, tests and CIs
- **E1par (decision): parlay ROI** = sum(profit) / n over ALL constructed parlays of a (pool, size) cell, pooled over the two seasons (resampling within season). By season reported.
- **CI: date-clustered** percentile bootstrap, B = 5,000, `default_rng(2026)`, resampling `game_date` within season (parlays on one date share legs and the slate, and parlays across the 20 draws share legs, so the date is the cluster; game clustering is not usable because a parlay spans games). 95% interval.
- **Cell and families.** Every T1, T2 and money word is decided **per (pool, size), pooled over the two seasons, with a per-season sign check** (a directional word needs the same sign of its statistic in each season; a season-only statement is never made). The 10 cells are 5 pools x 2 sizes. Each BH family below is over those **m = 10** cells, one family per statistic: (a) T1 directional words, (b) T2 DEPENDENT, (c) money PROFITABLE. The two `85` cells' p-values count in m = 10 although those cells get no word.
- **T1 joint calibration (the informative statistic).** gap = mean over the cell's parlays of (joint hit - product of leg P), date-clustered CI. Words: **OVERCONFIDENT** (gap < -0.02, interval upper < 0, per-season signs agree, BH q < 0.05), **UNDERCONFIDENT** (mirror), **CALIBRATED** (|gap| <= 0.02 AND the interval inside [-0.03, +0.03]; **only the cells that can issue it, see the gate**), else **INCONCLUSIVE**. The +-0.03 margin and 0.02 limit are carried from ALT_LINE_70 (Devin 2026-10-14 13:12). Under a common over- or under-confidence of the legs the joint gap is larger than the leg gap; that is T1's content, not an error.
- **T2 dependence (the independence test), on the excess-joint-hit scale.** Per season and pool, let g = the mean marginal residual of all the pool's legs (hit - P) and shift each leg P by g (clipped to [0, 1]); excess E = mean over the cell's parlays of (joint hit - product of the shifted leg P). E removes the legs' common bias (T1's job) and measures what dependence adds, in probability units. Words: **INDEPENDENCE SUPPORTED** iff |E| <= 0.01 AND the date-clustered interval lies inside [-0.03, +0.03] (**only in the cells that can issue it, see the gate**); **DEPENDENT** iff |E| > 0.01, the interval excludes 0, per-season signs agree and BH q < 0.05; else **INCONCLUSIVE**. **New rule text, disclosed:** the first draft put the word on rho with an interval inside +-0.02; the review showed that unreachable, and the scale, the 0.01 point limit and the 0.02 "can issue" bound below were set after the gate widths were seen and before any outcome. Residual date-level drift (the forecasts are refit in month blocks) will show up as E > 0; that is real dependence for a parlay holder and is not removed. rho = sum over leg pairs of the centred residuals r_i r_j (r = hit - P - g) over the sum of sqrt(v_i v_j) (v = P(1 - P)) is **reported with its date-clustered CI and no word**; **a pair is counted once per parlay it appears in (with multiplicity)**, which makes rho the parlay-relevant average (distinct pairs are fewer, for example 5,198 against 7,443 pair appearances in 3-leg `ge70` 2023 by the review). Also reported: the ratio (parlay hit rate) / (mean product P) and a week-clustered interval of E (descriptive sensitivity).
- **Decision words for money (per pool and size, pooled over seasons).** Let lo and hi be the date-clustered interval of pooled ROI.
  - **PROFITABLE:** ROI >= +0.02 AND lo > 0 AND the point ROI > 0 in each season AND BH q < 0.05 over the family of **10** cells (5 pools x 2 sizes), q from the one-sided date-clustered p for ROI <= 0.
  - **NOT PROFITABLE:** hi < +0.02.
  - **INCONCLUSIVE:** otherwise.
  A cell that fails the minimum-data gate below gets **no word** (money, T1 or T2). By-stat slices are descriptive.
- **Bankroll path (E2, descriptive):** per (pool, size) plan and season over the R plan draws: total staked, final profit (median, 5th, 95th percentile), P(final > 0), median and 95th-percentile max drawdown of the weekly cumulative path, share of losing weeks, longest losing run. Parlay outcomes are lumpy: with a 3-leg joint P near 0.4 and D near 2.5, one parlay's sd is about D sqrt(P(1-P)) = 1.2 units, so ten $2 parlays a week have a weekly sd of about $7.7 and a 30-week season about $42. A $20-a-week bankroll cannot tell skill from luck within a season; the E1par interval does the inference.
- **Break-even comparison (reported):** per cell, mean D, mean joint P, break-even decimal at the claim (1 / mean joint P) and at the realised rate (1 / hit rate), the compounded overround (product of the legs' overrounds, about 1.09 per leg, so about 1.19 for two legs and 1.30 for three), claimed EV (mean of P d - 1, what the model says) beside realised ROI, and **comparator C1: the same legs bet singly** (mean single-leg ROI of the legs that appear in the constructed parlays, weighted by appearance), to show whether the parlay multiplies the edge or only the margin.
- **Attacks (only if any cell is PROFITABLE, before anyone believes it):** planted-future and shuffled-P (within date and stat, 50 seeds) re-run through the same construction; leave out the best 5% of parlays and the best week; (vii) a rerun on the `t60` capture with games dropped whose official-report stamp falls in (snapshot_ts, tip - 60], the ROI sign must hold; (viii) **pulled lines:** units quoted at t60 and absent at t5 (played) enter the pools at their t60 price (`pool_legs_with_pulled`), the construction is re-run with the same seeds, and the ROI sign must hold; and a check that the result is not carried by one pool-season.
- **What it cannot say.** It prices parlays as a product, which no book guarantees; one capture (about T-9) per game, and **no single placement time**: each leg is priced at its own game's T-9 and forecast at its own game's T-60, so the plan is not a placeable ticket (a real ticket placed before the earliest tip would carry staler prices and forecasts for the later games; each leg is aligned, so this does not flatter the model); BetRivers availability, limits and boosts are unknown; BetRivers is not Devin's venue; "same slate date" legs share league-wide effects only.

## What each choice protects against
| Choice | Protects against |
|---|---|
| One leg per unit, distinct games, one slate date | stacking nested rungs or same-game legs whose dependence the product ignores |
| Seeded draws, 20 per date, games first | hand selection; days with 90 legs swamping days with 4; combinatorial blow-up |
| Date clustering only | an anti-conservative interval when parlays share legs and the slate |
| Excess joint hit E with centred legs in T2 | reading the model's common over- or under-confidence as dependence; a word nobody can reach (rho inside +-0.02) |
| "Can issue" bound from model-only widths | a CALIBRATED or INDEPENDENCE word issued by a cell too wide to ever support it, or read from an INCONCLUSIVE |
| One BH family of 10 per statistic; pooled cells with a per-season sign check | an unstated family; one lucky season |
| Pulled-line attack (viii) and counts | a likely losing over vanishing from the pools and flattering every parlay |
| Code hashes, numpy version and fingerprints in the rule | the construction changing after the freeze without the markdown hash noticing |
| Per-pool words, no cross-pool claim | picking the best pool after the fact |
| BH over 10 cells, gate before word | a lucky cell in a wide family; words on cells too thin to decide |
| Secondary to single legs | parlays being read as evidence about the model when they mostly multiply the margin |
| 2025 never read | a holdout spent on an exploratory question |
