## The rule in seven lines
1. **Arms** (per game, player, stat; all on the integer grid from the stored 19 integer quantiles at taus 0.05..0.95, `ceil(q - 0.5)`, floored at 0, running max; score = 2 x mean pinball over the 19 taus = `nba.daily.lt_live.integer_crps`; the same function for every arm): **AVG** = 0.5 pmf(SEQc) + 0.5 pmf(FULL), computed in process on the 199-point integer grid exactly as T478 and stored as 19 quantiles; **FULL** = the `forward_full_shadow` grid (RECENT_ROLE_FULL, the FULL_SHADOW_LIVE arm), the primary comparator; **A0** = production with the lower-tail mixture: for pts the latest `props_context_residual_lt` pts row, for reb/ast/fg3m production's own `props_context_residual` grid (no live mixture exists for them), both latest with `made_at <= tipoff - 60 min`; **SEQc** = the E2 transformer blended with A0 by T442's learned gate (stored, descriptive, feeds the control); **AVG_A0** = 0.5 pmf(SEQc) + 0.5 pmf(A0), built at read time from the stored atoms (mixture control). **Serving rule (hard):** AVG exists only for regular-season games (`game_id` prefix `002`) and rows with a SEQ forecast (`n_prior >= 3`); every other game or row is served FULL (the shadow writes no AVG row, and counts them). No learned minutes gate, no router (T440, T465-T466 closed).
2. **Population (intention to treat):** an eligible triple = season 2026, `002` game, **played** (minutes > 0), `n_prior >= 3` at the cutoff, with a FULL row and an A0 row at the cutoff, not flagged `stale_model`. Conditional on playing, as T478. **If the AVG row for an eligible triple is missing, late (`made_at > tipoff - 60 min`) or failed, the AVG arm for that triple is the FULL grid (delta 0), which is exactly what the serving rule serves.** Nothing is dropped for a failed inference. The per-protocol figure (only triples with an AVG row) is reported descriptively. A **missingness table** is printed at every look: AVG-missing rate by played/DNP-on-report, realised minutes < 5, starter, team win/loss, and pts above/below the player's median; any gap over 2 percentage points between the two sides of a split is reported and the steward explains it in DECISIONS before the look is read. Regular season only; play-in (`005`), playoffs (`004`), preseason (`001`) and Cup final (`006`) are not scored. (The `006` prefix for this season's Cup final is not verified.)
3. **Primary (one test, m = 1):** pts, AVG minus FULL, paired per row, integer CRPS, one-sided (H1: AVG lower). **PASS at the final look iff all hold:** (a) delta <= **-0.005** (point, game-clustered); (b) the fit-noise-inflated (sigma_F included) one-sided 95% bound `delta + 1.645 * sqrt(se^2 + 0.0010^2) < 0` under BOTH the game-clustered and the date-clustered bootstrap; (c) pts AVG minus A0 <= -0.005 with one-sided upper bound < 0; (d) AVG beats the past-only stale-adjustment placebo by <= -0.0025 (upper bound < 0); (e) the day-shuffled AVG is worse than FULL (lower bound > 0); (f) AVG minus AVG_A0 <= -0.003 (point); (g) the later half of the rows by date has AVG minus FULL <= -0.003 (point); (h) guards G1-G5 below; (i) integrity gates clean. Percentile bootstrap, 2,000 draws, seed 0, one shared cluster-bootstrap function (the AVG reader imports it, it does not copy `full_shadow_live.cluster_boot`). sigma_F enters (b) only; (c), (d), (f), (g), the secondary p-values and the interim use the plain bootstrap unless stated.
4. **Secondary family (BH over exactly these seven, game-clustered one-sided p, m = 7, as T478's family):** pts AVG minus A0; reb, ast, fg3m AVG minus FULL; reb, ast, fg3m AVG minus A0. Labels: improves (delta < 0 and p_BH <= 0.05), harms (delta > 0, one-sided lower bound > 0), no difference detected; "meets floor" stated separately (-0.005 with upper bound < 0). A stat below the floor is sub-floor and supports no serving claim. They cannot rescue or fail the primary.
5. **n and looks.** Weeks count from opening night Tue 2026-10-20 = week 1 whatever date counting starts; the look dates are FIXED calendar dates: interim Sun 2026-11-15 (week 4), final Sun 2027-01-10 (week 12). Final look = the first Sunday night on or after 2027-01-10 on which pts triples >= 6,900 (counts need reb 3,500, ast 1,500, fg3m 1,100; below: INCONCLUSIVE for that stat). **If 6,900 is not reached by the last regular-season Sunday, the final look is the end-of-season look on the same criteria, with no second look** (copying FULL_SHADOW_LIVE). **Kill-only interim** on Sun 2026-11-15 if pts triples >= 3,450 (else the first Sunday after reaching it): kill iff pts AVG minus FULL > 0 with a game-clustered one-sided lower bound (sigma_F included) > 0, or an integrity kill (below); it can never declare success. If the final look is INCONCLUSIVE (never after PASS or FAIL) there is **one** second look at the end of the regular season on the same criteria with z = 1.96 in (b) and in the FAIL half-width. The type-I cost is stated, not spent away: worst case at delta 0 about 0.04 for the final look plus about 0.025 for the second, and the floor (a) and the other conditions cut both far below that.
6. **Outcomes:** PASS = a serving PROPOSAL to Devin (pts, regular season, route to FULL elsewhere), made only after FULL_SHADOW_LIVE_2026_27 reaches its own final look with a PASS (AVG is an increment over FULL; if FULL fails, AVG minus A0 only supports "mixture of SEQc and A0", which is not this rule's arm). FAIL (not PASS with the one-sided half-width <= 0.005, the half-width being the larger of the game- and date-clustered sigma_F-inflated values; or an interim kill) = drop the AVG shadow from the daily path and close AVG for 2026-27. INCONCLUSIVE = report the half-width; the shadow keeps running only for the one second look. At the second look PASS and FAIL are decided as at the final look with z = 1.96; **still INCONCLUSIVE means AVG is closed for 2026-27 with no serving claim.** If FULL_SHADOW_LIVE ends INCONCLUSIVE, an AVG PASS is not a serving proposal (it is reported and waits; FULL itself was not a clean 2025 pass, holdout row 63). Promotion is read only from the live log and is Devin's decision.
7. **One pass, no reruns, no variants, no new arm, window, floor, stat set, seed count or blend weight after any live number is seen.** A count stat is proposable only if it meets the floor with upper bound < 0, p_BH <= 0.05 and G1, G2 pass for it.

## Why these choices (what each protects against)
* **Live log only.** 2025 is spent for this model (T478); 2022-24 chose the construction (W_ALL, real tips, era and 2021-22 handling were fixed after seeing 2023/24). Forward rows are the only clean ones, and they also test the one thing T478 could not: the training-serving gap (live report feed fresher than the 2022-24 archive; GPU walk vs CPU nightly inference; monthly Colab refit vs the offline walk).
* **Primary AVG minus FULL, co-requirement AVG minus A0.** FULL is what production would be if FULL passes; the question is what the sequence member adds on top (T478's primary). A0 is required too, as in T478, so that a win over FULL cannot come from FULL being weak.
* **pts only for the primary.** T478: reb, ast, fg3m AVG minus FULL were -0.0041, -0.0038, -0.0031 (all below the floor); 2024 regular-season rehearsal: -0.0054, -0.0031, -0.0036. They are asked as direction questions.
* **Floor -0.005, one-sided, two clusterings, fit-noise term 0.0010.** A gain nobody can use; the hypothesis is directional; games share an outcome, dates share a slate. The bootstrap covers row sampling only. One 3-seed ensemble has a seed sd of about 0.00074 (T485 review, from three values, 95% range 0.6-3.7x); the monthly refits average over about 5-6 independent fits by the final look, which makes 0.0010 conservative on the mean but honest about the three-value estimate. The T478 permutation null is not carried (its sd was 1e-6: by 2025 the learned gate gave a permuted member zero weight, so it had no power).
* **Calendar floor of 12 weeks on the look.** The history gain is concentrated in the early season (2023: -0.03 in Oct-Nov falling to -0.002 by March; 2024 rehearsal by month below). A look at week 8, when the row count first allows it, would read the best months only. Guard (g) asks that the later half of the log still carries at least -0.003.
* **Hard postseason and play-in route to FULL.** T479: AVG is flat to worse than FULL in the postseason (+0.0047 [-0.0026, +0.0120]) because SEQc equals A0 there by rule. Not learning a router is deliberate (T440, T465-T466).
* **Realised minutes < 5 is a slice guard, not a gate.** AVG is worse than FULL there (+0.0439 [+0.0360, +0.0518], T479); the only gate on minutes would be a learned one, which is closed. The guard asks whether the harm exceeds what the gain buys.
* **Placebo, shuffle, mixture control built from stored atoms** because the offline guard programs (E2P, E2S, K = 50 permutation) are Colab retrains that cannot run live. Disclosed weakness: the stale-adjustment placebo is a weak discriminator (the FULL rule measured +0.0148 for its stale copy); it guards, it does not prove. A raw label shuffle is NOT a guard for a mixture (T479 check 1: it is invalid for this contrast).
* **G1 on the 199-point pmf (the one change).** The calibration limits were written for a distribution, and T478 read them on the 199-point integer pmf; P3 showed the 19-atom reading fails even for production's own A0. Reading G1 on avg_pmf199 keeps the limits, the PIT and the seed, and changes only the resolution of the object being read. G1 is the only absolute limit; every contrast keeps the 19 atoms on both sides, where a reading bias cancels. It protects against shipping an AVG whose distribution is miscalibrated, without penalising it for the coarseness of the stored form. It does not protect against a calibration that is only good after interpolation of the 19 stored quantiles (stated in the disclosures).
* **Game and date clustering, BH over seven, kill-only interim, second look at z = 1.96:** as FULL_SHADOW_LIVE and T478; the second look pays for the chance it adds.

## Placebo, shuffle, control definitions (frozen with the rule)
* **Stale-adjustment placebo:** today's FULL grid plus the same player's previous (AVG minus FULL) quantile-wise integer adjustment (strictly earlier row, same stat); a first-ever row uses the FULL grid. Computed from the stored 19 quantiles.
* **Day-shuffle:** AVG grids permuted across the players of one calendar day (same stat, seed 0); must be worse than FULL (non-inert scorer check).
* **AVG_A0:** 0.5 pmf(SEQc) + 0.5 pmf(A0) on the 19-atom pmfs (equal mass 1/19 per stored quantile); the other arms in the contrast are scored from the same atoms, so the control is not penalised by coarseness.

Population: the same eligible triples as every other guard (rule 2). A triple without an on-time AVG row is read as its FULL grid under rule 2, so its 199-point pmf is `to_integer_support(ext19(stored FULL 19 quantiles))`, the construction the shadow uses for FULL inside the mixture; the share of such triples is printed beside G1 and is bounded by the coverage gate. The 19-atom reading is printed beside it, descriptive, and decides nothing. Slices do not enter BH.

## Integrity gates (any failure => no verdict for the affected stat, reported)
* **Stamp.** The row used is the latest with `made_at <= tipoff - 60 min` (real tip; `tip_source = "real"`, never a proxy); the writer refuses `made_at >= tipoff`; the reader counts AVG rows after tip (must be 0) and (game, player, stat) triples whose every AVG row is later than the cutoff; such a triple is scored as the FULL grid under item 2 (delta 0, never dropped) and counted in the missingness table; more than 2% of eligible triples late => INCONCLUSIVE. The report tokens used were snapshots with `as_of <= tip - 60`.
* **Shadow integrity.** For every row with `in_prod_log`, the stored A0 grid equals the latest production row at or before the shadow row (1e-9), and the stored FULL grid equals the **latest `forward_full_shadow` row with `made_at <=` the AVG row's `made_at`** for the same game/player/stat (1e-9; the FULL store is write-on-change, so a same-run pairing is not used); mismatches must be 0 (unverifiable => the steward's byte-identical production check stands in). Every arm is scored from the 19 stored quantiles, full stop; the SEQc 199-point pmf is stored as well, descriptive only; the AVG 199-point pmf is read by G1 only (it never scores an arm), so the existing checks on it are load-bearing: the stored AVG grid must be reproducible from it (exact) and, as a check, from the stored SEQc and FULL atoms within +-1 on at least 95% of cells (no new threshold; a failure is an integrity failure under this section's lead sentence). The scorer reproduces `forward_avg_scores` CRPS from the grids to 1e-9.
* **Refit staleness.** Install deadline: the monthly weights are installed by 12:00 CT on the 1st (one or two days of late install is about 5% stale rows, half the limit below). Every row records `model_id` (weights sha256), `fit_through` (last date in the pool), `month_block` and `installed_at`. A row is **valid** iff `month_block` equals the calendar month of the game date (ET) and `installed_at <= made_at` and `fit_through` < the first day of that month (strictly earlier pool, as the offline walk). A game served by an older model (a missed monthly refit) is written with `stale_model = 1`, scored in the stale slice and excluded from the verdict population. More than 10% of eligible rows stale at the look => INCONCLUSIVE for AVG; the first stale month also raises an alert.
* **Inference failure and heartbeat** (the verdict itself is intention-to-treat, rule 2; these gates say when the shadow is too broken to read at all).
