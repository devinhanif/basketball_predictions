## Plain answer: information or architecture?
* **Architecture. No new pre-tip information.** The detector reads the same stream A0 already reads: a player's prior played minutes. A0's trees see `min5, min10, min40, min_gap, min_gap10, starter10, starter40, last_starter, gap_days, since_flag, flag_dir` (`COMMON_FEATURES`, `player_states`, nba/props/context_residual.py), and `since_flag` / `flag_dir` are already a minutes CUSUM. "A role change is a fact visible in the box score" is true, and it is visible to A0 today; what is new is the estimator (a calibrated P(change) and a post-change mean) of an input the model already has.
* **Measured redundancy (label-free, as-of; gate.json `redundancy_with_A0_columns`).** On the scored rows with 10+ prior games, the BOCPD level gap (bocpd_mean - min10) has correlation 0.709 / 0.696 (2023 / 2024) with A0's own `min5 - min10`, and 61% / 60% of its variance is explained linearly by A0's `min5 - min10`, `min40 - min10` and the CUSUM flag; the unexplained part has sd 1.62 / 1.69 minutes (of 2.61 / 2.68). 54% of cohort rows are already flagged by production's CUSUM (`since_flag <= 8`: 2,671 of 4,985 in 2023; 2,660 of 4,943 in 2024), and the CUSUM direction agrees with the BOCPD direction on only 29% / 28% of cohort rows.
* **Synthetic ceiling (gate.json `synthetic_detection`; Gaussian series, sd 5, step at game 80, 60 series per cell).** BOCPD's level estimate in the first five games after an 8-minute step has absolute error 4.21 min versus 6.42 for a half-life-10 EWMA (34% better) but versus 4.63 for a half-life-4 EWMA (9% better); for a 4-minute step it is WORSE than the half-life-4 EWMA (2.78 vs 2.25). Median detection delay at the declared constants is 3 games for an 8-minute step (4 for a 4-minute step), the same as the two-window and CUSUM flags (3 / 3 / 3). **"1-2 games instead of ~10" is not available from minutes alone at this signal-to-noise ratio** (a step of 1.6 sigma needs about 3 games of evidence); what BOCPD can claim is a better-calibrated level estimate than a half-life-10 mean, and A0 already carries a half-life-5 mean.
* **What would distinguish information from architecture, stated before the run:** (i) B1 beats the capacity-matched tuned EWMA (B2) and two-window/CUSUM (B3) arms on the flagged rows; if B1 beats the base only, the word is `REEXPRESSION` (faster decay, not change-point structure) and nothing is claimed; (ii) the donor and lag placebos keep at most half the gain; (iii) the gain survives on the slice flagged by BOCPD where `|min5 - min10| < 2` (A0's own recency columns see no change). The genuinely informative variants are not in this rule: the starter flag as a second observation (a promotion to the lineup is a discrete fact that minutes alone only hint at) and the T-30 confirmed five (T-F11, T-30 arm). Each needs its own id.
* **What the 4.7-minute live miss is.** Not primarily a detection-lag problem: the DECISIONS row of 2026-10-23 21:xx (ROTATION_DEPTH_PPLAY audit, 17 games, exploratory) attributes most of the excess expected players to stale movers (4.30 per game forecast players not on their team's official roster) and a hand-set default p_play of 0.82 for players with fewer than 5 prior games (1.39 per game, played 0.50). Those are rows an in-season detector never sees. The bench-minutes bias given that a player plays (-4.7) is a separate observation on 17 games with no multiplicity correction; its share that is early-season window composition (last season's playoffs inside `min10`) belongs to SEASON_BOUNDARY (T515), not here. This is why the primary slice excludes the first 10 games of a season.

## Hypotheses
H1: replacing nothing and adding six as-of BOCPD columns to the minutes model M1 lowers continuous minutes CRPS on rows with a high P(change in the last 8 played games), beyond the floor, in both seasons.
H2: it does so beyond capacity-matched columns of a half-life-tuned EWMA (B2) and of a two-window/Page-CUSUM detector with a post-flag mean (B3). (This is the attribution to change-point structure.)
H3 (conditional on H1-H2 clearing the select season): the minutes arm fed through the MINUTES_V2 path lowers the composite props CRPS on the same rows.
Null: no change. Protects against: crediting faster decay (B2), a re-expression of A0's own CUSUM (B3), a calendar/tracking effect any column would absorb (donor and lag placebos), and a cohort too concentrated in a few players (top-5 share).

## Detector (constants final if frozen; code `research/eval/role_changepoint_rule.py`)
Per player, chain of PLAYED games (`minutes > 0`) in game order across seasons (DNP and absence are P(play), not this stage). Level model: minutes ~ N(level, SIGMA^2); each run's level ~ N(mu0, TAU^2) with mu0 = the player's exponentially weighted minutes mean (half-life 40 played games) at the moment the run starts (17 before a first game); constant hazard HAZARD per game; run length truncated at 60 (the last bucket keeps a forgetting sum). Constants at the gate: SIGMA 5.0, TAU 6.0, HAZARD 1/30. **Grids searched after the freeze, on season 2022 only, by one-step-ahead predictive log-likelihood of played minutes:** SIGMA {4, 5, 6, 7}, TAU {4, 6, 8}, HAZARD {1/15, 1/30, 1/60} (36 combinations); **tie-break and sanity band declared now:** the chosen point must give a flagged share (K = 8, threshold 0.5, `n_prior >= 10`, `n_season >= 10`) between 3% and 15% of 2022 regular-season played rows; otherwise the highest-likelihood grid point inside the band is used. Then the gate below is re-run at the chosen constants BEFORE any fit (bars below; a miss stops the run, no retuning). B2's half-lives {2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 15} and B3's FAST {2, 3, 4} x SLOW {10, 20, 30} x CUSUM threshold {3, 4, 5} are searched on 2022 by one-step-ahead absolute error of played minutes. History 2019-21 exists (242,945 box rows) and is NOT used for tuning (era-flagged: bubble, empty arenas; 2022 alone is enough for a 4-constant likelihood); it could be added as a sensitivity.
Outputs at row t use games strictly before t: `p3, p5, p8` = P(run length < k), `mean` = hazard-mixed predictive mean of the next game's level (the post-change mean), `sd`, `mode` of the run length, `mu0`. **Cohort** (the rows where P(change) is high) = `p8 >= 0.5`, `n_prior >= 10`; **primary slice** = cohort rows with `n_season >= 10`.

## Arms (minutes; all LightGBM mixture refits with the same code, features, seeds and month-block walk-forward as MINUTES_V2 `run_minutes`)
Information each arm has: B0 = base features; B1 = B0 + six BOCPD columns; B2 = B0 + six EWMA columns; B3 = B0 + six two-window/CUSUM columns; every added column is a function of prior played minutes only.
* **B0** = MINUTES_V2 **M1** (mixture, base features), reproduced from `minutes_scores.npz` (`M1_*`; identity checked at run time). Production `predict_minutes` (P) and recency (R) are reported, not contrasts. (M1, not P, is the base: P is 0.8 min worse, and a gain over a weak base would be the T508 incident.)
* **B1** = B0 + `bo_p8, bo_p3, bo_mean - min10, bo_mean, bo_sd, bo_mode`.
* **B2** (tuned EWMA, same column budget) = B0 + `ew_h`, `ew_h/2`, their gaps to `min10` (2), the EW std at h, the EW starter share at h; h tuned on 2022.
* **B3** (two-window and CUSUM) = B0 + `tw_z` (mean of last FAST games minus mean of the SLOW before, over local sd), CUSUM `since`, `dir`, `post_mean`, `post_mean - min10`, local sd (CUSUM with a LOCAL 20-game baseline, floored sd 2.0; production's CUSUM standardises by the all-prior mean and sd, so B3 is not a re-run of it); constants tuned on 2022.
* **PL_donor** (placebo, past-only): B1's six columns permuted within (game date, `min10` tercile), 3 seeds. It keeps the as-of timing and marginal law and breaks the player link; it is also the noise-column control under real labels (BEST_PRACTICES 2026-10-12).
* **PL_lag** (placebo, past-only): B1's six columns from the same player 10 played games earlier.
* **Why not the shuffled-game-order placebo as asked:** shuffling a player's order within a season puts later games into earlier rows, so the placebo carries season-level information the real arm lacks; it can only produce false failures (MODEL_PLAYBOOK question 3). Replaced by the two past-only placebos above. The shuffle is kept at the DETECTOR level only: gate.json shows the mean over 60 series of the maximum p8 in the 15 rows after an 8-minute step is 0.74 on the real order and 0.50 on shuffled order.
* **Positive control (arm-saw-it, fit level, after the freeze):** in a sandbox copy, shift the label of every primary-slice row by `3 * sign(bo_mean - min10)` minutes; B1 must close at least 50% of the planted bias, B0 must not. Scoring-level and detector-level positive controls are in the gate and pass.
* **Props (conditional, H3):** the six `mv_*` columns from B0 / B1 out-of-fold per month block appended to production's features (F0, F1 = MINUTES_V2 route unchanged). Run only if C1 clears the select season (cost); integer support (199-point grid, `ceil(q - 0.5)`) for EVERY props arm.

## Slice, metric, inference, family
* **Primary slice** (rows per stat = rows per minutes arm): flagged, in-season: **3,558 (2023) / 3,581 (2024)**; 384 / 386 player-seasons; 1,012 / 1,012 games; 23 / 23 ISO weeks (at the declared constants; re-measured at the tuned constants). Secondary (descriptive, no verdict): all scored rows (27,619 / 27,583), the season-start flagged rows (1,427 / 1,362), by direction (promotion = `bo_mean > min10`, cut = below), starter vs bench (`starter10 >= 0.5`: 1,391 / 1,360 flagged rows vs bench 3,594 / 3,583 before the in-season restriction), absence returns (`gap_days >= 7`: 558 / 527 rows), min10 buckets (<10, 10-20, 20-28, >=28), the slice BOCPD flags with `|min5 - min10| < 2`, and the rows production's CUSUM flags (`since_flag <= 8`) so that B3's own slice is shown.
* **Metric:** minutes = paired continuous CRPS (2 x mean pinball over the 199-point grid), minutes, per arm minus B0 or minus the named comparator. Props = paired integer-support CRPS per stat, then the composite CM = mean over stats of delta/floor (floors pts 0.005, reb 0.003, ast 0.002, fg3m 0.001; T507 construction, one joint bootstrap on shared clusters).
* **Floors:** minutes 0.10 min on the primary slice (about 3% of M1's CRPS of 3.4-4.0; the slice is about 13% of rows, so the all-row equivalent is about 0.013 min; Devin's call, open choice 3); comparator margin 0.05 min; props composite CM <= -1.0 floor unit.
* **CI:** 95% percentile bootstrap (2000, seed 0, `nba/props/metrics.py::_clustered_boot_means`) clustered by **game, player-season and ISO week; the widest binds** (the arm's effect is shared within a player-season: BEST_PRACTICES 2026-10-20).
* **Family and BH:** m = 4 contrasts per season, BH q = 0.05 within each season: C1 B1-B0 (minutes, primary slice) | C2 B1-B2 (minutes) | C3 B1-B3 (minutes) | C4 F1-F0 (props composite). **Select 2023, report 2024**; a cell is evaluated on 2024 only if it cleared 2023 (C4 is fitted only if C1 cleared 2023).
* **Verdict words.** `PASS_MINUTES`: C1, C2 and C3 each clear both seasons (point <= floor, binding CI upper < 0, BH p < 0.05) AND every guard holds. `REEXPRESSION`: C1 clears both seasons but C2 or C3 fails in either (the gain is faster decay or the other detector's information; nothing is claimed about change-point structure). `PLACEBO_TOO`: a placebo keeps more than half the C1 gain. `FAIL_GUARD`, `FAIL_ONE_SEASON`, `FAIL_NO_GAIN`, `HARM` (C1 point > +0.10 with CI lower > 0).
* **Guards (each must hold in both seasons for a pass):** (1) all-rows no-harm: B1-B0 point <= +0.01 min and CI upper <= +0.02; (2) no stratum (bench, starter, absence returns, min10 buckets, direction) with B1-B0 > +0.10 min and CI lower > 0; (3) top-5 player-season share of the primary-slice gain <= 0.5; (4) placebo: the mean B0-minus-PL gain (donor over 3 seeds; lag) keeps strictly less than half of the B0-minus-B1 gain; (5) calibration: B1's central 80% interval covers 0.75-0.85 on the primary slice and |mean(y - mean)| <= 0.5 min; (6) planted-future identity exact on every added column; (7) B0 reproduction exact (max abs diff 0.0) and harness `min10, starter10, n_prior` equal the stored A0 on every scored row (gate G2); (8) missingness audit: the cohort flag rate by realised-minutes bucket (<5, 5-10, 10-20, >20) must not differ across buckets by more than the same gap for production's `since_flag <= 8` flag plus 0.10 (M4 of ARCHETYPE_MATCHUP_ALL; the only place a realised minute is touched, and only the flag is compared); (9) reliability at P(play) is out of scope (minutes given plays only); (10) the positive control closes at least 50% of the planted bias.
* **Kill criteria.** C1 fails the select season: C2-C4 are not run. Any identity, reproduction or positive-control failure voids the run (new id, not an edit). One pre-registered follow-up at most (open choice 6), then closed.
* **Nothing about the rule changes after a result.** Constants are final at the freeze; the 2022 grid searches are pre-declared procedures, not edits.
