## The rule in ten lines (as it would be frozen if the gate allows it)
1. **Rows.** The A0 row set of ODDS_HISTORY: `reports/odds_history/model_rows_{2023,2024}_{pts,ast,fg3m}.parquet` (production out-of-fold forecasts, played rows, career `n_prior >= 5`, regular season), identical row sets across stats. Select 2023, report 2024; season 2025 is never loaded; every SQL carries `season <= 2024`. Primary stat pts; ast and fg3m secondary. **A0 = production props_context_residual as stored**, integer-grid CRPS (199-point grid, `ceil(q - 0.5)`) for EVERY arm on the same rows.
2. **As-of shares (no tuning).** For a row (player i, game g): the decayed sum of `matchup_minutes` over i's games STRICTLY before g in g's season or the previous one, weight `0.97^(number of i's own games between)`; `s_ij` = share over the players on TONIGHT'S OPPOSING TEAM (their last known team before tonight is the opponent) among the defenders i has faced (arm D) or, with roles swapped, among the offensive players i has guarded (arm O), renormalised over that opposing mix (amended before the second gate run, see 'Amendment 1'). The primary defender / primary assignment is the largest share in that mix, counted only if share >= 0.25 and i has >= 5 window games with matchup rows.
3. **Availability.** OUT = the latest usable pre-tip official-report OUT set (`flagged_from_availability`, `ReportTriggerConfig(tip_source="real")`, `max_season=2024`; the set production sees), AND the flagged player's last known team before tonight is tonight's opponent. Games without a usable report have no OUT information and fall in S3.
4. **Archetype layer (Devin 18:30).** Offensive archetype of every scorer and defensive archetype of every defender: the project's `research.coldstart.archetypes.fit_archetypes` (standardised features + position fractions, k-means, seed 0) with **fixed k = 6 and k = 6**, fitted on the PRIOR season only (S-1 regular season, players with >= 300 minutes; others are archetype -1). Offence features: pts, ast, fg3a, fta, tov, oreb per 36, height; defence features: reb, dreb, blk, stl, pf per 36, height, weight. Archetype-vs-archetype allowed points per partial possession `r(a, b)` come from S-1 matchups (cells below 300 partial possessions use the S-1 league rate). A defender's individual allowed rate is `e_j = (points allowed - sum of partial possessions x r(arch of scorer, arch_j)) / (partial possessions + 200)`, as-of and decayed, i.e. **shrunk to his archetype with a fixed prior weight of 200 partial possessions**. If i has no usable direct history (fewer than 5 window games), his expected allowed rate against tonight's opponent uses `r(arch_i, .)` averaged over the opponent's available rotation weighted by as-of minutes: the **fallback**, whose frequency is recorded by `min10` bucket.
5. **Arm D (defense faced).** Features: `D1` = expected allowed rate against tonight's AVAILABLE defenders (OUT removed, shares renormalised), `D1 - D0` where `D0` is the same quantity over his usual mix, and the indicator that the primary defender is OUT. **Arm O (offensive load).** Features: indicator that the primary assignment is OUT, the as-of share of his defensive time freed, and his own as-of matchup efficiency (points per partial possession relative to the league, shrunk with the same prior). Archetype is a COMPONENT of both arms (it supplies `r(a, b)`, `e_j` and the fallback); it is not a third scored arm. The layer is LightGBM, 4 leaves, 150 trees, learning rate 0.05, min 20 rows per leaf, seed 0, target = actual minus A0's mean, trained on rows of strictly earlier months only (2023 rows are in the pool for 2024); the served grid is rescaled by `lambda = (mean + prediction) / mean`, clipped to [0.5, 2] and re-served on the integer grid. Rows with no OUT information are served A0 exactly.
6. **Slices.** S1 = primary defender OUT (arm D's test slice); S2 = primary assignment OUT (arm O's test slice); S3 = every other row (control). S1 and S2 may overlap (counted).
7. **Metric and pass.** Paired integer-grid CRPS, arm minus A0, on the REPORT season (2024). **PASS for an arm = pts delta <= -0.005 in its slice (D in S1, O in S2), 95% CI excluding 0 under game, player-season and ISO-week clustering (the binding CI is the widest), BH q <= 0.10 across the 6-test family (2 arms x pts/ast/fg3m, each on its own slice), AND S3 not degraded (point and binding upper bound <= +0.002), AND the top-5 player-season share of the gain <= 0.5, AND the shuffled control keeps < 50% of the gain.** Floors for ast 0.002 and fg3m 0.001 are secondary, reported with the same CIs. The select season (2023) picks which arm(s) go forward: an arm that does not reach point <= -0.005 on pts in its slice in 2023 is not scored on 2024.
8. **Controls.** (a) Positive control: a planted per-player-season shift on A0's stored grid, oracle arm, must clear pts in the slice under the binding CI (measured in the gate); (b) shuffled control: defender identities permuted within team-game (and archetypes permuted within season) must show no gain; (c) knockouts, descriptive: drop the archetype layer (shares only), drop each feature group, drop the fallback.
9. **Explanations.** Each scored row carries a short reason string, e.g. "primary defender X (wing stopper) OUT; replacement mix allows +0.06 pts/poss vs his", built by `matchup_availability_rule.reason_string` from numbers already computed; reasons never feed any score and are not evaluated here.
10. **What changes in production: nothing.** A PASS is a candidate for a live shadow in the 2026-27 forward log and, on Devin's word, one logged 2025 touch (this rule contains none). A null is closed: no variant share threshold, decay, k, prior weight or slice is tried afterwards; one pre-registered follow-up id at most.

## Known-at
* `player_game_matchups` is **post-game** data of its own game. A game's row can only enter a feature of a LATER game: `decayed_asof` uses only events with day strictly before the query day (unit test with a planted future; the gate repeats it on the real table: scale every event on or after a cut date and require identical as-of output before the cut).
* OUT flags: the usable pre-tip official-report set as of 60 minutes before the real scheduled tip (`tip_source="real"`), the one production uses; box-score absence is never used. A flagged player counts as OUT only if his last known team before tonight is tonight's opponent (a traded player is not a defender).
* Archetypes: clustered on S-1 only; `r(a, b)` from S-1 matchups only; `e_j` decayed as-of; league rate from S-1. Position and height come from `players_static` (a time-invariant-ish static fact; birth/draft facts are not used).
* Missingness: a feature is undefined when the scorer has fewer than 5 window games with matchup rows, or his top share is below 0.25, or the game has no usable report; each is a function of games BEFORE tonight (and the report), never of tonight's minutes (the 10-08 `opp_adjusted_ridge` leak was NULL iff tonight's minutes < 5). The eval audits missingness of every new column against tonight's realised minutes (a labels step, after freeze); the gate audits it against `n_prior` and `min10` only.

### Amendment 1 (before the second gate run; dated 2026-10-21, modeler)
The first gate run (commit c6d16f4, harness as committed) CRASHED at the planted-future step after printing its first diagnostic line, which showed **S1 = 0 and S2 = 0 rows in 2023**. Diagnosis (a development look at 3,000 rows of 2023, no outcomes): the as-of shares were normalised over EVERY defender the scorer has faced in the window (a player meets each opponent only a few times a season), so the top share had a maximum of 0.36 and a median of 0.033 and the 0.25 bar almost never fires; the implementation did not match the intent "the defender he usually faces TONIGHT". The fix, applied once and without touching any bar or threshold: shares are renormalised over the players whose last known team before tonight is tonight's opponent (a vectorised strictly-before team lookup, tested), for both arms; the freed share and the available-defender renormalisation use the same mix. A second defect, a float32 dtype error in the planted-future step, was fixed (no semantics). Both fixes were found by the first run, so the first-run numbers (S1 = S2 = 0 under league-wide shares; max top share 0.36, median 0.033 on 3,000 rows) are disclosed and the amended priors below were written before the second run. Risk named: the amended definition could be read as fitted to make the slices non-empty; it is the definition the rule's own sentence ("his usual defender tonight") describes, it has no free parameter, and the gate verdict still rests on thresholds fixed before any number.

## Playbook check (docs/MODEL_PLAYBOOK.md; class: GBM residual layer, plus clustering inputs)
1. **Information or capacity?** Information in principle: who guards a scorer tonight is a pre-tip fact (the report) the production model does not use for the OPPOSING side; capacity is small (4-leaf tree on <= 10 features). Honest alternative reading: A0 already holds the scorer's recency minutes and usage, team-level defense was null (T434-T435, T439), and most prop error is minutes (CLAUDE.md), so the incremental information may be near zero.
2. **Enough data for the class?** A 4-leaf tree over two seasons of rows is fine for the layer; the scoring slices (a few hundred to a couple of thousand rows, a few hundred player-seasons) are the limit, which is why the gate is a power gate. k-means with k = 6 on 300-500 clustered players per season is thin but unsupervised and fixed.
3. **Valid test?** Real-tip gating inherited from A0 and from the OUT set; integer support for every arm; as-of planted-future test (unit and real data); missingness audited (against `n_prior`/`min10` in the gate, against tonight's minutes in the eval); shuffled-identity control that cannot leak the future; positive control and power stated before any null; a comparator missing nothing production had (same rows, same calibration, same window).
4. **Lineage.** M3A archetype matchup (T056-T059): never properly tested (no CI; its reb gate FAILED on the point estimate +0.0041; pts +0.0324 point-only) - treated here as no evidence either way; lineup archetype mix T042: real but tiny (R2 0.016); DEFENSE_CONTEXT (team-level defense) null for pts and fg3m (T434-T435, T439); `opp_adjusted_ridge` leaked through a NULL pattern (fixed by the missingness rule above); RAPM / on-off player strength and usage redistribution tied or lost; PRESEASON_ROLE_PRIOR (T500) taught the three-way clustering, top-5 guard and positive-control habits, all inherited. **Carried:** integer CRPS, the clustered CI machinery, the power method (reused code). **New:** player-level defender identity and the pre-tip OUT set on the opposing side.
5. **Scored against:** CRPS of the whole distribution on the slice, not a line; a CRPS gain has not reached a betting line before (T477). Any line analysis is a new id.

## What each choice protects against
* **Strictly-before decayed shares, previous-season window:** a feature built from the same game's matchup table would be the outcome (post-game leak).
* **Report OUT only, with a team check:** box-score absence is post-game; a traded or long-term-injured player is not tonight's defender.
* **Primary share >= 0.25 and >= 5 window games:** a primary defender defined from two games is noise; the bar is fixed so it is not tuned to the slice size.
* **Archetype shrinkage prior (200 partial possessions) and fallback:** individual defender rates on a few hundred possessions are noise; the fallback avoids a NULL pattern for new players (the 10-08 leak).
* **Fixed k, S-1-only clustering:** k picked by silhouette or on outcomes is a forking path; clustering on the target season would use tonight's season.
* **Slices S1/S2 with S3 as control:** an effect that only appears when the relevant player is OUT is the mechanism; a gain on S3 would mean a generic shrink or a leak.
* **Three-way clustering, binding = widest:** game-clustered CIs were anti-conservative in T500; the same defender and the same player recur across games.
* **Shuffled-identity control and top-5 guard:** a gain that survives permuted defenders is not about defenders; five players carrying the gain was T500's failure.
* **Gate before freeze, thresholds in code:** F9 and F12 could not pass their own gates; thresholds written after a count would be fitted to it.

## Kill criteria
* Gate FAIL (neither slice reaches the bars on both seasons): not freezable as drafted; recorded; no variant.
* Select season (2023): an arm that does not reach point <= -0.005 on pts in its slice is dropped before the report season is scored.
* Any of: the planted-future identity fails; the shuffled control keeps >= 50% of the gain; S3 degrades beyond +0.002; top-5 share > 0.5; a missingness pattern that predicts tonight's minutes. Each voids the arm, not just the claim.
* Report season clears but only under game clustering: FAIL_CLUSTER (a null for claim purposes).
