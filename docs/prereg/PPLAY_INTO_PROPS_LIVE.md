## The rule in six lines
1. Arms, same rows, same conditional distribution: **prod** = the logged production forecast, unconditional through production `p_play` (the raw hurdle, `p_play == p_play_raw`); **R** = identical conditional integer quantiles (`q_grid`) with the shadow column `p_play_r` in place of `p_play`. Only p differs, so conditional-on-playing metrics cannot move (p never enters the conditional mixture; RECENT_ROLE Stage 2a).
2. Row = one logged (game, player, stat) forecast in `forward_scores_elig` (latest `made_at` <= tip - 60 min, real tip), settled, with `q_grid`, `p_play` and `p_play_r` all present; y_u = stat if the player played (minutes > 0 in the box score) else 0.
3. Primary metric per stat (pts, reb, ast, fg3m): unconditional **integer-grid CRPS** `uncond_crps(to_integer_support(q_grid), p, y_u)` (a point mass 1-p at 0 plus the 19 integer quantile points at weight p/19; `research/eval/recent_role_props.py`), paired R - prod, 95% **game-clustered** percentile bootstrap (2000 draws, seed 0).
4. PASS for a stat, all of: delta <= **-0.005** with CI upper < 0; BH p <= 0.05 over the family of 4 stats; R beats the past-only placebo by <= -0.0025 with CI upper < 0; the non-inert shuffle is alive (shuffled-p arm worse than prod, CI lower > 0); no slice with n >= 300 worse than prod by more than +0.005; the integrity gates below clean. Not PASS but CI wider than +/-0.005 at the final look = **INCONCLUSIVE** (never FAIL); not PASS with CI half-width <= 0.005 = FAIL (the shadow column is dropped, P(play) feature work stays closed).
5. Minimum n (rows, per stat; sized below from 2024 variance): **fg3m 2,200, ast 7,400, reb 28,100, pts none reachable** (needs ~135,000, ~4.4 seasons). A stat's one final look is the first Sunday night with eligible rows >= its n_min; reb and pts get one final look at the end of the regular season. A kill-only interim look at half n (fg3m 1,100, ast 3,700; reb and pts 14,000 rows): kill (drop the shadow column) only if delta > 0 with CI lower > 0 for that stat; it can never PASS or declare success.
6. One pass per stat, no reruns, no variants. Money, production and promotion are Devin's: a PASS is a proposal to put `p_play_r` into the production unconditional path; nothing here touches a stake.

## Why these choices (what each protects against)
* **Verdict on the live log, not 2022-24 or 2025**: season 2025 is spent for this model and the walk-forward 2022-24 rows chose R's columns (RECENT_ROLE saw them); only forward rows are clean. Protects against re-reading a seen season as a verdict.
* **Unconditional integer CRPS on every row, DNP included**: P(play) only matters where the player might not play; a played-only score cannot move with p (it is structurally identical across arms). Integer grid for both arms (T114-T118: a continuous arm lost 92-126% of its "gain" to the support artifact).
* **Floor -0.005, CI upper < 0, BH over 4**: a gain too small to use, or one that is noise; four stats share the same rows and p, so the family is corrected.
* **Past-only placebo = the player's previous logged `p_play_r`** (strictly earlier row, same player): R must beat a stale copy of itself, so the gain is the current information in p, not "any smoother p". **Non-inert shuffle = `p_play_r` permuted across the players of one calendar day**: if mismatching p to players does not hurt, the scorer is blind and there is no verdict.
* **Game clustering**: players of one game share outcomes and the game-level miss (design effect 1.2-1.4 measured).
* **Slices (bench = `starter_rate` < 0.5, questionable on the T-60 report, second night of a back-to-back)**: a gain concentrated where it hurts or nowhere that matters. Slices do not enter the BH family (guards only).
* **Interim kill-only at half n**: harm should stop early; success should not be declared on a half look (optional stopping).

## Integrity gates (any failure => no verdict for the affected rows/stat, reported)
* **Stamp**: every row's `made_at` <= tip - 60 min and `p_play_r` written in the same run as the forecast; rows stamped after tip are excluded and counted; if the excluded share is > 2% of logged rows the stat is INCONCLUSIVE. The forward log already refuses writes after tip.
* **Shadow integrity**: the production `p_play`, `p_play_raw`, `q_grid`, `mean_uncond` of every row must equal what a run without the shadow column writes (the steward's check; the scorer reads production p from the same stored row). `p_play_r` present on >= 98% of eligible rows, else INCONCLUSIVE for the missing-share reason.
* **Rows where p_play_r is NaN or outside (0,1)** are dropped from BOTH arms and counted.
* **Fixed placebo and non-inert shuffle** as in rule 4; both are computed by the scorer on the same rows (`research/eval/pplay_into_props_live.py`: `prev_row_p`, `shuffle_p_within_day`).
* Population caveat, stated not corrected: the log only holds players with production raw `p_play` >= 0.5 (`ForwardConfig.min_p_play`), so DNP-heavy low-p players are mostly absent; the 2024 rehearsal below is restricted to that population.

## Secondary (descriptive, cannot rescue or fail the primary): decision score
`docs/DECISION_SCORING.md` at the DraftKings T-60 price (and T-30 if captured), legs whose side depends on playing. A sportsbook voids a prop when the player does not play, so P(over) conditional on playing sets the side and p_play cannot change it; p changes only whether a leg clears the threshold on the **void-aware EV** `p_arm * (p_side * decimal_odds - 1) - fee` (staked unit returned, profit 0, on a void). Arms differ only in p; thresholds 0, 2, 5, 10% as in DECISION_SCORING; reported per stat with n bets, ROI, game-clustered CI; below 300 bets "insufficient sample". Whether DraftKings voids every DNP leg is my understanding of standard house rules and is not verified here; if the stored forward P(over) is the unconditional `p_ge_uncond` rather than the conditional `p_ge`, the secondary is not computed and that is reported. Expected: tiny n and CIs straddling 0 (the 2026-10-11 descriptive run: ROI -0.8% [-1.5%, +0.1%] at 2%).

## Kill / stop criteria
* Interim kill (rule 5), final FAIL (rule 4), or a failed integrity gate on > 2% of rows: drop `p_play_r` from the daily path; P(play) feature work is closed for 2026-27. A new question gets a new id.
* No new arm, window, floor or stat set after any result is seen.

## Unstated details chosen before scoring
