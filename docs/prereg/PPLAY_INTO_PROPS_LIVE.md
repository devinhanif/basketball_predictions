## The rule in six lines
1. Source and arms: the shadow table `forward_pplay_shadow` (commit e105caf), read-only. Per (game, player) the latest row with `made_at <= tipoff - 60 min`; **prod** = `p_play_prod` (production's raw hurdle), **R** = `p_play_r`. Both arms use the SAME conditional distribution, the shadow row's `props_cond[stat].q_grid` (19 continuous quantiles at 0.05..0.95, conditional on playing), converted to integer support exactly as the stored 2023-24 components were: `to_integer_support` = `ceil(q - 0.5)`, floored at 0, running max. Only p differs, so conditional-on-playing metrics cannot move.
2. Population = every settled row (`forward_pplay_scores.status = 'scored'`, y = minutes > 0) with non-NULL `p_play_r` in (0,1) and a 19-point grid for the stat: all rostered not-OUT players with `n_played_prior >= 5`, logged by production or not (`in_prod_log` is reported, not filtered). y_u = box-score stat if played else 0.
3. Primary per stat (pts, reb, ast, fg3m): unconditional integer CRPS (point mass 1-p at 0 plus 19 integer points at weight p/19), paired R - prod, 95% game-clustered percentile bootstrap (2000 draws, seed 0), floor **-0.005**, BH over the 4 stats.
4. PASS for a stat, all of: delta <= -0.005 with CI upper < 0; BH p <= 0.05; beats the past-only placebo (the player's previous shadow `p_play_r`) by <= -0.0025 with CI upper < 0; the day-shuffle of `p_play_r` is worse than prod (CI lower > 0); no slice with n >= 300 worse by more than +0.005; integrity gates clean. Not PASS with half-width > 0.005 at the final look = INCONCLUSIVE; not PASS with half-width <= 0.005 = FAIL.
5. n_min (rows) and looks: **fg3m 2,200, ast 7,400, reb 29,700, pts unreachable (~138,000)**; one final look per stat at the first Sunday night with eligible rows >= n_min (reb and pts: end of the regular season at the latest). Kill-only interim at half n (fg3m 1,100, ast 3,700; reb and pts 14,000 rows): kill only if delta > 0 with CI lower > 0; it can never declare success.
6. One pass per stat, no reruns, no variants; a PASS is a proposal to Devin, a FAIL or kill drops `p_play_r` and closes P(play) feature work for 2026-27.

## Why these choices (what each protects against)
* **Verdict on the live log, not 2022-24 or 2025**: season 2025 is spent for this model and the walk-forward 2022-24 rows chose R's columns (RECENT_ROLE saw them); only forward rows are clean. Protects against re-reading a seen season as a verdict.
* **Unconditional integer CRPS on every row, DNP included**: P(play) only matters where the player might not play; a played-only score cannot move with p (it is structurally identical across arms). Integer grid for both arms (T114-T118: a continuous arm lost 92-126% of its "gain" to the support artifact).
* **Floor -0.005, CI upper < 0, BH over 4**: a gain too small to use, or one that is noise; four stats share the same rows and p, so the family is corrected.
* **Past-only placebo = the player's previous logged `p_play_r`** (strictly earlier row, same player): R must beat a stale copy of itself, so the gain is the current information in p, not "any smoother p". **Non-inert shuffle = `p_play_r` permuted across the players of one calendar day**: if mismatching p to players does not hurt, the scorer is blind and there is no verdict.
* **Game clustering**: players of one game share outcomes and the game-level miss (design effect 1.2-1.4 measured).
* **Slices (bench = production `proj_minutes` < 20, low availability = production `p_play` < 0.9, second night of a back-to-back from the `games` table)**: a gain concentrated where it hurts or nowhere that matters. The shadow table stores neither a starter rate nor a report tag, so bench and low-p replace the earlier starter-rate and questionable slices (chosen before any live row). Slices do not enter the BH family (guards only).
* **Interim kill-only at half n**: harm should stop early; success should not be declared on a half look (optional stopping).

## Integrity gates (any failure => no verdict for the affected rows/stat, reported)
* **Stamp**: the row used is the latest with `made_at <= tipoff - 60 min` (real tip); the writer refuses `made_at >= tipoff`, and the reader counts rows after tip (must be 0) and (game, player) pairs whose every row is later than the cutoff (excluded). If excluded pairs exceed 2% of shadow pairs the stat is INCONCLUSIVE.
* **Shadow integrity**: `p_play_prod` of every `in_prod_log` shadow row must equal `prediction->>'$.p_play'` of the production `forward_predictions` row of the same run, game and player (`stamp_and_shadow_gates`: mismatch must be 0; if nothing can be compared the gate is reported as unverifiable and the steward's byte-identical production check stands in). `p_play_r` non-NULL on >= 98% of rows whose `r_reason` is not `out_on_report` or `n_played_prior<5`, else INCONCLUSIVE.
* Rows with NULL or out-of-range `p_play_r`, a missing/short grid, or a played row without a box stat are dropped from BOTH arms and counted (`dropped_no_grid_or_box`).
* Fixed placebo and non-inert shuffle as in rule 4 (`prev_row_p`, `shuffle_p_within_day`).
* Population note: the population is all `p_play_r` rows, not the p >= 0.5 subset; this includes the low-p players the production log drops. The shadow `props_cond` grid is the shadow run's own and shared by both arms, so `in_prod_log = false` rows are scored on the same footing.

## Secondary (descriptive, cannot rescue or fail the primary): decision score
`docs/DECISION_SCORING.md` at the DraftKings T-60 price (and T-30 if captured), legs whose side depends on playing. A sportsbook voids a prop when the player does not play, so P(over) conditional on playing sets the side and p_play cannot change it; p changes only whether a leg clears the threshold on the **void-aware EV** `p_arm * (p_side * decimal_odds - 1) - fee` (staked unit returned, profit 0, on a void). Arms differ only in p; thresholds 0, 2, 5, 10% as in DECISION_SCORING; reported per stat with n bets, ROI, game-clustered CI; below 300 bets "insufficient sample". Whether DraftKings voids every DNP leg is my understanding of standard house rules and is not verified here; the shadow stores only conditional grids, so P(over) at a line comes from `props_cond`; if a leg's production P(over) is the unconditional `p_ge_uncond` instead, the secondary is not computed and that is reported. Expected: tiny n and CIs straddling 0 (the 2026-10-11 descriptive run: ROI -0.8% [-1.5%, +0.1%] at 2%).

## Kill / stop criteria
* Interim kill (rule 5), final FAIL (rule 4), or a failed integrity gate on > 2% of rows: drop `p_play_r` from the daily path; P(play) feature work is closed for 2026-27. A new question gets a new id.
* No new arm, window, floor or stat set after any result is seen.

## Unstated details chosen before scoring
