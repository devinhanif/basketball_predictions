## Timing: the one alternate capture is BEFORE the model's cutoff (the finding that shapes the rule)
- **Fact.** Alternates exist only at `t60`. The snapshot is the same timestamp as the main-market `t60` capture (mean difference 0.0 minutes in all 1,012 and 1,314 games). Lead before the real tip (`data/ops/model_clv_pairs`): **median 64.35 minutes (2023) and 64.37 (2024); the 1st to 99th percentiles are 64.33-64.38 in both seasons; the 2023 minimum is 64.2 and the maximum 79.37 (one game with lead > 66 minutes).** The model's report cutoff is tip - 60. So the price is **about 4.3 minutes OLDER than the model's information** in every game, and 19.4 minutes older in one. Quote freshness inside the snapshot is not the issue: `snapshot_ts - last_update` has p10 / median / p90 of -0.2 / 0.4 / 1.6 minutes (2023) and -0.3 / 0.2 / 1.3 (2024). No at-or-after-cutoff alternate capture exists (no `t5` alternate pull was made).
- **Why it matters, in one line.** A price older than the model's information lets the model know news the price did not, which can only push toward PROFITABLE (the 19:00-proxy precedent and ALT_LINE_70 review N1).
- **What the model's information is.** In the production forecast the only time-gated input is the official injury report: `nba/props/context_residual.py: flagged_from_availability` joins `player_availability` rows on `as_of` with real tip times and `LEAD_MIN = 60` (`research/eval/odds_history_eval.py:72`); the Elo margin is `asof_elo_margin` (date-level); the other features are recency and schedule. The stored report stamps are 11:00 and 17:00 Eastern only (`player_availability.as_of`, source `nba_official_report`). (Read from the code, not re-verified by a planted-future run; attack (i) includes a planted-future check of the P column.)
- **The rule: close the channel, do not just disclose it.** The PRIMARY arm drops (a) every game with a report stamp in `(snapshot_ts, tip - 60 min]` (Eastern clock; **39 of 1,012 games in 2023 and 62 of 1,314 in 2024**, 3.9% and 4.7%; ALT_LINE_70's review counted 50 of 1,318 and 62 of 1,315 over all games with a main-line snapshot, 2023 is smaller here because only games with alternate quotes count) and (b) every game whose lead exceeds 66 minutes (1 game, 2023). On the retained games the model's information at T-60 is, by construction, the information that existed when the price was taken, so the bet is placeable at the snapshot with exactly the model's information and the stale-price flattery is closed rather than footnoted. **Disclosed:** this removes the games in which news arrived, which are the games where a model with the news could have the most edge (the arm is conservative against PROFITABLE); publication latency is bounded by the measured gaps: in the retained games the latest usable stamp precedes the snapshot by >= 55.6 min in 99% of games (median 115.6); 2 games per season have a stamp 15-30 min before the snapshot and none within 15 min, so a late-published report could enter the model but not the price only if its latency exceeded 25 min (4 games) or 55 min (all others). No quote has `last_update` after tip - 60 (max 1.38 min after the snapshot).
- **What remains and its direction.** (1) The executable price at T-60 may differ from the price at T-64. Price drift between `t60` and `t5` on the MAIN markets of the same book, line and side (alternates have no `t5`): DraftKings mean absolute change in the over's implied probability 0.008 (2023, n = 48,492 shared rungs) and 0.012 (2024, n = 46,472); mean signed change -0.0004 and -0.0007; for rungs with model P >= 0.70 the signed mean is -0.0007 (n = 628) and -0.0012 (n = 414). FanDuel: |change| 0.010 / 0.0085, signed -0.0005 / -0.0011; model-selected -0.0007 (n = 292) and -0.0007 (n = 237). Reading: the over's implied probability tends to fall slightly after `t60`, i.e. the later price is slightly cheaper for the bettor on model-favoured overs: a stale price is not systematically in the model's favour; the effect is small and the model-selected n is small. (2) Whether DraftKings would accept the bet at that price and size at that minute is unknown. (3) News not in the report (late scratches, minutes limits reported elsewhere) can only hurt the model, because the model never sees it.

## Part 1: calibration at P >= 0.70 on posted DraftKings rungs (no prices used in the statistic)
**Object.** Primary arm over rungs of DraftKings at `t60` (half-point, joined to the stored model row on (game_id, player_id, stat)), minus the excluded games, one row per (game, player, stat, point). Model P(over) = P(Y >= point + 0.5) on the integer support from `reports/odds_history/model_rows_{2023,2024}.parquet` (no refit, no recalibration, no map; `p_ge[k-1] = P(Y >= k)`); hit = Y >= point + 0.5. P is conditional on playing, as a sportsbook voids a DNP (stated). Rungs beyond the support get P = 0 (as `p_over_at`).
**Cells (verbatim from ALT_LINE_70 unless noted).** Reliability bins [0.70,0.75), [0.75,0.80), [0.80,0.85), [0.85,0.90), [0.90,0.95), [0.95,1.0], P = 1.0 sub-row; **headline band [0.70,0.90)** per stat x season (8 cells); distance from the model main line (ALT_LINE_70 definition and INSUFFICIENT rule) inside the band, descriptive. The deep tail (P < 0.50 in bins 0-0.1 ... 0.4-0.5) is reported as gap and break-even prices, no word, no BH.
**Statistic, CI, words:** gap = hit rate - mean P over the cell's rungs; CI = game-clustered and date-clustered percentile bootstrap, B = 5,000 each (`default_rng(2026)` for games, `default_rng(2027)` for dates; one resample matrix per season shared by every cell and arm), decision interval = union (lower = min, upper = max), p for BH = max(p_game, p_date) with the +1 correction, two-sided. Words per headline cell: **CALIBRATED** (|gap| <= 0.02 AND the decision interval inside [-0.03, +0.03]); **OVERCONFIDENT** (gap < -0.02 AND interval upper < 0 AND BH q < 0.05); **UNDERCONFIDENT** (mirror); **INCONCLUSIVE** otherwise; BH over the 8 headline cells. Stat word = the same word in both seasons, else MIXED. Model word: **"calibrated at 70%+ on posted DraftKings rungs"** iff all four stat words are CALIBRATED; **"OVERCONFIDENT at 70%+ on posted DraftKings rungs for <stats>"** if any stat word is OVERCONFIDENT; else "NOT SHOWN calibrated" with the list. The +-0.03 margin is Devin's choice of 2026-10-14 13:12, carried (see the disclosure in ALT_LINE_70 Part 1: set after the half-widths were counted; at +-0.02 the conservative-bound gate would fail every headline cell here as well, widths 0.023-0.030).
**Descriptive.** Starter / bench split (`starter10 >= 0.5`), and the same gap on the all-games arm (S1).
**What Part 1 cannot say.** Not a profit claim; covers only rungs DraftKings posted at about T-64.

## Part 2: profit at posted prices on $20 a week (DraftKings, primary arm)
**Bet set A (primary).** Primary-arm DraftKings over rungs with model P >= 0.70 (tolerance 1e-12), one row per (game, player, stat, point). Flat stake; decimal odds d from the American price; profit = d - 1 on a win, -1 on a loss (stake units); no pushes (half-point lines); no fee beyond the posted price (a sportsbook has none; promotions, boosts and taxes are not modeled). **Plan:** $2 per bet, at most 10 bets a week, the Monday-start week of `game_date`, no carry, no restaking; when more than 10 units (game, player, stat) are eligible in a week, draw 10 units uniformly without replacement, then one rung uniformly within each unit; `default_rng(2026)`, R = 2,000 plan draws per season (a fresh generator per plan, seasons in order). DraftKings has 876 (2023) and 1,121 (2024) eligible units in the median week, so the plan is spent in all 29 and 35 weeks and inference rests on E1, not on the plan.
**Estimands and CIs (as ALT_LINE_70).** E1 (decision): rung ROI = sum(profit) / n over ALL eligible rungs, pooled over both seasons (resampling stratified by season), by stat and by season reported; E1u (descriptive): mean over units of each unit's mean rung profit; CI game-clustered and date-clustered, B = 5,000 each, ratio estimator on cluster totals, decision interval = union; E2 (plan dollars, descriptive): per season over the R plan draws, total staked, final profit (median, 5th, 95th), P(final > 0), median and 95th-percentile max drawdown of the weekly cumulative path, share of losing weeks, longest losing run. A season of $20 weeks cannot tell skill from luck (the claimed per-bet sd is about 0.46, so a season's P&L sd is about $16 to $20); E1 does the inference.
**Decision words (pooled E1, both seasons):** **PROFITABLE** iff pooled ROI >= +0.02 AND lo > 0 AND the point ROI is > 0 in each season; **NOT PROFITABLE** iff hi < +0.02; **INCONCLUSIVE** otherwise. The word is always read as **"profitable at the DraftKings t60 snapshot (about T-64)"**; it makes arm A a candidate for the forward log only, never a bet (CLAUDE.md non-negotiable 6). By-stat words descriptive (a stat-level claim needs BH q < 0.05 over the 4 stat cells, two-sided p = max(p_game, p_date)). Floor +0.02: as in ALT_LINE_70.
**Break-even table (reported, per P bin).** Rungs, mean claimed P, realised hit rate with its interval, mean decimal price, break-even decimal at the claim (1/P) and at the realised rate (1/h) with American equivalents, ROI at the claim (mean P d - 1) beside the realised ROI.
**Comparators (descriptive).** C1 price lean: DraftKings over rungs whose own raw implied probability `1/d >= 0.70`, model ignored. C2: the expected ROI of a random DraftKings rung = the ROI of all primary-arm DraftKings rungs (the vig baseline). Arm B: the same eligibility on the best over price across all books at that exact rung (highest decimal, ties to the alphabetically first book), reported with the number of books. Replicates: FanDuel (full pipeline; descriptive), Fanatics 2024 only (fails the Part 1 gate; descriptive), and a count table for the other six books.
**Attacks (only if the word is PROFITABLE, before anyone believes it):** (i) planted future (shift each model P to a different player-game of the same stat; ROI must fall to the C2 level); (ii) shuffle P within (game, stat) among DraftKings rungs, 50 seeds, ROI must not clear the interval; (iii) leave out the best 5% of bets and the best week; (iv) FanDuel's full pipeline and arm B must have the same sign; (v) missingness audit on `last_update` and quote age; (vi) sensitivity to the overround: ROI minus the C2 baseline; (vii) the report-window games added back (arm S1) must keep the sign of the primary result, and their own ROI is reported beside it: if the stale-price channel exists, the window games should sit above the primary arm; (viii) not applicable (no second alternate capture); (ix) FanDuel with the FIRST listing of each duplicated key (read from the raw files) must keep the sign of its last-listing result; (x) S3, the excluded rungs and ladders added back, is reported beside the primary result. A failed attack is reported as 'PROFITABLE, not believed (attack <x> failed)'; arm A is then not a forward-shadow candidate under this id.

## Secondary family: four disjoint P bins, reported, none promoted
Labels by midpoint: **55** = [0.50,0.60), **65** = [0.60,0.70), **75** = [0.70,0.80), **85** = [0.80,0.90); bins 75 and 85 partition the primary headline band; the primary set also contains P >= 0.90 rungs, reported as a descriptive row. None is selected, ranked or promoted after the result; a profitable bin is a hypothesis for a new rule on the live log, never a claim, and it never replaces or softens the primary word.
**Part 1S.** Cells = (bin, stat), **pooled over the two seasons: 16 cells**, same CI, words (CALIBRATED: pooled |gap| <= 0.02 and the pooled decision interval inside +-0.03; OVERCONFIDENT / UNDERCONFIDENT: |gap| > 0.02, interval beyond 0, **same sign in both seasons**, BH q < 0.05 over the 16 cells), same p. The per-season gaps are reported beside. No distance slices. Asymmetry, disclosed: CALIBRATED is decided on the pooled cell alone, while OVERCONFIDENT / UNDERCONFIDENT also require the same sign in both seasons, so offsetting season gaps can pool to CALIBRATED; the per-season gaps are printed beside every word.
**Part 2S.** For each bin, DraftKings arm A restricted to rungs whose model P is in the bin, same plan (one plan per bin, never added), E1 / E1u / E2 / break-even; words per bin: PROFITABLE needs the primary definition AND BH q < 0.05 over the 4 bin cells (one-sided p for ROI <= 0, larger of the two clusterings); NOT PROFITABLE / INCONCLUSIVE as primary.
**Clustering.** Games (and, separately, dates) are drawn once per season with one resample matrix shared by every bin, stat and arm; a game's rungs in different bins stay together.
