## Why this rule exists
The app has no blocks, steals or combo forecasts; the books list all three, and `nba/parlay` states "PRA/PR/PA/RA and steals/blocks markets are skipped (no joint marginal for sums yet)". Devin wants them in the log before opening night so that the live season, the only clean test left, can say whether they are any good. The question is not "can we find an edge" (no steals price from a sharp book exists; FAN_KNOWLEDGE: consensus steals over-prices the over by 1-3 points); it is "is this forecast better than what a user could compute from a player's last games, and is it honest". A clean "no" is a result.

## The rule in eight lines
1. **Targets (7):** `blk`, `stl` (marginals), and the sums `pra` = pts+reb+ast, `pr`, `pa`, `ra`, `bs` = blk+stl. Every forecast is conditional on playing, an integer pmf stored as a 199-point equal-mass grid (`pmf199`) and its 19 quantiles (`q19` = `q19_of_grid199`, reproducible from the grid).
2. **Shadow arm S per target.**
   * `blk`, `stl`: the **NB count head** (`nba.props.count_head.NBCountModel`): LightGBM `objective="poisson"` mean on the stock columns of `nba.props.stock_features` (production's feature recipe recomputed for blk/stl), dispersion `alpha(mu)` fitted by maximum likelihood in 4 bins of the predicted mean on the newest 15% of the training window, integer NB quantile function at the 199 taus. Refit nightly on played rows dated strictly before the slate date, production learner settings.
   * `pra`, `pr`, `pa`, `ra`: a **Gaussian copula** over five marginals: pts = production's lower-tail-mixture row (`props_context_residual_lt`, the A0 of the other shadows), reb and ast = production's primary rows (`props_context_residual`), each rebuilt from the stored `p_ge_full` (n = 199, exact), blk and stl from the NB heads. Latent correlation `R` fixed on 2023 (below), 10,000 frozen common draws (`base_normals`, seed 20261014, whitened and antithetic), each draw pushed through each row's marginal CDF, sums taken, pmf stored.
   * `bs`: the **independent convolution** of the blk and stl marginals (the copula did not beat it on the 2023 select half, so no dependence is claimed; `R = I` through the same sampler).
3. **Comparators (all logged for the same rows):** `RECENT` (all seven): the recency-weighted empirical distribution of the same quantity over the player's last 40 played games (weights `0.5 ** (games_ago / 10)`, production's recency), at least 5 prior games. `INDEP` (the four copula combos): the same sampler with `R = I`; never a product of marginals computed any other way. Descriptive only, not part of any verdict: `DIRECT` (production's context-residual head applied to the combo as if it were a stat; optional arm, droppable on 10-16 without changing any verdict), `RECENT_CAL` (recency mean/std with production's conformal shape).
4. **Population (intention to treat on servable rows):** season 2026, regular season (`game_id` prefix `002`), **played** (minutes > 0), `n_prior >= 5`, with on-time production rows for pts (lt), reb and ast (`made_at <= tipoff - 60 min`) and an on-time shadow row. A player without a production context row (recency fallback, late roster, not rostered) is **not servable**: counted and printed, not scored. Missing or late shadow rows of servable players are unscored and enter the coverage gate (G-COV); nothing is imputed. Play-in (005), playoffs (004), preseason (001), Cup final (006) are not scored.
5. **Metric:** exact integer CRPS of the stored 199-point grid, `2 * mean over the 199 taus of the pinball loss` (`nba.eval.context_residual_eval.crps_from_quantiles`; the midpoint rule is exact for a 199-atom pmf), the same function for every arm; the 19-atom `nba.daily.lt_live.integer_crps` is printed beside it, descriptive. Paired per row, **one-sided** (H1: S lower than RECENT). Percentile bootstrap, 2,000 draws, seed 0, one shared cluster function (`nba.eval.full_shadow_live.cluster_boot` / `one_sided`, imported, not copied), clustered by **game** and by **date** (cross-game slate effects).
6. **Family and PASS (per target):** family F = the seven targets, BH over their game-clustered one-sided p-values (S minus RECENT), m = 7, q = 0.05. A target **PASSES at the final look iff all hold:** (a) point delta (S minus RECENT) <= **-floor_t** (blk 0.0050, stl 0.0050, pra 0.0438, pr 0.0401, pa 0.0363, ra 0.0188, bs 0.0061; each = max(0.005, 1% of RECENT's 2023 regular-season CRPS)); (b) the one-sided 95% upper bound < 0 under BOTH the game-clustered and the date-clustered bootstrap; (c) BH-adjusted p <= 0.05 over F; (d) **G1 calibration** on the stored `pmf199` (n >= 3,000): randomised PIT with seed 0 (`nba.eval.lower_tail_eval.randomized_pit`), |mean(y - mean of the grid)| <= 0.05 x mean(y), 80% interval coverage in [0.75, 0.85], coverage within 0.02 of nominal at the 0.10, 0.20, 0.80, 0.90 quantiles (0.05 and 0.95 reported); (e) slice guard: no slice with n >= 300 (starter / bench by `starter10 >= 0.5`; realised minutes < 10, 10-20, > 20; teammate OUT on the report; second night of a back-to-back; cold start `n_prior < 10`; first 15 team games) worse than RECENT by more than floor_t; (f) the later half of the rows by date has S minus RECENT <= -floor_t (point); (g) for the four copula combos, COPULA minus INDEP < 0 with game-clustered one-sided upper bound < 0 (the dependence must earn its place; for `bs` this condition does not apply because S = INDEP); (h) integrity gates (below) clean.
7. **Looks and outcomes.** Weeks count from opening night Tue 2026-10-20. Interim (kill-only) **Sun 2026-11-15** if every target has >= 1,500 rows (else the first Sunday after): a target is killed iff S minus RECENT > 0 with a game-clustered one-sided LOWER bound > 0, or an integrity kill; it can never declare success. Final **Sun 2027-01-10**, the first Sunday on or after it on which the target has **>= 3,000 rows** (below that: INCONCLUSIVE for that target). If 3,000 is not reached by the last regular-season Sunday, the final look is the end-of-season look on the same criteria with no second look. **PASS** = a proposal to Devin to drop the label "new model, being tested" for that target (his decision). **FAIL** (not PASS and the larger of the game- and date-clustered one-sided half-widths <= floor_t, or an interim kill) = the target stays labelled, or is shown only as RECENT labelled "recent form", Devin's call; the shadow stops after its second look. **INCONCLUSIVE** = keep running to ONE second look at the end of the regular season, same criteria with z = 1.96 in the half-width; still INCONCLUSIVE means closed with no claim. A target that PASSES does not make the others better. The app label is "new model, being tested" for every target until that target's PASS.
8. **One pass, no variants.** No arm, window, floor, draw count, seed, estimator, marginal, stat set or tolerance changes after any live number is seen; a new idea is a new id. Descriptive output (DIRECT, RECENT_CAL, slices of non-verdict arms, sampler error, per-month tables, line log loss where a price exists) never changes a verdict.

## Hypothesis
H-stocks: on a played row, the NB count head forecasts the integer distribution of blocks (steals) better than the player's own recent empirical distribution, by at least floor_t, and is calibrated. H-combo: a Gaussian copula over calibrated integer marginals forecasts PRA, PR, PA, RA better than the player's recent empirical combo distribution (information), and better than the same marginals treated as independent (structure), and is calibrated. For `bs` only the information claim is made.

## Primary metric, practical floor, CI, family, select/report seasons, slices, kill criteria (the list asked for)
Primary metric: integer CRPS of the 199-point pmf, S minus RECENT. Practical floor: per target, above. CI: one-sided 95% percentile bootstrap, game- and date-clustered, 2,000 draws, seed 0 (row-level CIs were anti-conservative, BEST_PRACTICES 3). Multiplicity: BH over the seven targets. Select season 2023, rehearsal report season 2024, **verdict venue the 2026-27 live log**. Slices: rule 6(e), fixed now. Kill: rule 7. Holdout: none. **What each choice protects against:** the floor stops "significant but unusable" gains; both clusterings stop slate-level correlation from posing as precision; BH stops one lucky target of seven being read alone; G1 stops a sharp-looking forecast that is miscalibrated at the tails (the independent sum fails it badly: PRA 80% coverage 0.71); the slice guard stops a win that lives only in starters or only in bench rows; the later-half condition stops an early-season-only win (the sequence models lost theirs this way); the INDEP condition stops a copula that adds cost and no skill; fixed look dates stop optional stopping; intention-to-treat on servable rows and the coverage gate stop a quiet inference failure from selecting its own sample.

## Integrity gates (any failure blocks PASS; a persistent one is an integrity kill)
Stamp: every scored row has `made_at <= tipoff - 60 min` and `tip_source = real`; late rows (`made_at > tipoff - 60 min`) are excluded and counted. **G-COV:** at least 95% of servable played rows have an on-time shadow row for every target (90% in any slice, kill below 80%); the missingness table (by DNP-on-report, realised minutes < 5, starter, win/loss, above/below the player's median) is printed at every look and any gap over 2 points between two sides is explained before the look is read. Reproducibility: `s_q19` equals `q19_of_grid199(s_pmf199)` exactly on every row; `r_id` and `base_id` equal the frozen values; `model_id` changes only on a refit date. Staleness: a row whose heads were fit through a date more than 3 days before the slate counts as stale (flagged, share <= 10%). Inference failures are heartbeat `failed`, never retried after tip, and never touch production.
