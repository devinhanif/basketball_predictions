## Question
Devin's panel shows the top 3 two-leg and three-leg parlays per slate, ranked by `p_joint_cal`: legs calibrated by the frozen CAL map, cross-game legs multiplied as if independent, same-game legs joined by a copula (contract `top_parlays_v2`). T490 found that model "70%+" parlays hit about 10 points less, and T492 traced that to leg miscalibration compounding (a ticket's gap is roughly the sum of its legs' gaps), with cross-game dependence pushing the other way. CAL fixes the legs (T493: Pinnacle log loss -0.0117 vs raw, Stage 1 pass). Questions:
1. **Joint calibration.** Is mean(hit) equal to mean(`p_joint`) (within 0.02, interval inside +-0.03) for 2- and 3-leg tickets, separately for cross-game (independent product) and same-game (copula), once legs are CAL?
2. **Did fixing the legs fix the parlay?** Joint log loss of the CAL arm against the raw-leg arm on the same tickets.
3. **Ranking-relevant.** Is the top-3-per-date by `p_joint_cal`, which is what the panel prints, calibrated, or does selecting the highest estimates reinstate over-confidence (winner's curse)?
4. **Descriptive only (no word):** the copula against independence for same-game tickets, and an optional joint-level recalibration J.

## Tickets (fixed now, seeded, no hand selection)
Four cells: {cross-game, same-game} x {2 legs, 3 legs}. Legs are first sorted by (game_date, game_id, player_id, stat).
1. **Random tickets U-ALL (the calibration universe).** Per (season, type, size, date), up to **20** draws.
   - *Cross-game:* a draw picks m distinct games uniformly without replacement among that date's games with eligible legs, then one leg uniformly within each picked game. Seeds `default_rng([2026, season, m, 1])`.
   - *Same-game:* a draw picks ONE game uniformly among that date's games with at least m eligible legs, then m distinct (player, stat) legs uniformly without replacement. Seeds `default_rng([2026, season, m, 2])`. Same-player pairs (different stats) are allowed and labelled.
   - A leg set already drawn that date is dropped (no refill). Games first, then a leg, weights games equally, not legs (as in ALT_PARLAY_X).
2. **The panel slice TOP (ranking-relevant).** Per (date, size), the `TOP_N = 3` tickets with the highest `p_joint_cal` among all candidates, ties broken by lower leg-index tuple.
   - *Cross-game candidates:* each game keeps its 3 highest-q legs; all m-subsets of games times leg choices. This is exact for the top 3 of a product (a top-3 ticket never uses a leg ranked above 3 in its game).
   - *Same-game candidates:* each game's 8 highest-q legs; all m-subsets. An approximation, stated: a lower-q leg could enter the top 3 only through copula uplift, which is small.
   - **The union panel** ranks cross-game and same-game candidates together by `p_joint_cal` (the contract prints one list per size with a `same_game` flag); TOP-cross and TOP-same are also reported separately. Same-game candidates are ranked by the copula arm, cross-game by the product. **TOP is this rule's own deterministic proxy of the panel under the widest candidate set, not the panel.** The producer differs: it keeps the global top-40 legs per book (not per game), ranks across every book that has a CAL map (no ticket mixes books), uses the newest pre-tip main-line quote (not T-60), and prices same-game tickets by Monte Carlo with production pair correlations. The live log scores the producer's real list.
3. **Settlement.** A ticket hits iff every leg's side hits (`over` from the paired frame). No pushes (half-point lines).

## Arms (probabilities for the same tickets)
- **RAW-IND:** product over legs of the raw production probability of the CAL side, `P_raw(side_cal) = p_model if p_cal >= 0.5 else 1 - p_model`, clipped to [0.5/199, 1 - 0.5/199]. On 12.4% of 2024 DraftKings legs this is below 0.5 (CAL flipped the side); part of D1 is that flip, by design. The descriptive RAW-ranked top-3 slice (below) is the one place that keeps T490's meaning of "the model's 70%": it ranks by RAW-IND with the RAW side (`p_model >= 0.5`).
- **CAL-IND:** product of the CAL side probabilities. **The arm this rule treats as served for cross-game tickets and the claim for cross-game cells.**
- **RAW-COP** is the copula with the RAW-IND marginals (the raw probability of the CAL side), **CAL-COP** the copula with the CAL marginals; both are Gaussian copulas. Latent correlation by (relation in {same player, teammate, opponent}, stat pair), the estimator of `nba.parlay.copula.estimate_pair_corr` with `corr_shrink_k = 200` (`configs/parlay.yaml`). Fully pinned:
  - *Residual rows:* every `model_rows_{2023,2024}` row (all four stats, every player; played-only by construction) with `game_date` strictly before the month block's first day; the harness asserts, before each block's fit, that no residual row has `game_date >= block start`. `y` is `model_rows.y`.
  - *PIT:* with S = `p_ge` (S[k-1] = P(Y >= k), k = 1..len(S), and S[k-1] = 0 beyond the vector): F(y) = 1 - S[y] and F(y-1) = 1 - S[y-1], with F(-1) = 0. One `default_rng([2026, 7]).random(N)` draw over all rows of both seasons sorted by (game_date, game_id, player_id, stat), reused by every block. `z = Phi^-1(clip(F(y-1) + u (F(y) - F(y-1)), 1e-9, 1 - 1e-9))`.
  - *Pairs:* `ResidualRow(team_id = model_rows.team_id)`; relations from `_relation` (same player, teammate, opponent). Pair accumulation may be vectorised provided it equals `estimate_pair_corr` on the same rows to 1e-9 (the harness ships the equivalence test); within a game, rows are in the sorted order above, and a pair is ordered by stat name with ties in row order, as in `estimate_pair_corr`.
  - *Per ticket:* R from `build_corr_matrix` on the ticket's legs (the per-ticket `nearest_correlation` repair; entries outside the ticket's game are zero); D = diag(+1 over, -1 under); P(all hit) = Phi_m(Phi^-1(q_1), ..., Phi^-1(q_m); D R D), with q_i the arm's probability of the leg's side. A ticket whose R is the identity (to 1e-12) is priced by the exact product, which equals the orthant probability.
  - *Orthant evaluation:* `scipy.stats.multivariate_normal(mean=0, cov=D R D).cdf` called with a **fresh `default_rng(2026)` per call**, `abseps = releps = 1e-6`, default `maxpts`. Measured on a 3-D case: error 1.6e-7 against a 1e-10 reference, about 0.04 s per call; at 1e-8 a call costs 0.35 to 0.48 s, which is infeasible (about 30 CPU-hours). The 1e-6 error is four orders below the panel's 0.003 Monte Carlo noise. Cost: about 286,000 3-D calls (TOP same-game 3-leg candidates and U-ALL same-game 3-leg tickets, two books, two arms); about 3 CPU-hours is the worst case at 0.04 s per call. **The adversary timed the harness's own `orthant` at 1e-6: median 1.7 ms (|rho| about 0.03), 7.3 ms (0.08), 10.6 ms (0.15), 19.5 ms (0.3), p90 up to 28 ms; so the full scoring run is estimated at 45 to 95 minutes, with 3 hours as the worst case. It exceeds the 600 s watchdog and runs as a background job under `heavy.lock`.** **The scoring job takes `data/ops/heavy.lock` (`mkdir`, retry 120 s, `rmdir` in `finally`), logs progress per (book, season, cell) and writes `<book>.json` when a book completes (no finer checkpoint).**
  - *Pricing:* not the panel's 20,000-draw Monte Carlo. **CAL-COP is the arm this rule treats as served for same-game cells.** Cross-game tickets have no copula term, as in the contract.
- **J (optional, descriptive): joint-level recalibration.** Per (type, size): `logit P = a + b logit p_joint_cal`, IRLS with ridge 1.0 on `b`, weights `0.5 ** (age_days / 365)`, walk-forward by calendar month on the earlier U-ALL tickets (strictly before the block; a ticket's label is known only after its last game, asserted), scored on 2024 only. Fewer than 2,000 earlier tickets means identity. 2023 is J's burn-in. J is never shown on the panel unless a later rule says so.

## Estimands, CIs, words
**CI everywhere:** `research.eval.alt_harness_common.make_boot(sizes, 5000, 2026)` with `sizes = {2023: D23, 2024: D24}`, where D_s is the number of distinct CAL-served DraftKings slate dates of season s (171, 212). One generator draws seasons in ascending order, and the same matrices are shared by every cell, arm, statistic and book (Pinnacle uses its own CAL-served dates and its own `make_boot(..., 2026)`). A cell with no ticket on a date contributes 0 to that date's totals. Statistics are ratio estimators on date totals (`boot_ratio`; for a mean over tickets the numerator is the sum over a date's tickets and the denominator the ticket count). The interval is `percentile_interval` (95%). T1 and T3 p is `p_two_sided` of the statistic's bootstrap; T2 p is `p_le0` applied to the bootstrap of -D1 (small when D1 < 0). A one-season statistic uses only that season's matrix. Tickets of one date share legs and the slate, and the 20 draws share legs, so the date is the cluster (game clustering is not usable because a ticket spans games; the date cluster subsumes the game cluster for same-game tickets). Every number is reported with its ticket count and date count.

**T1, joint calibration in the large (word-bearing; Family A, m = 4 cells).** Per cell, the 2024 DraftKings U-ALL tickets, served arm as defined above (cross: CAL-IND; same: CAL-COP): gap G = mean(hit - p_joint).
- **CALIBRATED:** |G| <= 0.02 AND the interval lies inside [-0.03, +0.03] AND the 2023 point |G| <= 0.03.
- **OVERCONFIDENT:** G < -0.02, interval upper < 0, 2023 point G < 0, BH q < 0.05. **UNDERCONFIDENT:** the mirror.
- else **INCONCLUSIVE**.
- p is the two-sided date-clustered bootstrap p of G against 0 (+1 correction). The +-0.02 limit and +-0.03 margin are carried from ALT_LINE_70 / ALT_PARLAY_X (Devin 2026-10-14 13:12). The gate below sets which cells can issue CALIBRATED (all four).

**T2, did fixing the legs fix the parlay (word-bearing; Family B, m = 4).** D1 = mean ticket log loss of the served arm minus its raw counterpart (cross: CAL-IND - RAW-IND; same: CAL-COP - RAW-COP), on the 2024 U-ALL tickets, probabilities clipped to [1e-6, 1 - 1e-6]. **CAL HELPS THE JOINT:** D1 <= **-0.005** (practical floor, set at about 40% of the algebraic estimate of -0.012 below, and well under two legs' worth of the Stage 1 per-leg gain of 0.0117) AND the interval upper < 0 AND the 2023 point D1 < 0 AND BH q < 0.05 (one-sided, Family B). **CAL DOES NOT HELP** if the interval lower > -0.005 (no practical gain); else INCONCLUSIVE. Power is approximate (an algebraic estimate, not a gate): if raw tickets are about 0.07 over-confident, D1 is about -0.012 against an independent-tickets half-width near 0.005.

**T3, the panel slice (word-bearing, directional only; Family C, m = 2 sizes).** Per size, the TOP union (3 per date), DraftKings, **pooled over the two seasons** (CAL-served 2023 dates plus 2024; resampling dates within season), gap G_top = mean(hit - p_joint_cal) with the served arm per ticket type; p is `p_two_sided` on the pooled bootstrap of G_top. **TOP OVERCONFIDENT:** G_top <= -0.05, interval upper < 0, the same sign in each season, BH q < 0.05. **TOP UNDERCONFIDENT:** the mirror. Else INCONCLUSIVE. **No CALIBRATED word is issued for TOP in any cell**: the gate shows a model-only half-width of 0.049 to 0.057 per season (0.035 to 0.037 pooled), above the 0.02 bound. The -0.05 floor sits at about 1.4 pooled half-widths: a true gap of -0.05 is flagged about half the time, -0.07 about 85%.

**Descriptive, no word (labels only, never a claim):**
- **D2 copula against independence**, same-game cells: mean log loss CAL-COP - CAL-IND with both the game-date interval and the conservative bound. The gate says it cannot be decided: at an assumed equicorrelation 0.10 the expected gain per ticket is 0.0006 (2-leg) and 0.0023 (3-leg) against a conservative half-width of 0.0046 and 0.0093 (independent-tickets 0.0010 and 0.0021). Prior evidence: about 0.0011 log loss in total (JOINT work, 2026-10-11).
- **J against CAL** (joint log loss and gap), same four cells. The gate cannot size it (a 3-point miscalibration is worth about 0.002 per ticket against an independent-tickets half-width of about 0.002).
- Pinnacle for every statistic above (direction only); Brier; the calibration slope of `logit(hit)` on `logit(p)`; reliability in ten equal-count bands of `p_joint` (the top band is the ranking-relevant one); the TOP-minus-U-ALL gap contrast (winner's curse: claimed uplift against realised uplift in hit rate); RAW-ranked top-3 (what the panel would have shown without CAL) scored under both RAW-IND and CAL-IND; slices by stat mix, side mix (all-under, all-over, mixed), relation (same-player pair or not, teammates, opponents), month, and tickets whose raw joint is >= 0.50 (the T490 "70%" analogue; the count is reported even if small).

**Multiplicity.** Family A (T1) m = 4; Family B (T2) m = 4; Family C (T3) m = 2. Benjamini-Hochberg within each family at q < 0.05. Pinnacle and every descriptive row are outside every family. **CALIBRATED is decided by the interval inside +-0.03 and is not BH-adjusted**; at most 2.5% per cell of a false CALIBRATED when |G| = 0.03, at most 10% over the four cells.
