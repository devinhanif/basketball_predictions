## What the gate found, before any rule text (the five facts that shaped it)
1. **The select-2023 / report-2024 split is not the design, and the reason is narrower than the first draft said.** DraftKings 2023 alone has 36,145 rungs every arm can be scored on (of 58,106), from February 2024 only: alternates start 2023-12-07, so December and January have too few earlier rungs to fit (9 of 28 blocks are identity). None of the five 2023 bin cells passes the **inherited conservative width gate** (conservative half-width 0.024 to 0.042 against 0.03; that bound treats all 74 rungs of a game as perfectly correlated). The pre-freeze review found that under synthetic unit coupling the realistic 2023 per-bin half-width is about 0.011 to 0.018, which would pass; so the 2023 cells fail a pessimistic bound, not necessarily the data. Pooled 2023-24 passes 5 of 5 on either bound (134,462 rungs, 1,818 games, conservative half-widths 0.014 to 0.024). The departure from "select on one season, report on the next" therefore rests on (c) nothing is selected and (d) both seasons are already A0-exposed (see "Seasons"), not on 2023 being unusable. The rule is **one walk-forward pass over both seasons with a per-season replication check**.
2. **The frozen map has never seen a high-probability leg.** DraftKings main-line legs have x = logit(P) between -1.29 and +0.89 at the 1st to 99th percentile, i.e. P between 0.22 and 0.71. Of the rungs the map must score, **0% of bin 55, 13% of bin 65, 92% of bin 75, and 100% of bins 85 and 95 lie outside that band** (49% of all scored rungs; the z term is outside its band for 15%, 92%, 100%, 100% in bins 65 to 95). "CAL-main" at P >= 0.70 is an extrapolation test, not a test of what the map learned. That is stated in every verdict word.
3. **Where the effect is.** If the T491 gaps were the truth and a map recovered them exactly, the expected log-loss gain over A0 is 0.00421 per rung (pooled), and 62% of it sits in the 12,093 rungs with P >= 0.90 (9.0% of rows); bin 85 carries 20%, bins 55 and 65 together 17%, bin 75 under 1%.
4. **The test is powered for the effect.** Projected 95% half-width of the primary contrast, pooled: 0.00071 (game bootstrap on coupled synthetic labels, times sqrt(1.51)), against a floor of 0.002 (half of it is 0.001). The conservative bound (every rung of a game perfectly correlated) is 0.0034 and would not resolve the floor; the truth lies between, and the unit-level bound (rungs of a ladder perfectly correlated, units independent) is 0.00074.
5. **A plain reliability table is the strong baseline, but only in shift form.** The pattern is a function of P alone in T491's table. A step table (one constant per 0.05 bin) discards A0's resolution inside the bin and pays a built-in +0.00055 per rung even with unlimited data (pre-freeze review B1), 5.5 times the prior CAL-rung minus C0 effect. So C0 is the **shift table**: A0 plus a per-bin shift, which has zero structural cost under the oracle truth; the step table is not a comparator.

## The rule in ten lines
1. **Rung set S.** DraftKings (`draftkings`), `t60` snapshot, the four player `*_alternate` markets (pts, reb, ast, fg3m), **over** rungs at half-point lines, de-duplicated by the ALT_LADDER_HIST rule, joined to a played model row, in the **primary arm of ALT_LADDER_HIST** (games with a report stamp in (snapshot, tip - 60] excluded, lead <= 66 minutes, the DraftKings points defect window 2023-12-07 to 2023-12-19 excluded, ladders failing either ladder-integrity test excluded), **with A0 P(over) >= 0.5** and a DraftKings main-market line for the same player and stat. The set is 162,745 rungs before the main-line and fit-size conditions (the T491 primary set with P >= 0.5, equal to the ALT_LADDER_HIST gate counts in all four book x season cells). The DraftKings ast (760) and reb (27) rungs dated 2023-12-07 to 12-19 are kept under the frozen ALT_LADDER_HIST rule (only the points window is excluded); they are never scored (identity months) but they ARE fit rows for CAL-rung and C0 from 2024-02 on; T492 found the excluded "points" ladders were rebounds ladders, so a label arm refits DraftKings CAL-rung without any fit rung whose `game_date` is 2023-12-07 to 2023-12-19 (defined on `game_date`, for every stat; the vendor window itself is defined on the snapshot date, which differs only for late tips) (m1).
2. **A0** is the stored production P(over) at the rung: P(Y >= point + 0.5) on the integer grid, clipped to [0.5/199, 1 - 0.5/199].
3. **CAL-main** is the LINE_CALIBRATION CAL map, frozen at a5441242, applied **as is**: per (stat, calendar-month block), one time-weighted ridge logistic fit on DraftKings T-60 **main-line legs** strictly before the block's first day, terms `[1, x, z, bench, bench * x]` with `x = logit(A0)`, `z = clip((point - median) / max(IQR/1.349, 0.5), +-5)` from the continuous 199-point grid, `bench = starter10 < 0.5`; weight `0.5 ** (age_days / 365)`; ridge 1.0 on the four non-intercept terms; identity below 2,000 fit rows. The only change from LINE_CALIBRATION is the row it is applied to: the rung's `point`, not the book's main line.
4. **CAL-rung** is the same procedure refit on S **rung labels** strictly before each block (same book and stat, same weights, ridge 1.0, identity below 2,000 fit rows) with terms `[1, x, zm, r, r * x, bench, bench * x]`: `zm = clip((main - median) / spread, +-5)` is the position of the book's main line against the model's median (constant within a ladder), `r = clip((point - main) / spread, +-8)` is the rung's distance from the book's main line in model-spread units, and `spread = max(IQR/1.349, 0.5)`. The `r * x` term lets the slope change with distance, which is the shape T491 found (underconfident near the middle, overconfident high). No price enters any term.
5. **C0** (pinned, review B1) is a walk-forward **shift table** on the same fit rows: per (stat, block), 10 bins of A0 of width 0.05 on [0.5, 1.0] (tolerance 1e-12, the last bin closed); g = the bin's time-weighted mean of (hit - A0) over the fit rungs, with A0 clipped to [0.5/199, 1 - 0.5/199]; **g = 0 when the bin has fewer than 100 RAW fit rungs**; C0 = clip(A0 + g, 0.5/199, 1 - 0.5/199). LINE_CALIBRATION's C0, a constant base rate, is degenerate on rungs; it is kept as the reference row **BASE**. The step table is not reported as an arm.
6. **Rows scored** are the rows every arm can score: the block has at least 2,000 fit rows for CAL-main (main-line legs) and CAL-rung (rungs), and the unit has a DraftKings main line. Rows dropped by either condition are counted, never hidden, and their A0 reliability is reported (S2, label only).
7. **Primary metric:** mean per-rung log loss, arm minus A0, on the scored rows, four stats pooled, every rung weighing the same. CI: paired 95% percentile bootstrap over game resamples (B = 5,000, seed 2026, one matrix shared by every statistic, the estimator being the ratio of resampled per-game sums, `research.eval.model_clv_eval.Boot`); the date-clustered CI (B = 5,000, seed 2027) is reported beside it. A decision uses the game-clustered p; the P2 per-bin and P8 per-season intervals reuse the same shared matrices restricted to their rows.
8. **Walk-forward, not select/report:** one pass over the calendar months of 2023-12 to 2025-06, every month fit on strictly earlier months only; the verdict is read on the pooled scored rows; the same checks are read per season for replication (P8).
9. **Verdict words** are issued in a fixed order from the checks below (P1 to P3 and P5 to P8; P4 is a label): MAIN_MAP_FIXES, RUNG_REFIT_FIXES, TABLE_FIXES, NOT_FIXED.
10. **The confirmatory test is the 2026-27 live log**; no 2025 touch, ever, under this id. A pass here earns a forward shadow rule (its own id, frozen before its first scored game), nothing more.

## Checks and words
Let X be CAL-main or CAL-rung (and, for TABLE_FIXES, C0). "Bins" are the five A0 bins **55** [0.5, 0.6), **65** [0.6, 0.7), **75** [0.7, 0.8), **85** [0.8, 0.9), **95** [0.9, 1.0], each pooled over the four stats (T491's Part 1S bins plus its descriptive top band). Rows are assigned to bins by A0, so every arm is scored on the same rows in each bin. gap_X = hit rate minus mean X over the bin's rows.
- **P1 (it helps):** X - A0 mean log loss <= **-0.002** with one-sided game-clustered p < **0.0167** (Bonferroni over the three arms that can issue a word: CAL-main, CAL-rung and C0).
- **P2 (the pattern is fixed):** in **each** of the five bins, |gap_X| <= 0.02 and the union (min lower, max upper) of the game- and date-clustered 95% intervals lies inside [-0.03, +0.03] (T491's CALIBRATED definition and margins).
- **P3 (it adds to a table):** X - C0 < 0 with one-sided game-clustered p < 0.025, C0 being the shift table. **Power (m4):** the prior CAL-rung minus C0 effect is -0.0001 (80% -0.0008 to +0.0006), below the interval half-width the synthetic rehearsal measures (reported below), so P3 is underpowered for the expected effect and a P3 failure is read as "not shown", which is what TABLE_FIXES already says.
- **P4 (label only, review M1):** X - mean(SH_k), reported with its null sd, never a pass. SH_k, k = 0..49, is CAL-rung refit after permuting the realised stat among the fit **units** of the same stat and block (a rung's hit follows its unit's permuted stat, so ladders stay nested); for CAL-main it is the leg-label permutation of LINE_CALIBRATION with seeds `[1000 + k, stat, block, 0]`. Both nulls are **inert as guards**: permuting stats across different players destroys the scale of every ladder, so SH is 0.015 to 0.057 per rung worse than A0 even where the map adds nothing (the review's check 9), and a CAL arm clears any bar against it. P3 and the knockouts carry the information question.
- **P5 (no stat is harmed):** no stat has X - A0 with BH-adjusted (m = 4) one-sided p < 0.05 for "worse".
- **P6 (no slice is harmed):** the starter and bench slices each have X - A0 <= 0 (point), and each of the five bins has X - A0 <= +0.001 (point).
- **P7 (a second book agrees):** on FanDuel's primary-arm rungs built the same way, X - A0 < 0 (point). FanDuel CAL-rung is fit on FanDuel rungs and its own main line; FanDuel CAL-main is the **DraftKings** map applied unchanged (a book swap), because LINE_CALIBRATION never fit FanDuel. FanDuel is pooled over both seasons.
- **P8 (replicates across seasons):** X - A0 < 0 (point) separately on the 2023 rows and the 2024 rows, and in every bin the per-season |gap_X| <= 0.03 (point). **Power (M5):** the 2023 per-bin gap sd is 0.005 to 0.0075 under synthetic unit coupling; P8 never false-fails a perfect arm (0 of 400 simulated) but cannot detect per-season gaps below about 0.02. The 2023 rows are February to June 2024 only (36,145 rungs, 580 games). P8 is a sign-and-band replication, not a held-out one: the 2024 fits use 2023 labels.

**Words, in this order:**
- **MAIN_MAP_FIXES** iff CAL-main passes P1, P2, P5, P6, P7, P8. (If P3 fails the word carries "a table does as well".) Always printed with the share of rows outside the fit-leg band by bin; bins 75 to 95 are extrapolation.
- **RUNG_REFIT_FIXES** iff CAL-main does not, and CAL-rung passes P1, P2, P3, P5, P6, P7, P8.
- **TABLE_FIXES** iff neither of the above, and either C0 passes P1, P2, P5, P6, P7, P8 on its own, or CAL-rung passes P1, P2, P5, P6, P7, P8 but fails P3. The proposal then says in words that a reliability table, not the map, is what repairs the pattern.
- **NOT_FIXED** otherwise: no arm repairs it on these rows; the next candidate is the PIT/grid recalibration under a new id (LINE_CALIBRATION "If this fails" item 1), not a variant here.

**Multiplicity.** The primary family is the three P1 contrasts that can issue a word (CAL-main, CAL-rung, C0): Bonferroni, m = 3 (p < 0.0167 each; review M2). The words are one fixed-order classification of pre-declared checks; no arm is added after a result. The per-stat contrasts (m = 4 per arm) carry BH (P5 uses BH q < 0.05) and are labels otherwise. The 20 stat x bin cells carry T491's four words (CALIBRATED, OVERCONFIDENT, UNDERCONFIDENT, INCONCLUSIVE, the same margins) only where the cell passes the width gate (17 of 20 do pooled; the three that cannot are bin 95 for ast 2,491, fg3m 1,780 and reb 2,793 rungs); no BH is applied to them; labels only, not a pass.

**Secondary, label only (no pass):** per-stat contrasts; the full 10-bin reliability table per arm; calibration in the large by stat and role; slices by month, by rung distance from the main line (|r| tertiles) and by whether a row is inside the fit-leg band for CAL-main; the CAL-rung minus C0 knockouts (terms `[1, x]`, without `r` and `r*x`, without `zm`) and the refit without the 2023-12-07..19 rungs (m1); drop-the-best-month (the month with the largest log-loss gain removed; the gain is 62% in P >= 0.90 and the no-main-line drop spikes in April; m3); **S2** the rows with no DraftKings main line: share by role and by month (DK 2024 bench 15.5% against starter 3.3%; 2025-04 14.2%), A0 reliability on them, and a statement that CAL-rung's served object is undefined there (m2); **S4** unit-mean weighting instead of rung weighting; BASE. ## Hypothesis, null and priors
- **H1 (the question as asked).** The frozen main-line map, applied to rungs as is, repairs the T491 pattern. **H2 (the repair if H1 fails).** A refit on rung labels with rung distance repairs it. **Null.** Neither repairs it beyond what a reliability table on A0 does.
- **Why I expect H1 to fail (reasoned, not measured):** the map's slope on x was about 0.46 where x lies within +-1.3 (the POVER_BIAS scratch; the LINE_CALIBRATION disclosure); carried to x of 2 to 5 it compresses high P toward 0.5 whatever the truth there is, and the z term, near-collinear with x on rungs (|corr| 0.98), has coefficients identified only inside a band that 92% to 100% of high-P rungs leave. I did not open the CAL coefficients or apply the map to any rung.
- **Binary priors (stated before any result; also appended to PREDICTIONS_LEDGER; restated after pre-freeze review B1).** They are **not blind**: I know the T491 gaps and the T493 gain. Events are on the pooled 2023-24 scored DraftKings rows. Unchanged from the 00:46 ledger row: P(CAL-main P1 passes) **0.25**; P(CAL-main P2 passes in all five bins) **0.05**; P(CAL-main point estimate worse than A0, i.e. CAL-main - A0 > 0) **0.50**; P(CAL-main raises bin-85 log loss over A0, point) **0.60**; P(CAL-rung P1 passes) **0.85**; P(CAL-rung P2 passes in all five bins) **0.60**. **Restated because C0 is now the shift table (a stronger comparator than the step table the first row priced):** P(CAL-rung passes P3, beats the table) **0.20** (was 0.30); P(C0 passes P1, P2, P5, P6, P7, P8) **0.85** (was 0.78).
