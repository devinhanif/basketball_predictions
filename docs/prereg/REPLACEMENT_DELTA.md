## The rule in seven lines (as it would be frozen)
1. **Candidate RD** = production `context_residual` stat heads (pts, reb, ast, fg3m) refit walk-forward by production month blocks WITH four as-of columns per stat head, read out through the STORED lower-tail mixture (stored `pi`, `w_q`), production LightGBM, seed 0, no tuning: `qv_s` (team quality-weighted vacancy), `rd_s` (= `qv_s` x the scored player's usage share of the remaining pool), `share`, `n_out` (definitions: Features). The minimal production change would add these to `stat_feature_names`; this rule does not make it (live path, Devin's go, new replay oracle).
2. **Comparator A0** = stored production + stored mixture (`reports/lower_tail/components_*.npz`). A CONTROL walk (same code, no new columns) must reproduce the stored A0 to 1e-6 BEFORE any other arm is built, else the run refuses (G4). **Existing vacancy is already in production** (`COMMON_FEATURES` has `n_out_rot`, `vac_min`, `has_report`, and `stat_feature_names` adds the head's own `vac_<stat>`; `nba/props/context_residual.py` lines 721-722 and 750), so "A0 plus existing vacancy-only" IS A0 and is not a separate arm. The control that stands in its place is **V0**, the same construction with the replacement rate NOT subtracted (isolates the claim).
3. **Arms**: A0, V0 (`v0_s`, `v0d_s`, `share`, `n_out`), **RD**, **PQ** (placebo: `pq_qv_s`, `pq_rd_s`, `share`, `n_out`; each OUT player's quality replaced by that of a random same-team as-of pool member who is NOT out tonight, his own possessions kept).
4. **Primary: RD minus A0**, integer-support CRPS (199-point grid, `ceil(q - 0.5)`) on AFFECTED rows (a reported game with at least one OUT rotation player and a finite `share`), per stat; floor **-0.005**; 95% percentile bootstrap clustered by `game_id` (5,000 draws, seed 2026 + season), CI upper < 0; one-sided p BH-adjusted over the **4 stats** within each season (q = 0.05); all-rows no-harm: CI upper of RD minus A0 on ALL rows <= +0.001. **Select 2023 / report 2024**: nothing is chosen on 2023 (every constant is fixed below), so "select" is the first check: a stat passes only if it passes in BOTH seasons.
5. **Guards** (each stat, each season): G1 no harm (above); G2 identity: RD minus PQ <= -`T_C` on affected rows; G3 coverage at tau 0.10 / 0.20 / 0.80 / 0.90 within 0.01 of A0 (all rows); G4 A0 reproduction; G5 slices with n >= 300 not worse than +0.01; G6 label-free preflight (pins, production OUT-set parity, planted future). **`T_C` is UNSIZED**: it is `max(0.003, 2 sd)` of a K >= 30 permutation null of PQ-style draws, not yet run; `run` refuses while it is None (freeze blocker 2).
6. **Words** per stat: PASS_QUALITY (primary and BH in both seasons, every guard, and RD minus V0 <= -`T_C` on affected rows in both seasons: the gain is the replacement subtraction); PASS_WARM_ONLY (primary and guards, but RD minus V0 does not clear: the gain is the warm per-possession vacancy, not quality); GUARD_FAIL (primary passes, a guard fails); CLOSED (either season's point estimate > -0.002); else INCONCLUSIVE. A pass is a CANDIDATE.
7. No 2025 touch is part of this rule. A CLOSED or GUARD_FAIL stat is closed; at most one pre-registered follow-up in this family (below); no variant chasing.

## Hypothesis, information, and what is NOT new
**H1.** When rotation teammates are OUT on the T-60 report, the players who absorb their role are better forecast by the vacated production ABOVE a replacement-level player (his long-run, shrunk, per-possession rate minus the league fringe rate, times the possessions he would have played), handed out by usage, than by production's raw vacancy columns. Null: no change.

**What is genuinely new information.** (1) A long-run, empirically shrunk per-possession rate of each absent player (production's `vac_<stat>` is a 10-game half-life per-game average, noisy and minutes-confounded). (2) The explicit subtraction of what a replacement would produce. (3) A usage split of the result among the remaining pool. **What is NOT new, said plainly:** who is out (the report), his expected minutes (production's own `min10`) and the team totals are all in production already. And by construction `qv_s = v0_s - repl_s x vp` with `vp` = vacated possessions, which is proportional to production's `vac_min`: the subtraction is a LINEAR combination of two quantities the trees already see (`vac_<stat>`/`v0_s` and `vac_min`). A tree can approximate it; the question the rule asks is whether making the combination explicit and the rates long-run helps. This is also exactly WOWY_STRUCT's allocation with a minutes term subtracted (see "Relation to WOWY_STRUCT"); STRUCT's own minutes variant had the wrong sign (its forking path 4).

## Features (as-of; `nba/props/repl_delta.py`, tests `tests/props/test_repl_delta.py`)
* **Box inputs:** every played row (minutes > 0) of `data/history` (2013-2021) and `nba.duckdb` (2022-24): minutes, pts, reb, ast, fg3m, fga, fta, tov, oreb. **Possessions:** a game's possessions = mean of both teams' fga + 0.44 fta - oreb + tov; a player's possessions on the floor `P` = game possessions x minutes / (team minutes / 5). **Usage numerator** = fga + 0.44 fta + tov. (Box-only approximation; stints give exact on-floor possessions, see the capture audit.)
* **Replacement rate** of season S = pooled per-possession rate (pts, reb, ast, fg3m, usage) of FRINGE player-seasons of seasons S-2 and S-1 (rank above 9 by total minutes within the team-season, team = where he logged the most minutes; deterministic ties). Season S itself never enters (unit-tested). 2022: pts 0.1822, reb 0.0910, ast 0.0400, fg3m 0.0201, usage 0.1921 per possession; 2023 and 2024 in `gate.json`.
* **Player rate (empirical Bayes):** at game g, `r_s = (S_s + k_s x repl_s) / (Q + k_s)` over ALL of the player's earlier games (strictly before g's date, across seasons and teams), `Q` = his possessions on the floor. `k_s` by normal-normal method of moments on player-seasons 2015-2021 with >= 200 possessions (prior centred on the season's replacement rate): **pts 103.3, reb 57.3, ast 64.3, fg3m 111.9, usage 49.7** possessions (3,217 player-seasons; `K_BOUNDS = (25, 20000)`, none clipped). The prior is WEAK (about 1 to 2 games): shrinkage toward replacement is mild because players are spread far from replacement. (Disclosed: the first computation used a lower bound of 100, which clipped three of five; the bound was moved to 25 before any outcome of 2023-24 existed, because a clip is an arbitrary choice; this is the draft's only edit after seeing a prior estimate.)
* **OUT set:** production's, exactly: `flagged_from_availability` (status out on the latest official-report snapshot with `as_of <= real tip - 60 min`), restricted to rotation players (as-of `min40 >= 12`, >= 5 prior games). Parity with production on the warm frame: `n_out` equals `n_out_rot` on all 161,185 reported rows (0 mismatches) and the per-game sum of `min10` equals `vac_min` (max abs diff 0.0).
* **Expected possessions** of an OUT player j tonight = production's `min10` x the team's as-of possessions per minute (mean of its previous 10 games' possessions / 48).
* **Columns:** `qv_s` = sum over OUT rotation players of (r_js - repl_s) x P_j; `v0_s` = the same without the subtraction; `share` = w_i / (sum of w over the pool minus the OUT list, plus w_i if he is not in the pool), `w` = usage rate x expected possessions; **pool** = players with >= 5 appearances and a mean of >= 12 minutes in the team's previous 10 games of the same season (the WOWY_STRUCT pool; empty before a team's 6th game, so `share` and the `rd` / `v0d` columns are NULL there: 5.4% of A0 rows); `rd_s = qv_s x share`; `v0d_s = v0_s x share`; `n_out` = number of OUT rotation players. **Limitation, v1:** the split is by usage for every stat including rebounds (rebound share would be more apt); the dev coefficient for reb is negative.
* **NULL rule (production's):** reported game with nobody rotation-OUT -> explicit 0; no usable report -> NULL, never "nobody out". A traded player stays in his old team's pool until his appearances roll off (a known small inflation).

## Relation to WOWY_STRUCT (recommendation)
`struct_u_pts(i)` = (sum of OUT players' per-game pts) x w_i / sum w. `rd_pts(i)` = (sum of OUT players' [long-run rate - replacement] x possessions) x w_i / sum w = STRUCT with a minutes-proportional term subtracted and long-run rates. It is **a variant of STRUCT, not a rival**, and the variant loses to its own unsubtracted twin on dev data (V0 >= RD on the large-vacancy slice in D1, D2a, D2b; RD beyond V0 about 0.0 +/- 0.1% everywhere). **Recommendation: REPLACEMENT_DELTA does not supersede WOWY_STRUCT and does not complement it as a second test; close REPLACEMENT_DELTA at its gate (record the dev numbers as STRUCT forking-path 9, "replacement-subtracted variant: V0 >= RD in all three designs, closed"), and let WOWY_STRUCT continue or close on its OWN gate.** The dev data do not speak against STRUCT (they are about the subtraction, not the raw allocation); they do carry one caution for it: the placebo (wrong-identity column of the same shape) gains almost as much as the real column on affected rows, the same pattern STRUCT's own guards (P+R, ROLE, G1 margins) exist to catch, and STRUCT's `T_C`-style margins should be checked against a PQ-type null before its freeze.
