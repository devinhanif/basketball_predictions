## The question
At a book's line, is the probability the combo shadow serves for "over" honest? COMBO_STOCKS_SHADOW's G1 checks the lower tail of the whole pmf (pra and pa miss q10 narrowly, 0.1211 and 0.1204 against 0.12). Whole-pmf calibration and calibration at the line are different questions (playbook cross-cutting question 5): for pts the lower tail of the pmf was close and P(over) at the line was still 4.3 pp low (T477). This rule asks the line question for pra, pr, pa, ra, stl, blk and bs, with the map that fixed it for pts, reb, ast and fg3m (LINE_CALIBRATION Stage 1, T493: CAL - A0 -0.0117 on Pinnacle).

## The rule in eight lines (as it would read if the gate passed for the family)
1. **Family F (fixed by the gate, mechanically, at freeze):** a target is in F iff its row in the gate table passes on the data_version at freeze. On today's data: **F = {stl, pra, pr, pa, ra}, m = 5**. `blk` and `bs` are out (gate fails; they are reported descriptively, no claim, when live main-line legs allow). The gate is label-free, so choosing F from it is not selection on results.
2. **Object.** The served quantity is A0 = P_S(Y >= ceil(line)) read from the stored `s_pmf199` of the target's `forward_combo_shadow` row (S = the COMBO_STOCKS_SHADOW arm; exact at x.5 lines; whole-number lines are excluded and counted, none exist in the held data). **CAL** is LINE_CALIBRATION's map, unchanged except for one term: per (book, target, calendar-month block) one weighted logistic regression on every earlier T-60 leg of that book and target, terms `[1, x, z, bench, bench*x]`, `x = logit A0` clipped at 0.5/199, `bench = starter10 < 0.5`, weights `0.5 ** (age_days / 365)`, ridge 1.0 on the four non-intercept terms, IRLS tolerance 1e-10, a block with fewer than 2,000 fit rows uses the identity. **The one changed term is z**: S is stored as an integer 199-point grid, so the continuous grid LINE_CALIBRATION recovered does not exist. z = (line - q50) / max((q75 - q25) / 1.349, 0.5), clipped to [-5, 5], with q25, q50, q75 interpolated at grid positions 49.25, 99, 148.75 of the stored integer grid (same code path, different grid). Lines are x.5 and the integer median is an integer, so the distance is never zero. How often the 0.5 floor binds is a label-free number measured at the gate re-run (check G-z below); if it binds on more than 50% of a target's legs, the fallback z = (line - mean_S) / max(sd_S, 0.5) replaces it for that target, decided by that count and by nothing else.
3. **Fit data and test data.** *Fit-only history:* 2023-24 and 2024-25 ladder-derived legs (below), which need the S out-of-fold forecasts for those games (repair R-F). *Test:* **live 2026-27 main-line legs only**. The map applied to a live leg is fit strictly before the leg's month block, from history plus live legs already settled, and is written to a versioned file (sha recorded, `fit_through` recorded) before the block starts. Because history legs are the ladder rung nearest even money and live legs are posted main lines, the live test also tests that transfer; nothing in the held data can.
4. **Primary metric.** Mean per-leg log loss of P(over) at the line, CAL minus A0, pooled over F's primary cells (one book per target: the first of DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, BetRivers that passes the gate, fixed at freeze; today stl BetMGM, pra DraftKings, pr DraftKings, pa DraftKings, ra FanDuel), every leg weighing the same. CI: paired 95% percentile bootstrap, 2,000 resamples, seed 0, clustered by **game**, and again by **date** (a slate effect is cross-game); both upper bounds must be below 0. Practical floor **-0.003** (LINE_CALIBRATION's: the log-loss value of removing a 4 pp bias at p near 0.45).
5. **Checks and verdicts (LINE_CALIBRATION P1-P7, adapted).** P1: primary <= -0.003 with both CI uppers < 0. P2: CAL - C0 CI upper < 0 (C0 = weighted base rate of the fit rows, same book, target, block). P3: CAL - mean(SH_0..49) <= -2 null sd and CI upper < 0 (SH = CAL refit on label-permuted fit rows, `default_rng([1000+k, target, block, book])`, null sd recomputed inside each look from its own 50 permuted refits, same seeds). P4: |mean CAL - realised| <= 0.015. P5: no target significantly worse (CI lower > 0), BH over m = |F|. P6: the starter slice and the bench slice each have a point estimate <= 0. P7: the next book in the order that passes the gate agrees in direction (today stl BetRivers; pra, pr, pa FanDuel; ra has none, so P7 is evaluated on the cells that have one and ra is reported). **CALIBRATED:** P1-P7. **SHRINKAGE_ONLY:** P1 and P4-P7 pass, P2 or P3 fails; the served object is C0 and the wording is "at the line the model carries no information beyond the base rate" (the pre-freeze review B2 of LINE_CALIBRATION, carried). **FAIL:** anything else with n >= n_min. **INCONCLUSIVE:** fewer than n_min = 3,000 on-time legs in a cell (the combo shadow's n_min; that cell is then excluded from the pooled primary, counted, and reported INCONCLUSIVE; if fewer than three of the five cells reach n_min the pooled verdict is INCONCLUSIVE), or P1's point <= -0.003 with an upper bound >= 0; a second look at the end of the regular season only, same criteria.
6. **Stages.** *Stage 1 (rehearsal, descriptive, kill-only):* 2024 ladder-derived legs, walk-forward by month on 2023 plus earlier 2024, run once after the freeze. It cannot pass the rule. If pooled CAL - A0 is **positive** with a game-clustered one-sided lower bound > 0, the rule closes before the live look (the map hurts even on the population it was fit for). *Stage 2 (live, the verdict):* interim **Sun 2027-01-10**, kill-only (same kill criterion on live legs); final **Mon 2027-04-12** on games through the regular-season finale **Sun 2027-04-11**, once. No second look except the INCONCLUSIVE one above.
7. **Slices (fixed now):** starter / bench (P6), line tertile within target, target, month, book. Labels only except P6. Reliability in ten bins at the line. Coin flip and the de-vigged market are reference rows only; **no price enters any fit** (`design()` takes no price argument).
8. **One pass, no variants.** No arm, term, floor, family, book order, tolerance or look changes after any live number is seen; a new idea is a new id. **No 2025 touch is requested now or ever** (the confirmatory test is the live log, as for LINE_CALIBRATION, DECISIONS 2026-10-15 00:24). Production, serving and promotion are Devin's.

## Hypothesis and what each choice protects against
- **H1.** The CAL map lowers line log loss against A0 by at least 0.003 per leg, pooled over F's primary cells at live main lines, and beats the base rate. **Null:** the map only reproduces the base rate, or does not reach the floor, or does not transfer from ladder rungs to main lines.
- Priors are in their own section below (binary events, drafted before any result).


| choice | protects against |
|---|---|

## Playbook check (docs/MODEL_PLAYBOOK.md; class: quantile and distribution layer on a count head and a Gaussian copula)
1. **Information or capacity?** Neither new: the map adds no pre-tip information; it re-expresses S at the line. The class's typical failure ("P(over) 4 pp low at the line while overall CRPS looks good") is exactly the target. C0 and SH exist so that a correction with no information is labelled as such.
2. **Enough data for the class?** A 5-parameter map per (book, target, month). Fit rows at the first live block: 4,199-8,752 per primary cell from 2023 alone, 8,478-18,721 effective with 2024 at the 365-day half-life; the smallest 2024 block has at least 4,199 fit rows for every cell in F. blk (105 DraftKings rows in 2023) and bs (none) do not have enough, hence out of F. The test side is the weak part: per-target live half-widths 0.0018-0.0038 against a 0.003 floor.
3. **Valid test?** Held: snapshots 100% >= 60 min before the real tip; played only (DNP legs voided as the books do); x.5 lines; integer support (P(over) from the 199-atom integer grid is exact at x.5). Live: stamp S `made_at <= tipoff - 60` and `tip_source = real`; line snapshot `received_at <= tipoff - 60`; late rows excluded and counted. Open invalidity risks stated, not hidden: the ladder-to-main transfer; the integer-grid z; the combo OOF (monthly blocks) and the live nightly refit are different constructions, so a map fit on history may meet a shifted S (LINE_CALIBRATION's replay-oracle caveat applies: any change to S invalidates the map until refit); a cluster of 3-4 correlated combos per player-game inflates nominal n (game clustering handles it; the pooled hw above is borrowed and unmeasured).
4. **Lineage.** (1) LINE_CALIBRATION (`a5441242`): same map, carried: all of P1-P7, C0/SH, B2 wording, no price in a fit, 365-day half-life; changed: books (no Pinnacle), z grid, test venue (live, not 2025), family. (2) CALIBRATED_GRID (T339): shrink-style maps beat real maps in a shuffled control; carried as C0/SH. (3) COMBO_STOCKS_SHADOW G1 / ALT_LADDER_HIST integrity tests: carried as the price-only ladder exclusions. (4) Follow-up 1 (CAL at alternate rungs) is a different population (all rungs); here only the near-even rung is a fit leg and only main lines are test legs.
5. **What does it score against?** Log loss of P(over) at the line against the realised side, CAL against raw S, the base rate and permuted-label maps; the de-vigged market is a reference row. It does not claim CRPS, and says nothing about money or edge.
