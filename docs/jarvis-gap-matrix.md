## Decisions to record before implementation (what was decided tonight)

- **Target repository, framework/version, entry point, routing, owner.** parlayhitters `app/`: Preact + TypeScript on
  Vite (web, `app/web/src/main.tsx` and `main.artifact.tsx` for the single file), a read-only FastAPI service
  (`app/api`, GET only). Hash routes in `app/web/src/router.ts`; betting screens under `#/bet/*`, the existing
  `#/hedge` untouched. Owner: Devin (decisions), the main session (merges). (`JARVIS_WORKLOG.md`)
- **Existing model call path, schema, provenance, latency, errors.** The nba/ nightly export, read by the API's
  `ExportStore`; a sha mismatch is `503 export_refused` on every store route including `/api/betting/*`
  (lane J1 decision 3). The betting routes serve no model number, only `model_ref` (lane J4a decision 1).
- **Canonical IDs and collision rules.** NBA game and player ids from the export; a market's identity is event, date,
  player, stat, line, period and settlement rule (`MarketIdentity` in `app/web/src/betting/types.ts`); quotes are
  unique per event, provider, market and side (lane J3); the board keeps the newest pre-tip snapshot per line,
  provider and side, main market before alternate on a tie (lane J4a decision 4). Season 2025 refused on both sides
  (lane J3 decision 5, lane J4a decision 8).
- **Market equivalence.** Cross-provider coverage needs a verified, non-null `settlementRuleId`; none exists yet, so
  equivalence is never claimed (lane J3 decision 3). Related lines on one player and stat are never summarised as
  coverage (lane J3 decision 2). Fees stay `unknown` unless the viewer types one (lane J4b).
- **State and persistence ownership.** All betting state in this browser, versioned and scoped
  (`ph.betting.workspace:device`), with export/import; no server write route; the server `WorkspacePort` is a marked,
  unbuilt seam (lane J3). Unpriced picks are an optional `picks` list in version 1 (lane J4b decision 1).
  Planned prices go stale after 15 minutes (lane J3 decision 1, lane J4b decision 5).
- **Which target components already meet the donor behaviour; which additions are kept.** Kept as the app's own:
  Signals, Model History, Alerts, Admin, `#/hedge`, avatars (rows above marked `TARGET-ONLY`). The visual layer
  (`jarvis.css`, `theme.css` tokens, `app/web/src/ui/`) applies everywhere and is not behind the switch (lane J2a).
- **Backend services for photos, roles, ideas, history, positions, notifications, realtime.** Photos: NBA headshot by
  id or an editor upload; no roles or accounts; ideas and positions in the browser; history and alerts from the desk
  export; live play-by-play from the existing live API. No new service was added.
- **Intentional differences from the reference UI.** 12 px text floor instead of the donor's 6 to 9 px (lane J2a
  decision 2); the mode chooser is a step of the in-page intro, never a blocking page (lane J2a decision 3); the
  betting page is "Slip Hedge Finder" because "Hedge Finder" exists (lane J4b decision 7); purple feature cards are
  not used behind small text (AA, lanes J2b-A and J2b-B); the slip panel opens only on wide screens after an add
  (lane J4b decision 8).
